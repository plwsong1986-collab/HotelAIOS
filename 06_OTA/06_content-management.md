# Purpose

This document defines the content management standards for OTA integrations within HotelAIOS.

Content management ensures hotel information remains accurate, consistent, and synchronized across all OTA platforms while preserving brand identity and supporting reservation conversion.

---

# Objectives

The Content Management should:

- Standardize OTA content
- Maintain brand consistency
- Synchronize property information
- Improve content quality
- Support multilingual content
- Enable centralized governance

# Property Information

Property information should include:

- Hotel name
- Property description
- Address
- Contact information
- Location details
- Business hours

Property information should remain consistent across every OTA platform.

---

# Room Information

Room information should define:

- Room types
- Room descriptions
- Capacity
- Bed configuration
- Facilities
- Room features

Room content should follow standardized naming and descriptions.

---

# Amenities

Amenity information should include:

- Hotel facilities
- Dining services
- Recreation
- Transportation
- Guest services
- Accessibility features

Amenities should accurately reflect available services.

---

# Media Assets

Media management should support:

- Property images
- Room images
- Facility images
- Promotional images
- Brand assets

Media assets should comply with approved branding standards.

---

# Policies

OTA content should publish approved policies, including:

- Check-in policy
- Check-out policy
- Cancellation policy
- Payment policy
- Child policy
- Pet policy

Policies should remain synchronized across all distribution channels.

---

# Content Synchronization

Content synchronization should:

- Publish approved updates
- Maintain channel consistency
- Validate content before distribution
- Record synchronization status
- Support scheduled updates

Only approved content should be distributed.

---

# Monitoring

Content monitoring should:

- Detect outdated content
- Identify synchronization failures
- Measure publication status
- Record operational metrics
- Support quality reviews

Monitoring should ensure ongoing content accuracy.

---

# Governance

Content governance should define:

- Content ownership
- Review procedures
- Approval workflow
- Publication process
- Retirement process

Governance should ensure long-term content quality.

---

# Management Principles

Content management should:

- Be centralized
- Be consistent
- Be traceable
- Support multilingual content
- Protect brand integrity
- Follow Documentation First principles
