# Purpose

This document defines the governance framework for OTA distribution within HotelAIOS.

Distribution governance establishes policies, responsibilities, operational controls, and review processes that ensure consistent, reliable, and compliant hotel distribution across all connected OTA platforms.

---

# Objectives

The Distribution Governance should:

- Standardize OTA governance
- Define operational responsibilities
- Maintain distribution consistency
- Support business compliance
- Improve operational transparency
- Enable continuous improvement

# Governance Policies

Governance policies should define:

- Distribution objectives
- Operational standards
- Approval procedures
- Configuration management
- Documentation requirements

Policies should be reviewed regularly.

---

# Roles & Responsibilities

Distribution governance should clearly assign ownership for:

- OTA integrations
- Channel configuration
- Inventory management
- Pricing management
- Reservation synchronization
- Operational monitoring

Responsibilities should remain clearly documented.

---

# Distribution Standards

Distribution standards should define:

- Supported OTA platforms
- Content quality requirements
- Inventory consistency
- Pricing consistency
- Reservation accuracy
- Operational procedures

Standards should apply across every connected channel.

---

# Change Management

Distribution changes should follow a controlled process that includes:

- Change request
- Impact assessment
- Approval
- Implementation
- Validation
- Documentation

Every significant change should be recorded.

---

# Compliance

Distribution operations should comply with:

- Business policies
- Partner agreements
- Security requirements
- Data protection policies
- Operational procedures

Compliance reviews should be performed regularly.

---

# Risk Management

Risk management should:

- Identify operational risks
- Assess business impact
- Define mitigation strategies
- Monitor identified risks
- Review mitigation effectiveness

Risk management should support stable OTA operations.

---

# Performance Review

Governance reviews should evaluate:

- Channel performance
- Synchronization reliability
- Reservation accuracy
- Distribution consistency
- Operational efficiency
- Incident trends

Performance reviews should support informed decision-making.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Governance should support:

- Process optimization
- Distribution improvements
- Operational standardization
- Technology enhancements
- Documentation updates
- Team knowledge sharing

Continuous improvement should be based on measurable operational outcomes.

---

# Governance Principles

Distribution governance should:

- Be centralized
- Be transparent
- Be auditable
- Support accountability
- Encourage continuous improvement
- Follow Documentation First principles
