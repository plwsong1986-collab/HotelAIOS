# Purpose

This document defines how the Sanlitian brand communicates.

Unlike the Brand Manifesto, which defines philosophy, and Core Values, which define decision-making principles, this document defines the tone, language, vocabulary, and writing style used across every communication channel.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for brand communication within HotelAIOS.

---

# Our Voice

Sanlitian does not speak like a luxury hotel.

We do not speak like a travel agency.

We do not speak like an advertising company.

We speak like a thoughtful local host welcoming friends to Lijiang.

Every sentence should make guests feel comfortable, respected, and genuinely welcomed.

---

# Our Personality

If Sanlitian were a person, it would be:

- Warm
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Thoughtful
- Authentic
- Knowledgeable about Lijiang
- Humble
- Patient

We never try to impress people.

We help people feel at home.

---

# Our Tone

Every communication should feel consistent, regardless of channel.

## Warm

Write with kindness.

Make guests feel welcomed.

Never sound distant.

---

## Calm

Do not rush people.

Do not pressure people.

Allow readers to slow down.

---

## Honest

Never exaggerate.

Never overpromise.

Always be truthful.

Trust is built through honesty.

---

## Local

Speak from experience.

Recommend places we genuinely love.

Share real stories instead of marketing slogans.

---

## Respectful

Respect every guest.

Respect local culture.

Respect different travel styles.

Never judge.

---

# Preferred Language

Whenever possible, prefer natural and human language over marketing language.

| Instead of | Prefer |
|------------|--------|
| Luxury Accommodation | A peaceful place to stay |
| Premium Experience | Experience Lijiang comfortably |
| Best Hotel | A boutique guesthouse beside the Three-Eyed Well |
| Activities | Experiences |
| Customers | Guests |
| Services | Hospitality |

# Vocabulary to Avoid

Avoid words that sound exaggerated, promotional, or sales-oriented.

Examples include:

- Luxury
- Perfect
- Cheapest
- Best
- Five-Star
- Exclusive
- Limited-Time
- Amazing
- World-Class
- Ultimate
- Guarantee
- Unforgettable
- Premium
- VIP

These words create marketing expectations.

We prefer authenticity.

---

# Writing Principles

When creating written content:

- Keep sentences short and natural.
- Use clear international English.
- Avoid unnecessary technical or complex vocabulary.
- Write for international travelers.
- Write as if speaking face to face.
- Prioritize clarity over creativity.
- Be informative without sounding promotional.

---

# Story Before Marketing

We tell stories before promoting products.

We share experiences before introducing rooms.

We inspire before we persuade.

Hospitality comes before marketing.

---

# AI Communication

All AI-generated responses should follow the same brand voice.

AI should be:

- Friendly
- Helpful
- Honest
- Calm
- Patient
- Clear
- Human

AI should never sound robotic.

AI should never sound like a scripted customer service agent.

AI should sound like someone who genuinely knows and loves Lijiang.

---

# Photography Language

When describing photographs, focus on feelings rather than objects.

Instead of:

> Beautiful room.

Prefer:

> Wake up with morning sunlight in a quiet courtyard.

Instead of:

> Tea Room.

Prefer:

> Share a peaceful afternoon with a cup of Yunnan tea.

Describe experiences before describing facilities.

---

# One Simple Rule

If a sentence sounds like an advertisement,

rewrite it.

If it sounds like genuine hospitality,

keep it.
