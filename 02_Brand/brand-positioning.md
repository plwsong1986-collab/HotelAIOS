# Brand Positioning

## Position Statement

Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse is a premium boutique guesthouse located in the heart of Lijiang Ancient Town, offering comfortable accommodation with professional oxygen support, traditional hospitality, and authentic local experiences for travelers from around the world.

Unlike ordinary guesthouses, we combine modern comfort, local culture, peaceful surroundings, and altitude-friendly facilities to help every guest enjoy Lijiang with confidence.

What makes us different:

Instead of simply providing accommodation, we focus on helping guests experience Lijiang comfortably and confidently.

Our local knowledge helps guests discover authentic Lijiang beyond the typical tourist experience.
