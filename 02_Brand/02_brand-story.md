# Purpose

This document tells the origin and evolution of the Sanlitian brand.

Unlike the Brand Manifesto, which defines the philosophy and principles of the brand, this document explains how the brand came into existence and the story behind its identity.

It serves as the official narrative for all storytelling across HotelAIOS.

---

# Every Place Has a Story

Some hotels are built around business.

Some are built around tourism.

Sanlitian was built around a place.

Before there was a guesthouse, there was an old well.

Before there were travelers, there were fields.

Before there was a business, there was a quiet corner of Lijiang where people lived with nature.

Everything began here.

---

# The Name "Sanlitian"

Sanlitian literally means "Three-Li Field."

The name comes from the land surrounding the historic Three-Eyed Well, one of the oldest wells in Lijiang Ancient Town.

For generations, the well supplied clean spring water to nearby homes and farmland.

It nourished crops.

It supported families.

It became the center of daily life.

The surrounding fields gradually became known as Sanlitian.

Today, although Lijiang welcomes visitors from around the world, the peaceful character of this neighborhood remains.

The old well still flows.

The trees still provide shade.

The atmosphere is still calm.

Our guesthouse proudly carries this name because we believe every stay should begin with understanding the place where you are.

---

# Why an Oxygen Guesthouse?

Lijiang sits at an elevation of more than 2,400 meters.

Many travelers experience mild altitude discomfort.

Some feel tired.

Some cannot sleep well.

Others cannot fully enjoy the beauty of Lijiang because they never completely recover from the journey.

We believed there should be a better way.

Instead of asking guests to simply tolerate the altitude, we designed every stay around comfort.

Continuous in-room oxygen support allows guests to sleep better, recover naturally, and enjoy every day of their journey.

Comfort is not an upgrade.

It is our responsibility.

---

# More Than Oxygen

Oxygen alone does not create memorable travel.

People do.

Stories do.

Culture does.

That is why Sanlitian is not only about comfortable accommodation.

It is also about helping guests discover the real Lijiang.

Not only through famous attractions,

but through everyday local life.

---

# Living Like a Local

Our favorite recommendations are often the simplest ones.

Walking through the morning market.

Choosing fresh ingredients.

Sharing tea in the courtyard.

Brewing Yunnan coffee together.

Listening to stories beside the old well.

Watching clouds move across the mountains.

These moments rarely appear in travel guides.

Yet they often become the memories guests remember most.

---

# Tea, Coffee, and Yunnan

Yunnan is famous for both tea and coffee.

We believe travelers should experience both.

The tea we share comes from Yunnan.

The coffee is personally selected from local coffee farms in Baoshan.

Every cup tells a story about the land, the farmers, and the people behind it.

Hospitality begins with sharing what we truly love.

---

# Our Home

Sanlitian is not designed to impress.

It is designed to make people feel comfortable.

The courtyard is quiet.

The rooms are peaceful.

The oxygen helps guests rest.

The old town is only a short walk away.

Everything is intentionally simple.

Because true luxury is peace.

---

# What We Hope Guests Remember

When guests leave, we do not hope they only remember our rooms.

We hope they remember how they felt.

Perhaps they discovered a quiet street.

Perhaps they shared tea with new friends.

Perhaps they finally slept well after arriving in Lijiang.

Perhaps they found a slower rhythm of life.

If that happens,

then our story becomes part of theirs.

---

# The Story Continues

Sanlitian is still growing.

Every guest adds another page.

Every conversation creates another memory.

Every recommendation leads someone to discover a different side of Lijiang.

Our story is never finished.

It continues with every traveler who chooses to stay.
