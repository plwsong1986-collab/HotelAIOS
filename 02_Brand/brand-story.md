# Our Story

Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse was created with one simple belief:

Travel should be comfortable, relaxing, and memorable.

Located in the heart of Lijiang Ancient Town, our guesthouse was designed to provide guests with a peaceful place to rest while experiencing the unique culture, history, and natural beauty of Lijiang.

Because Lijiang is located at a high altitude, many travelers are concerned about altitude discomfort. For this reason, we have integrated professional oxygen support into our guesthouse, allowing guests to enjoy their journey with greater comfort and confidence.

We believe that hospitality is more than providing a room.

It is about making every guest feel welcomed, respected, and cared for.

Every morning begins with a complimentary Chinese breakfast.

Every evening offers a peaceful courtyard surrounded by flowers, traditional architecture, and local culture.

Whether you are visiting Lijiang for the first time, traveling with your family, exploring Yunnan as a photographer, or simply looking for a quiet place to relax, we hope Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse becomes part of your unforgettable journey.
