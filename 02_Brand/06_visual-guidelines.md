# Purpose

This document defines the visual principles of the Sanlitian brand.

Rather than specifying detailed design assets such as logos, colors, or typography, it establishes the visual atmosphere and design philosophy that should guide all brand expressions.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for visual identity across HotelAIOS.

---

# Our Visual Identity

The visual identity of Sanlitian should express the same feeling as staying at the guesthouse.

Quiet.

Natural.

Comfortable.

Authentic.

Every photograph, illustration, video, interface, and design choice should reinforce this atmosphere.

---

# The Feeling We Create

Guests should immediately feel:

- Calm
- Relaxed
- Welcomed
- Close to nature
- Connected to Lijiang
- Comfortable enough to slow down

Our visual identity should create peace rather than excitement.

If a visual feels loud or distracting, it probably does not represent our brand.

---

# Photography Principles

Photography should document real experiences rather than create artificial ones.

Whenever possible:

- Use natural light.
- Capture genuine moments.
- Show authentic guest experiences.
- Preserve natural colors.
- Minimize editing and retouching.

Our goal is to build trust through authenticity.

---

# Preferred Subjects

Show experiences before facilities.

Examples include:

- Morning sunlight in the courtyard
- Guests enjoying tea together
- Freshly brewed Yunnan coffee
- Quiet corners of the guesthouse
- The Three-Eyed Well
- Stone paths in Lijiang Old Town
- Local markets
- Mountain scenery
- Traditional architecture
- Small everyday moments

People remember feelings more than buildings.

---

# Color Direction

Our visual language should take inspiration from the natural environment of Yunnan.

Examples include:

- Natural wood
- Stone
- Earth
- Forest green
- Mountain mist
- Warm sunlight
- Spring water
- Tea leaves
- Coffee beans

Colors should feel soft, warm, and timeless.

Avoid highly saturated, artificial, or trend-driven palettes.

---

# Composition

Images should feel open and breathable.

Whenever possible:

- Leave generous negative space.
- Avoid visual clutter.
- Balance people, architecture, and nature.
- Let natural light become part of the composition.
- Allow stillness to be visible.

Silence can be communicated visually.

---

# Human Presence

Whenever appropriate, include people naturally using the space.

Examples include:

- Reading in the courtyard
- Drinking Yunnan tea
- Walking through Lijiang
- Talking with local residents
- Watching the morning sunlight

Avoid posed promotional photography whenever possible.

Real moments create stronger emotional connections.

---

# Design Principles

Our design language should be:

- Clean
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Functional
- Timeless

Every design decision should improve clarity rather than attract attention.

Decoration should never compete with content.

Good design quietly supports hospitality.

---

# Visual Consistency

A consistent visual identity should appear across every guest touchpoint, including:

- Official Website
- OTA platforms
- AI interfaces
- Social media
- Printed materials
- Signage
- Photography
- Videos
- Internal presentations where appropriate

Guests should recognize Sanlitian regardless of where they encounter the brand.

Consistency builds familiarity.

---

# What We Avoid

Avoid visuals that feel:

- Overly luxurious
- Artificial
- Over-edited
- Crowded
- Flashy
- Aggressive
- Generic
- Trend-driven
- Stock-photo-like

If a photograph could belong to any hotel,

it probably does not belong to Sanlitian.

---

# One Simple Rule

Before publishing any visual, ask:

> Would this image make someone feel more peaceful?

If the answer is yes,

it belongs to Sanlitian.
