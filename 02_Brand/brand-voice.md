# Brand Voice

Our voice represents how Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse communicates with guests across all platforms.

Whether on our website, social media, OTA listings, emails, or AI assistants, our tone should remain consistent.

---

## Friendly

We speak like a warm and welcoming host.

Our language should feel natural, approachable, and kind.

Guests should feel comfortable from the very first sentence.

---

## Professional

We provide accurate information with confidence.

We avoid exaggerated marketing language and focus on facts, clarity, and trust.

---

## Helpful

Our goal is to solve problems before guests ask.

We provide useful travel information, practical advice, and clear guidance.

---

## Authentic

We tell real stories.

We share genuine experiences instead of using empty promotional language.

Honesty builds long-term trust.

---

## Calm

Travel should feel relaxing.

Our writing should create a peaceful feeling rather than excitement or pressure.

Simple language is better than complicated language.

---

## Respectful

We respect every guest regardless of nationality, language, age, or travel style.

Our communication is always polite and inclusive.

---

## Positive

We focus on what guests can enjoy.

Our messages inspire confidence without making unrealistic promises.

---

## Writing Principles

Always:

- Be friendly.
- Be clear.
- Be honest.
- Be helpful.
- Be respectful.
- Be professional.

Never:

- Exaggerate.
- Mislead guests.
- Use aggressive sales language.
- Create unnecessary pressure.
- Overpromise.

---

## Examples

Instead of:

> We are the best hotel in Lijiang.

Use:

> We provide comfortable accommodation, professional oxygen support, and warm hospitality in the heart of Lijiang Ancient Town.

---

Instead of:

> Luxury experience guaranteed!

Use:

> We focus on creating a comfortable, peaceful, and memorable stay for every guest.

---

## Future Applications

This voice will be used across:

- Official Website
- OTA Platforms
- AI Knowledge Base
- Social Media
- Marketing Materials
- Customer Service
- Email Communication
