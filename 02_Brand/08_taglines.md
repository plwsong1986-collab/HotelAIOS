# Purpose

This document defines the official messaging framework of the Sanlitian brand.

It standardizes how Sanlitian introduces itself across every public communication channel, including the website, OTA platforms, AI assistants, social media, printed materials, interviews, and future marketing.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for official brand messaging within HotelAIOS.

---

# Brand Statement

Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse is a peaceful courtyard guesthouse beside the historic Three-Eyed Well in Lijiang Ancient Town, where travelers enjoy comfortable stays, fresh oxygen, and authentic Yunnan experiences.

This statement represents the official introduction of the Sanlitian brand.

---

# Elevator Pitch

Sanlitian is a boutique oxygen guesthouse located in the heart of Lijiang Ancient Town.

We combine continuous in-room oxygen support with genuine local hospitality, helping travelers rest comfortably while discovering the culture, nature, and slower rhythm of Yunnan.

Our goal is simple:

Help every guest experience Lijiang comfortably.

---

# Short Description

A peaceful oxygen boutique guesthouse beside the historic Three-Eyed Well, offering comfortable stays and authentic local experiences in Lijiang Ancient Town.

---

# Medium Description

Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse is located beside the historic Three-Eyed Well in Lijiang Ancient Town.

Designed around comfort, authenticity, and local culture, we provide continuous in-room oxygen support, peaceful courtyard spaces, and genuine hospitality that helps travelers enjoy Lijiang at their own pace.

---

# Long Description

Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse believes travel should create memories, not discomfort.

Located beside the historic Three-Eyed Well in Lijiang Ancient Town, we provide a peaceful courtyard where guests can sleep comfortably with continuous in-room oxygen support while discovering authentic Yunnan culture.

Beyond accommodation, we share local recommendations, tea, coffee, neighborhood walks, and everyday experiences that help travelers connect with the real Lijiang.

We are not simply a place to stay.

We are a place to experience Lijiang comfortably.

# Channel Guidelines

## Official Website

Prefer the Brand Statement or the Long Description.

---

## OTA Platforms

Prefer the Medium Description.

Adjust wording only when platform character limits require it.

---

## AI Assistant

Use the Brand Statement naturally during introductions.

Avoid repeating slogans unnecessarily.

---

## Social Media

Use Supporting Taglines only when they naturally complement the story.

Stories should always take priority over slogans.

---

## Printed Materials

Prefer the Short Description together with the Primary Tagline.

# Messaging Principles

Every introduction should be:

- Human
- Honest
- Calm
- Clear
- Easy to understand
- Consistent with the Brand Voice

Avoid:

- Exaggeration
- Generic tourism language
- Empty marketing phrases
- Unsupported claims

Consistency builds recognition.

Authenticity builds trust.

---

# One Simple Rule

People should remember how Sanlitian feels,

not simply what Sanlitian says.

A tagline may attract attention.

A genuine experience creates lasting memories.
