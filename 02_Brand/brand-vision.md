# Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the most trusted boutique guesthouses in Lijiang Ancient Town, recognized for comfortable accommodation, professional oxygen support, authentic local experiences, and outstanding hospitality.

We aim to create a guesthouse that travelers confidently recommend to family and friends after every stay.

---

## Long-term Goals

We strive to:

- Deliver consistently excellent guest experiences.
- Build a trusted and respected local brand.
- Welcome travelers from around the world.
- Share the culture and beauty of Lijiang.
- Continuously improve through innovation and guest feedback.
- Use digital technology and AI to enhance hospitality.

---

## Our Future

We envision a future where Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse is known not only as a place to stay, but as a destination that represents warmth, comfort, and authentic travel experiences in Lijiang.
