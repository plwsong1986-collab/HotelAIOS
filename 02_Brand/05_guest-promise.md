# Purpose

This document defines the experience every guest should consistently receive at Sanlitian.

Unlike the Brand Manifesto, which defines philosophy, Core Values, which define decision-making principles, and Brand Voice, which defines communication style, this document defines the guest experience that the Sanlitian brand commits to delivering.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for guest experience standards across HotelAIOS.

---

# Our Promise

Every guest chooses Sanlitian for their own reason.

Some come to explore Lijiang.

Some come to rest.

Some come to experience local culture.

Some simply want a comfortable night's sleep.

Whatever brings them here, we make the same promise to everyone.

---

# 1. A Comfortable Stay

Comfort is our first priority.

From continuous in-room oxygen support to a peaceful courtyard environment, every detail is designed to help guests rest well and enjoy their journey.

---

# 2. Honest Hospitality

We recommend what we genuinely believe is best for our guests.

We never recommend a restaurant, attraction, or experience simply because it benefits us.

Trust is earned through honesty.

---

# 3. Local Knowledge

We help guests discover the Lijiang we know and love.

Beyond famous attractions, we share quiet streets, neighborhood cafés, traditional markets, tea culture, and everyday local life.

Every recommendation comes from personal experience.

---

# 4. Respect for Every Journey

Every traveler is different.

Some enjoy planning every detail.

Others prefer to explore freely.

Some ask many questions.

Others simply enjoy quiet moments.

We respect every travel style without judgment.

---

# 5. A Peaceful Environment

Travel should provide space to slow down.

We strive to create an atmosphere where guests can breathe deeply, sleep peacefully, and enjoy unhurried moments throughout their stay.

---

# 6. Clear Communication

We communicate clearly, honestly, and patiently.

If we know the answer, we explain it clearly.

If we do not know, we say so honestly and help guests find the right information.

---

# 7. Continuous Care

Our hospitality does not begin at check-in.

It begins with the first conversation.

It continues throughout the stay.

It ends only after guests return home with good memories.

---

# 8. Continuous Improvement

Every suggestion matters.

Every review is carefully considered.

Every stay teaches us how to become a better host.

Improvement is part of our promise.

---

# What Guests Should Feel

When guests leave Sanlitian, we hope they feel:

- Rested
- Comfortable
- Welcomed
- Respected
- Connected to Lijiang
- Inspired to return

These feelings matter more than luxury.

---

# Our Standard

We do not measure success by occupancy alone.

Instead, we ask questions such as:

- Did our guests sleep well?
- Did they feel genuinely welcomed?
- Did they experience the real Lijiang?
- Did we earn their trust?
- Would we be happy to welcome them again?

If the answer is yes,

we have fulfilled our promise.

---

# Every Day

This promise is not a marketing statement.

It is the standard we hold ourselves to every single day.

Every decision, every conversation, and every interaction should reflect these commitments.

That is what it means to be part of Sanlitian.
