# Purpose

This letter expresses the long-term vision and personal philosophy behind Sanlitian.

Unlike the Brand Manifesto, which defines the brand's philosophy, or the Brand Story, which explains its origin, this document shares the founder's perspective on hospitality, travel, technology, and the future of Sanlitian.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for the founder's message within HotelAIOS.

---

# Why We Built Sanlitian

When people ask why we opened a guesthouse in Lijiang, they often expect a business story.

The truth is much simpler.

We wanted to create a place where people could travel comfortably, slow down, and experience the real Lijiang.

That idea became Sanlitian.

---

# A Different Way to Travel

Modern travel often feels rushed.

People hurry from one attraction to another.

They take photographs.

They check destinations off a list.

Then they leave.

We believe there is another way.

Travel becomes more meaningful when people have time to breathe, observe, and appreciate ordinary moments.

Sometimes the most memorable experience is not a famous attraction.

It is sharing tea in a quiet courtyard.

Watching morning sunlight enter an old stone alley.

Talking with local people.

Sleeping peacefully after a long journey.

---

# Why Oxygen Matters

Lijiang is beautiful because of its mountains.

Its altitude is part of that beauty.

But for many visitors, it also brings discomfort.

We did not want guests to spend their first nights struggling to sleep.

Providing continuous in-room oxygen support was never about creating a luxury experience.

It was about helping guests enjoy everything that brought them here.

Comfort should never be an afterthought.

---

# What Hospitality Means to Us

Hospitality is not measured by how many services we provide.

It is measured by how people feel when they leave.

Did they feel welcomed?

Did they feel respected?

Did they experience something genuine?

If the answer is yes,

then we have done our job.

---

# The Lijiang We Hope to Share

There is a famous Lijiang that everyone visits.

There is also a quieter Lijiang.

Morning markets.

Neighborhood cafés.

Ancient wells.

Tea shared without ceremony.

Fresh coffee from Yunnan.

Conversations that cannot be planned.

These are the moments we hope our guests discover.

---

# Technology with a Human Heart

HotelAIOS is part of our future.

Technology helps us answer questions more quickly, organize information more effectively, and improve the guest experience.

But technology should never replace kindness.

It should create more opportunities for meaningful human connection.

The purpose of innovation is not to remove people.

It is to help people care for one another even better.

---

# A Promise for the Future

Sanlitian will continue to grow.

Rooms may change.

Technology will evolve.

New ideas will appear.

But our purpose should remain the same.

Help every guest experience Lijiang comfortably.

Share the culture we love with honesty.

Treat every guest with respect.

Protect the peaceful spirit of this place.

If we continue to do these things,

Sanlitian will always remain true to itself.

---

# Thank You

Whether you are a guest,

a colleague,

a partner,

or simply someone learning about Sanlitian for the first time,

thank you for being part of our story.

We hope that one day our paths will cross in the courtyard beside the Three-Eyed Well.

Until then,

welcome to Sanlitian.

# Notes

This document is the authoritative source for the founder's message.

It should remain intentionally personal, timeless, and authentic.

As Sanlitian grows and HotelAIOS evolves, this letter should continue to remind future team members, partners, and guests why the brand exists—not simply what it does.

It is to preserve the spirit behind it.
