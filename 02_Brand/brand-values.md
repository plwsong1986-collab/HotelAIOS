# Our Core Values

Our values guide every decision we make and every service we provide.

They define who we are and how we create memorable experiences for every guest.

---

## Guest First

Every decision begins with one question:

"Will this improve our guests' experience?"

Guest satisfaction is always our highest priority.

---

## Honest Hospitality

We believe hospitality begins with honesty.

We provide clear information, transparent pricing, and sincere service without hidden costs or misleading promises.

---

## Comfort and Well-being

Travel should be enjoyable and stress-free.

We provide clean rooms, quality facilities, professional oxygen support, and a peaceful environment so guests can fully relax.

---

## Local Culture

We respect the history and traditions of Lijiang.

We encourage guests to experience authentic local culture, architecture, cuisine, and daily life.

---

## Professional Service

Small details create great experiences.

We continuously improve our service quality, operational standards, and guest support.

---

## Trust

Trust is built through consistent actions.

We value long-term relationships more than short-term business opportunities.

---

## Continuous Improvement

We believe there is always room to improve.

Every guest review, suggestion, and experience helps us become better.

---

## Sustainability

We respect nature, our community, and local culture.

Whenever possible, we support environmentally responsible and sustainable practices.

---

## Innovation

We embrace new technologies, artificial intelligence, and digital tools to improve guest experiences while preserving the warmth of traditional hospitality.

---

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing:

- Comfortable Accommodation
- Warm Hospitality
- Reliable Service
- Authentic Local Experiences
- Professional Oxygen Support
- Clean and Safe Environment
- Honest Communication
- Continuous Improvement

---

## Future Development

These values will guide:

- Brand Development
- Guest Experience
- Employee Training
- Website Content
- AI Knowledge Base
- Marketing Strategy
- Business Expansion
