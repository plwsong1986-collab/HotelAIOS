# Purpose

This document defines the core values that guide every decision made by the Sanlitian brand.

Unlike the Brand Manifesto, which defines the brand philosophy and long-term vision, this document defines the principles that influence daily decisions, guest interactions, products, services, and future development.

These values serve as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for value-based decision-making across HotelAIOS.

---

# Our Core Values

At Sanlitian, values are not slogans displayed on a wall.

They shape every decision we make, every conversation we have, and every experience we create.

These values define how we put our philosophy into practice.

---

# 1. Guest Before Business

A successful stay is always more important than a successful sale.

We recommend what guests truly need, not what generates the highest revenue.

Long-term trust is worth more than short-term profit.

---

# 2. Comfort Is a Responsibility

Guests come to Lijiang to create meaningful memories.

They should not lose those memories because of poor sleep or altitude discomfort.

Providing continuous in-room oxygen support is not a luxury.

It is part of our responsibility as a host.

Comfort always comes first.

---

# 3. Respect the Place

Sanlitian exists because of Lijiang.

Because of the Three-Eyed Well.

Because of the people who have lived here for generations.

We respect local culture, local history, local traditions, and the rhythm of everyday life.

We introduce the real Lijiang rather than creating artificial experiences.

---

# 4. Share, Don't Sell

Hospitality begins by sharing.

A good cup of Yunnan tea.

Freshly brewed Baoshan coffee.

A morning walk through Zhongyi Market.

A quiet conversation in the courtyard.

These experiences are gifts.

Not products.

---

# 5. Simplicity Creates Comfort

Luxury is not about having more.

It is about needing less.

Simple spaces.

Simple communication.

Simple design.

Simple hospitality.

Peace comes from simplicity.

---

# 6. Every Guest Is Different

No two travelers arrive with the same story.

Some seek rest.

Some seek adventure.

Some seek culture.

Some simply need a good night's sleep.

We listen before we serve.

---

# 7. Grow Through Listening

Every guest teaches us something.

Every review is valuable.

Every suggestion is welcome.

Improvement is never finished.

Learning never stops.

---

# 8. Stay Human

Technology helps us work better.

Artificial Intelligence helps us respond faster.

But kindness can never be automated.

People will always come before technology.

Hospitality remains deeply human.

---

# Applying Our Values

These values guide every important decision across the Sanlitian brand.

They influence areas including:

- Guest service
- Website content
- AI interactions
- OTA content
- Marketing
- Photography
- Local experiences
- Interior design
- Future products and services

If an idea does not support these values,

it does not belong to the Sanlitian brand.

---

# Decision Framework

Before making any decision, we ask one simple question:

> **Will this make our guests feel more comfortable, more welcomed, and more connected to Lijiang?**

If the answer is yes,

we move forward.

If the answer is no,

we choose another way.
