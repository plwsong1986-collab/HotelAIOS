# Overview

The Legal module defines the legal, regulatory, compliance, contractual, and policy framework for HotelAIOS.

Its purpose is to ensure that the platform operates in accordance with applicable laws, industry standards, contractual obligations, and internal governance requirements while protecting customers, partners, employees, and the organization.

Legal requirements should be integrated into platform design, business processes, operational procedures, AI capabilities, and data management throughout the entire system lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

The Legal module aims to:

- Establish legal governance
- Support regulatory compliance
- Protect customer rights
- Define platform policies
- Manage contractual obligations
- Protect intellectual property
- Reduce legal risks
- Enable continuous compliance improvement
