# Purpose

This document defines the Terms of Service framework for HotelAIOS.

The Terms of Service establish the legal conditions governing the use of the HotelAIOS platform, define the rights and responsibilities of all parties, set acceptable use requirements, and provide the contractual foundation for platform services.

---

# Objectives

The Terms of Service should:

- Define platform usage conditions
- Establish user responsibilities
- Protect platform rights
- Clarify service limitations
- Reduce legal disputes
- Support regulatory compliance

# Service Scope

The Terms of Service should define:

- Platform purpose
- Available services
- Service availability
- Supported regions
- Service limitations
- Eligibility requirements

Users should understand the scope of services before using the platform.

---

# User Accounts

User account requirements should include:

- Account registration
- Identity verification
- Credential security
- Account responsibilities
- Account suspension
- Account termination

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their accounts.

---

# Acceptable Use

Users should agree to:

- Use the platform lawfully
- Respect intellectual property rights
- Protect account credentials
- Avoid unauthorized access
- Avoid abusive or malicious activities
- Comply with applicable laws

Violations may result in service restrictions or account termination.

---

# Platform Services

Platform services should define:

- Service functionality
- Service updates
- Maintenance windows
- Third-party integrations
- AI-assisted features
- Operational availability

Services may evolve as the platform continues to improve.

---

# Payments and Billing

Where applicable, payment terms should define:

- Subscription plans
- Billing cycles
- Payment methods
- Refund policies
- Taxes and fees
- Service suspension for non-payment

Payment processes should remain transparent and clearly documented.

---

# Intellectual Property

The Terms of Service should clarify:

- Platform ownership
- Software licensing
- Trademark usage
- Copyright protection
- User-generated content
- Third-party intellectual property

Intellectual property rights should remain protected at all times.

---

# Liability

Liability provisions should address:

- Service availability
- Limitation of liability
- User responsibilities
- Third-party services
- Force majeure
- Dispute resolution

Liability provisions should comply with applicable laws and regulations.

---

# Governance

Terms of Service governance should define:

- Document ownership
- Approval procedures
- Version management
- Policy review
- Legal compliance
- Continuous improvement

Governance should ensure the Terms of Service remain current and enforceable.

---

# Terms of Service Principles

The Terms of Service should:

- Be transparent
- Be understandable
- Be enforceable
- Be regularly reviewed
- Support legal compliance
- Follow Documentation First principles
