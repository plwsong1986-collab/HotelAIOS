# Purpose

This document defines the legal architecture for HotelAIOS.

Legal Architecture establishes the governance framework, legal principles, regulatory requirements, contractual responsibilities, compliance structure, and policy management necessary to support lawful, transparent, and accountable platform operations throughout the entire system lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

The Legal Architecture should:

- Establish a platform-wide legal framework
- Define legal governance responsibilities
- Support regulatory compliance
- Protect organizational and customer rights
- Reduce legal and regulatory risks
- Enable continuous legal improvement

# Legal Principles

The legal architecture should follow these principles:

- Compliance by Design
- Privacy by Design
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Auditability
- Continuous Compliance

Legal requirements should be incorporated into every business and technical process.

---

# Legal Domains

The legal framework should include:

- Privacy
- Data Protection
- Consumer Protection
- Intellectual Property
- Contract Management
- Regulatory Compliance
- Risk Management
- Corporate Governance

Each domain should maintain clearly defined ownership and responsibilities.

---

# Regulatory Compliance

Compliance activities should support:

- Applicable laws
- Industry regulations
- Regional requirements
- Data protection regulations
- Security compliance
- Internal governance standards

Compliance requirements should be regularly reviewed and updated.

---

# Policy Management

Legal policies should define:

- Privacy policies
- Terms of service
- Data governance policies
- Acceptable use policies
- Information security policies
- Internal compliance policies

Policies should be version-controlled and approved before publication.

---

# Contract Governance

Contract governance should manage:

- Customer agreements
- Vendor contracts
- Partner agreements
- Licensing agreements
- Service-level agreements
- Contract lifecycle management

Contracts should be centrally managed and regularly reviewed.

---

# Risk Management

Legal risk management should include:

- Regulatory risk
- Contractual risk
- Compliance risk
- Intellectual property risk
- Privacy risk
- Operational legal risk

Risks should be assessed, documented, monitored, and mitigated.

---

# Legal Reviews

Legal reviews should include:

- Policy reviews
- Contract reviews
- Compliance assessments
- Regulatory updates
- Risk evaluations
- Governance reviews

Review outcomes should drive continuous legal improvements.

---

# Governance

Legal governance should define:

- Legal ownership
- Approval authority
- Review procedures
- Compliance responsibilities
- Documentation standards
- Continuous improvement

Governance should ensure consistent legal practices across the platform.

---

# Architecture Principles

Legal Architecture should:

- Be compliant
- Be transparent
- Be auditable
- Be measurable
- Support continuous improvement
- Follow Documentation First principles
