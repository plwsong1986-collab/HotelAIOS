# Purpose

This document defines the legal governance framework for HotelAIOS.

Legal Governance establishes the policies, organizational responsibilities, decision-making processes, compliance mechanisms, review procedures, and continuous improvement practices required to ensure lawful, transparent, consistent, and accountable management of all legal activities across the HotelAIOS platform.

---

# Objectives

The Legal Governance should:

- Establish legal governance standards
- Define legal roles and responsibilities
- Standardize legal policies
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Support organizational accountability
- Enable continuous governance improvement

# Governance Framework

The governance framework should define:

- Governance objectives
- Legal principles
- Decision authority
- Accountability model
- Governance responsibilities
- Review mechanisms

The framework should ensure consistent legal oversight across the platform.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

Legal governance should define responsibilities for:

- Executive Management
- Legal Team
- Compliance Team
- Security Team
- Platform Engineering
- Operations Team
- Business Owners

Every legal process should have a clearly assigned owner.

---

# Legal Policies

Legal policies should establish requirements for:

- Privacy
- Terms of Service
- Data compliance
- Intellectual property
- Contract management
- Regulatory compliance
- Risk management
- Record retention

Policies should be documented, approved, and regularly reviewed.

---

# Compliance Management

Compliance management should include:

- Regulatory monitoring
- Policy implementation
- Compliance assessments
- Evidence collection
- Corrective actions
- Compliance reporting

Compliance activities should be continuously monitored.

---

# Decision Management

Legal decision management should support:

- Policy approvals
- Contract approvals
- Regulatory decisions
- Risk acceptance
- Exception management
- Governance documentation

Decisions should be documented and traceable.

---

# Legal Reviews

Legal reviews should include:

- Policy reviews
- Compliance reviews
- Contract reviews
- Regulatory assessments
- Governance evaluations
- Audit reviews

Review outcomes should produce measurable improvement actions.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement should support:

- Policy optimization
- Regulatory adaptation
- Governance maturity
- Documentation improvements
- Process optimization
- Lessons learned

Improvement initiatives should be prioritized according to legal risk and organizational value.

---

# Governance Metrics

Legal governance should measure:

- Policy compliance
- Regulatory compliance
- Contract review completion
- Audit findings
- Risk reduction progress
- Legal issue resolution time
- Governance review completion
- Compliance training completion

Metrics should support evidence-based governance decisions.

---

# Governance Principles

Legal Governance should:

- Be transparent
- Be accountable
- Be measurable
- Be auditable
- Support continuous improvement
- Follow Documentation First principles
