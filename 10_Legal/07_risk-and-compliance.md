# Purpose

This document defines the risk and compliance management framework for HotelAIOS.

Risk and Compliance establishes the policies, governance structure, operational processes, assessment methods, monitoring activities, and continuous improvement mechanisms required to identify, evaluate, mitigate, and monitor legal, regulatory, operational, and business risks while ensuring ongoing compliance across the platform.

---

# Objectives

The Risk and Compliance should:

- Establish enterprise risk management
- Standardize compliance processes
- Reduce legal and operational risks
- Support regulatory compliance
- Improve organizational resilience
- Enable continuous governance improvement

# Governance Framework

The governance framework should define:

- Governance objectives
- Risk ownership
- Compliance ownership
- Decision authority
- Reporting responsibilities
- Review mechanisms

Governance should align risk management with organizational objectives.

---

# Risk Identification

Risk identification should include:

- Legal risks
- Regulatory risks
- Operational risks
- Information security risks
- Vendor risks
- Business continuity risks
- AI governance risks
- Reputational risks

Risks should be identified continuously throughout the platform lifecycle.

---

# Risk Assessment

Risk assessments should evaluate:

- Likelihood
- Business impact
- Operational impact
- Financial impact
- Regulatory impact
- Risk priority

Risk evaluations should be documented using standardized assessment criteria.

---

# Risk Treatment

Risk treatment strategies should include:

- Risk avoidance
- Risk reduction
- Risk transfer
- Risk acceptance
- Control implementation
- Continuous monitoring

Treatment plans should include owners, timelines, and measurable outcomes.

---

# Compliance Management

Compliance management should support:

- Regulatory compliance
- Internal policy compliance
- Contractual obligations
- Industry standards
- Audit preparation
- Evidence management

Compliance activities should be integrated into operational processes.

---

# Compliance Monitoring

Compliance monitoring should include:

- Policy compliance
- Control effectiveness
- Regulatory updates
- Risk indicators
- Corrective actions
- Compliance reporting

Monitoring should provide ongoing visibility into organizational compliance.

---

# Audit Management

Audit management should include:

- Internal audits
- External audits
- Compliance reviews
- Control verification
- Evidence collection
- Audit remediation

Audit findings should result in documented corrective actions.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement should support:

- Policy optimization
- Risk reduction
- Process improvements
- Regulatory adaptation
- Lessons learned
- Governance maturity

Improvement initiatives should be prioritized according to organizational risk.

---

# Risk and Compliance Principles

Risk and Compliance should:

- Be proactive
- Be measurable
- Be transparent
- Be auditable
- Support continuous improvement
- Follow Documentation First principles
