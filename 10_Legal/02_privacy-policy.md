# Purpose

This document defines the privacy policy framework for HotelAIOS.

The Privacy Policy establishes the principles, responsibilities, and operational requirements for collecting, processing, storing, sharing, protecting, and deleting personal information while ensuring compliance with applicable privacy regulations and maintaining user trust.

---

# Objectives

The Privacy Policy should:

- Protect personal information
- Define lawful data processing
- Ensure transparency
- Respect user privacy rights
- Support regulatory compliance
- Promote responsible data handling

# Privacy Principles

Privacy management should follow these principles:

- Lawfulness
- Fairness
- Transparency
- Purpose Limitation
- Data Minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage Limitation
- Integrity and Confidentiality
- Accountability

Privacy requirements should be integrated throughout the platform.

---

# Personal Data Collection

The platform may collect:

- Account information
- Reservation information
- Contact information
- Payment-related information
- Device information
- Usage analytics
- Communication records

Only information necessary for legitimate business purposes should be collected.

---

# Data Processing

Personal information may be processed for:

- User authentication
- Reservation management
- Customer support
- Payment processing
- Platform security
- Service improvement
- Legal compliance

Processing activities should have an appropriate legal basis.

---

# Data Storage

Personal information should be stored using:

- Secure infrastructure
- Encryption at rest
- Access controls
- Backup protection
- Geographic redundancy
- Retention management

Stored information should remain protected throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Data Sharing

Personal information may be shared with:

- Authorized service providers
- Payment providers
- OTA partners
- Regulatory authorities
- Legal authorities when required
- Approved business partners

Data sharing should be limited to legitimate and authorized purposes.

---

# User Rights

Users should be able to exercise applicable privacy rights, including:

- Access personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion where applicable
- Request data portability where applicable
- Object to certain processing where applicable
- Withdraw consent when consent is the legal basis for processing

Requests should be handled according to applicable laws and internal procedures.

---

# Data Retention

Retention policies should define:

- Business retention requirements
- Legal retention requirements
- Archive policies
- Secure deletion procedures
- Backup retention
- Record ownership

Personal information should not be retained longer than necessary.

---

# Governance

Privacy governance should define:

- Privacy ownership
- Data controller responsibilities
- Data stewardship
- Privacy reviews
- Compliance monitoring
- Continuous improvement

Governance should ensure consistent privacy protection across the platform.

---

# Privacy Principles

The Privacy Policy should:

- Be transparent
- Be accountable
- Be secure
- Be compliant
- Support continuous improvement
- Follow Documentation First principles
