# Purpose

This document defines the data compliance framework for HotelAIOS.

Data Compliance establishes the policies, controls, responsibilities, and operational procedures required to ensure that all data collection, processing, storage, sharing, retention, and disposal activities comply with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and organizational requirements.

---

# Objectives

The Data Compliance should:

- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Protect personal and business data
- Standardize compliance processes
- Support data governance
- Reduce legal and operational risks
- Enable continuous compliance improvement

# Compliance Framework

The compliance framework should define:

- Compliance objectives
- Applicable regulations
- Organizational responsibilities
- Compliance controls
- Documentation standards
- Continuous improvement

Compliance activities should be integrated into daily platform operations.

---

# Regulatory Requirements

Compliance should support applicable requirements including:

- Privacy regulations
- Consumer protection laws
- Data protection regulations
- Financial regulations where applicable
- Industry standards
- Local legal requirements

Regulatory requirements should be reviewed regularly.

---

# Data Processing Compliance

Data processing should comply with:

- Lawful processing requirements
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy requirements
- Storage limitation
- Accountability obligations

Processing activities should be documented and periodically reviewed.

---

# Cross-Border Data Transfer

Cross-border data transfers should include:

- Legal transfer mechanisms
- Regional compliance validation
- Data protection safeguards
- Vendor assessments
- Contractual protections
- Transfer documentation

International data transfers should comply with applicable jurisdictional requirements.

---

# Record Management

Compliance records should include:

- Processing activities
- Consent records
- Audit evidence
- Compliance reviews
- Risk assessments
- Regulatory communications

Records should remain accurate, complete, and securely maintained.

---

# Compliance Auditing

Compliance audits should evaluate:

- Policy compliance
- Regulatory compliance
- Operational controls
- Data governance
- Security controls
- Documentation completeness

Audit findings should generate measurable corrective actions.

---

# Incident Reporting

Compliance incidents should include:

- Regulatory violations
- Data handling issues
- Privacy complaints
- Unauthorized disclosures
- Reporting obligations
- Corrective actions

Incidents should be documented, investigated, and resolved according to established procedures.

---

# Governance

Data compliance governance should define:

- Compliance ownership
- Regulatory responsibilities
- Review schedules
- Audit requirements
- Reporting procedures
- Continuous improvement

Governance should ensure consistent compliance across the platform.

---

# Compliance Principles

Data Compliance should:

- Be compliant
- Be transparent
- Be auditable
- Be measurable
- Support accountability
- Follow Documentation First principles
