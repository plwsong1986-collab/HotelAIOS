# Purpose

This document defines the contract management framework for HotelAIOS.

Contract Management establishes the policies, lifecycle processes, operational responsibilities, approval procedures, and governance required to create, review, execute, maintain, renew, and retire contracts throughout the platform ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Contract Management should:

- Standardize contract management
- Protect organizational interests
- Reduce contractual risks
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Improve contract visibility
- Support continuous governance

# Contract Lifecycle

The contract lifecycle should include:

- Contract request
- Draft preparation
- Internal review
- Legal review
- Approval
- Execution
- Active management
- Renewal
- Termination
- Archive

Every contract should follow a documented lifecycle.

---

# Contract Types

Contract management should support:

- Customer agreements
- Supplier contracts
- Vendor agreements
- Partner agreements
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Software licenses
- Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

Each contract type should follow standardized templates where appropriate.

---

# Contract Approval

Contract approval should define:

- Approval authority
- Legal review requirements
- Financial approval
- Risk assessment
- Executive approval
- Version control

Approval procedures should be documented and auditable.

---

# Contract Repository

Contracts should be maintained in a centralized repository containing:

- Executed agreements
- Contract versions
- Approval records
- Amendments
- Supporting documents
- Renewal schedules

Access to contracts should be restricted according to organizational roles.

---

# Contract Monitoring

Contract monitoring should track:

- Contract status
- Performance obligations
- Service commitments
- Compliance requirements
- Expiration dates
- Renewal milestones

Monitoring should ensure contractual obligations are fulfilled.

---

# Contract Renewal

Renewal management should include:

- Renewal notifications
- Contract review
- Performance evaluation
- Pricing review
- Risk reassessment
- Renewal approval

Renewal decisions should be completed before contract expiration.

---

# Risk Management

Contract risk management should address:

- Legal risks
- Financial risks
- Operational risks
- Compliance risks
- Vendor risks
- Business continuity risks

Contract risks should be regularly assessed and mitigated.

---

# Governance

Contract governance should define:

- Contract ownership
- Approval responsibilities
- Repository management
- Review schedules
- Audit requirements
- Continuous improvement

Governance should ensure consistent contract management across the organization.

---

# Contract Management Principles

Contract Management should:

- Be standardized
- Be transparent
- Be auditable
- Be secure
- Support accountability
- Follow Documentation First principles
