# Purpose

This document defines the official experience-related information associated with the hotel.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for all experiences that are officially offered, organized, supported, or recommended by the hotel.

Brand storytelling, promotional campaigns, travel inspiration, and itinerary design belong to the **Brand**, **Website**, or **Operations** modules and should not be maintained here.

---

# Overview

Experiences extend the guest journey beyond accommodation.

This document provides standardized, factual information about experiences connected with the hotel for HotelAIOS, AI systems, websites, OTA platforms, and operational documentation.

Only stable, verifiable information should be maintained.
