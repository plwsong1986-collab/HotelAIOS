# Purpose

This document defines the official information for all public facilities within the hotel.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for shared spaces, guest-accessible facilities, infrastructure, and public amenities.

Operational procedures, maintenance records, promotional descriptions, and marketing content belong to other modules and should not be maintained here.

---

# Overview

Facilities are the shared physical spaces and amenities available to guests during their stay.

This document provides standardized, factual information for HotelAIOS, AI systems, websites, OTA platforms, and hotel operations.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.

# Scope

This document should answer questions such as:

- What public facilities are available?
- Where are the facilities located?
- When are they available?
- What amenities do they provide?
- Are they accessible?
- Are there any permanent access restrictions?

It should not describe operational procedures, maintenance activities, or promotional experiences.
