# Purpose

This document defines the official guest policies of the hotel.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for all policies that govern reservations, guest stays, payments, occupancy, safety, and expected guest conduct.

Operational procedures, staff workflows, legal agreements, and internal emergency procedures belong to the **Operations** or **Legal** modules and should not be maintained here.

---

# Overview

Policies establish the official rules and conditions that apply to guests before, during, and after their stay.

This document provides standardized, factual policy information for HotelAIOS, AI systems, websites, OTA platforms, and hotel operations.

Only approved and currently effective policies should be maintained.

# Scope

This document should answer questions such as:

- What are the hotel's official policies?
- When can guests check in and check out?
- What payment methods are accepted?
- Are pets allowed?
- What are the smoking rules?
- What guest conduct is expected?

It should not describe staff procedures, operational workflows, or legal contracts.

# Relationship with Other Knowledge Documents

- Location explains where the hotel is located.

This document explains **the rules that apply during a guest's stay**.

# Notes

This document is the authoritative source for hotel guest policies within HotelAIOS.

It should remain factual, stable, and easy to maintain.

Whenever an official hotel policy changes, this document should be reviewed and updated before any downstream modules are modified.

Operational procedures, staff instructions, legal agreements, pricing rules, and promotional terms should remain in the Operations, Legal, Website, or OTA modules to preserve clear ownership boundaries.
