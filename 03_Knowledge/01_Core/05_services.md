# Purpose

This document defines the official guest services provided by the hotel.

It serves as the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** for all service-related information, including service availability, scope, eligibility, operating hours, and reservation requirements.

Service procedures, staff workflows, operating standards, and internal instructions belong to the **Operations** module and should not be maintained here.

---

# Overview

Services are the assistance and activities officially provided to guests during their stay.

This document provides standardized, factual information for HotelAIOS, AI systems, websites, OTA platforms, and hotel operations.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.

# Paid Services

Record optional services requiring an additional charge.

Examples may include:

- Airport transfer
- Laundry service
- Equipment rental
- Guided tours
- Special transportation

Pricing information belongs in commercial or operational documents.

---

# Seasonal Services

Some services may only be available during specific seasons or holidays.

Record:

- Service availability
- Applicable dates or seasons
- General conditions

Avoid temporary promotions or marketing campaigns.

---

# Guest Eligibility

Record permanent eligibility requirements where applicable.

Examples include:

- Registered guests only
- Advance reservation required
- Age restrictions
- Capacity limitations
- Availability by room type

Only include official service eligibility rules.

---

# Scope

This document should answer questions such as:

- What services does the hotel provide?
- When are services available?
- Which services are complimentary?
- Which services require payment?
- Does a service require reservation?
- Who is eligible to use a service?

It should not describe internal workflows, service procedures, or pricing strategies.

---

# Relationship with Other Knowledge Documents

This document owns all guest service information.

Other Knowledge documents define different aspects of the hotel:

- Hotel Overview provides the property's overall identity.
- Location explains where the hotel is located.
- Rooms describe accommodation.
- Facilities describe physical spaces.
- Experiences describe local activities and recommendations.
- Policies define operational rules.
- FAQ answers common guest questions.

This document explains **what services the hotel provides**.

Other documents explain **where guests stay**, **what facilities exist**, and **how hotel policies apply**.

# Notes

This document is the authoritative source for guest services within HotelAIOS.

It should remain factual, concise, and operationally accurate.

Whenever services are introduced, discontinued, or their permanent characteristics change, this document should be reviewed and updated.

Operational procedures, staff responsibilities, service workflows, pricing policies, and promotional campaigns should remain in the Operations, Business, Website, or OTA modules to preserve clear ownership boundaries.
