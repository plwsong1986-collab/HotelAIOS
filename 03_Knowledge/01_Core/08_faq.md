# Purpose

This document provides a centralized collection of frequently asked questions about the hotel.

It serves as the navigation layer for guests, staff, websites, OTA platforms, and AI systems by directing users to the authoritative Knowledge documents.

This document does **not** create or own hotel knowledge.

Every answer should summarize information while referencing the document that owns the topic.

---

# Overview

The FAQ organizes the questions guests most frequently ask before, during, and after their stay.

Questions should be written from the guest's perspective.

Answers should remain concise, factual, and easy to understand.

Detailed information belongs in the corresponding Knowledge document.

---

# FAQ Principles

## Reference Before Duplication

The FAQ summarizes information.

It should always direct readers to the document that owns the subject.

Never duplicate detailed information.

---

## Accuracy

Whenever hotel information changes, update the authoritative Knowledge document first.

Then review this FAQ to ensure all references remain correct.

---

## Guest-Oriented Language

Questions should reflect how guests naturally ask for information.

Answers should use clear, friendly, and factual language.
