# Brand Identity

## Brand Name

Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse

---

## Brand Positioning

Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse is a boutique guesthouse located in the heart of Lijiang Ancient Town, China.

The guesthouse combines modern comfort with traditional Naxi architectural style, offering travelers a peaceful courtyard experience, professional oxygen support, authentic local culture, and convenient access to the most famous attractions in Lijiang.

---

## Core Mission

To provide every guest with a comfortable, healthy, relaxing, and memorable stay while exploring the beauty of Lijiang.

## Vision
