# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for airport transfer information related to the hotel.

It provides factual guidance on transportation options between nearby airports and the hotel, helping guests plan their arrival and departure.

This document does not include temporary pricing, promotional offers, or operational dispatch procedures.

---

# Overview

Airport transfer information is one of the most frequently requested topics before arrival.

This document records official transportation methods, estimated travel times, and planning considerations to support guests, AI assistants, websites, and customer service.
