# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for parking information related to the hotel.

It records factual information about parking availability, parking locations, vehicle access, and parking-related guidance for guests arriving by car.

This document does not include temporary parking arrangements, daily availability updates, or operational parking management procedures.

---

# Overview

Parking information is an important consideration for guests traveling by private vehicle.

This document provides standardized parking information for AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational reference.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Parking availability
- Parking locations
- Parking restrictions
- Vehicle access information
- Electric vehicle charging (if applicable)
- Guest parking guidance
