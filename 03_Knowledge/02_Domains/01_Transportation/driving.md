# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for self-driving transportation information related to the hotel.

It provides factual guidance for guests traveling by private vehicle, including road access, driving considerations, and arrival planning.

This document does not include live traffic conditions, navigation software instructions, or temporary road closures.

---

# Overview

Driving is a common transportation option for domestic travelers and road-trip guests.

This document standardizes self-driving information for AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational reference.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.
