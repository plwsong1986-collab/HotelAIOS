# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for transportation accessibility information related to the hotel.

It records accessibility considerations for guests with mobility needs, including transportation options, arrival methods, and accessibility-related guidance.

This document does not define medical assistance, personal care requirements, or hotel accessibility facilities.

---

# Overview

Accessible transportation information helps guests plan their journeys with confidence.

This document provides standardized accessibility guidance for AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational reference.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.
