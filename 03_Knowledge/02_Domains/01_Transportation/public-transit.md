# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for public transportation information related to the hotel.

It records factual information about public transit options that guests may use to travel to, from, and around the destination.

This document does not include real-time schedules, temporary service disruptions, or fare promotions.

---

# Overview

Public transportation provides an economical and sustainable way for guests to reach the hotel and nearby attractions.

This document standardizes public transit information for AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational reference.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Public bus services
- Urban transit systems
- Airport public transportation
- Railway station public transportation
- Major transfer points
- General public transit guidance

Operational transit management is outside the scope of this document.
