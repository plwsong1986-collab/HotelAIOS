# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for walking-related transportation information around the hotel.

It records factual information about walking access, pedestrian routes, nearby destinations, and walking considerations for guests exploring the local area.

This document does not replace official location information or provide real-time navigation instructions.

---

# Overview

Walking is an important transportation method for guests staying in destinations with historic streets, cultural areas, and nearby attractions.

This document provides standardized walking information for AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational reference.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.
