# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for railway transportation information related to the hotel.

It records factual information about nearby railway stations, railway travel options, and guidance for guests arriving or departing by train.

This document does not include ticket pricing, train schedules, or booking procedures.

---

# Overview

Railway transportation is one of the primary travel methods for domestic visitors.

This document provides standardized railway information for guests, AI assistants, websites, and operational reference.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.
