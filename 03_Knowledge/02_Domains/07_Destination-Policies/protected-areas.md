---

# Overview

Many destinations designate specific locations as protected areas to preserve natural environments, biodiversity, cultural heritage, archaeological sites, or historically significant locations.

Protected areas are managed by authorized public agencies or designated organizations. Access conditions, visitor activities, and conservation requirements vary according to the responsible authority and the type of protected area.

This document defines general concepts without describing destination-specific management policies.

# Core Information

Common categories of protected areas include:

| Protected Area Category | General Description |
|--------------------------|---------------------|
| National Parks | Protected natural areas managed for conservation and public enjoyment |
| Nature Reserves | Areas established to preserve ecosystems, habitats, or biodiversity |
| Marine Protected Areas | Coastal or marine environments under conservation management |
| Cultural Heritage Sites | Protected locations of historical or cultural significance |
| Archaeological Sites | Areas preserving archaeological resources |
| Scenic Conservation Areas | Landscapes protected for natural, cultural, or aesthetic value |
| Wildlife Sanctuaries | Areas designated for wildlife protection |
| Protected Forests | Forest areas managed for long-term conservation |

Management objectives and visitor access differ according to the responsible authority.
