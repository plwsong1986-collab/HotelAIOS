# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for destination guest etiquette knowledge within the Destination Policies Domain.

It defines factual, implementation-independent information about commonly accepted visitor behavior, cultural courtesy, and public etiquette expectations encountered while traveling. The document supports AI assistants, concierge teams, guest services, reservation support, and other downstream systems through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Many destinations encourage visitors to follow generally accepted standards of respectful behavior when interacting with local communities, public facilities, cultural sites, and natural environments.

Guest etiquette reflects social expectations rather than legal requirements. Expectations vary across destinations, cultures, and individual communities.

This document describes common hospitality and travel etiquette concepts without prescribing destination-specific customs.

# Core Information

Common etiquette expectations may include:

| Etiquette Category | General Description |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| Respect | Show consideration for local residents, visitors, and staff |
| Public Behavior | Conduct oneself appropriately in shared spaces |
| Noise Awareness | Minimize unnecessary disturbance to others |
| Queuing | Follow local practices when waiting for services |
| Photography Courtesy | Respect photography restrictions and personal privacy |
| Cleanliness | Help maintain clean public environments |
| Cultural Sensitivity | Recognize that customs may differ across destinations |
| Personal Conduct | Behave responsibly in public areas |

Appropriate etiquette depends on the destination and local community expectations.
