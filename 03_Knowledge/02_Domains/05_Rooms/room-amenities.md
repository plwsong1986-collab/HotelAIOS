# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for room amenity knowledge within the Rooms Domain.

It defines factual, implementation-independent information about amenities, facilities, and standard in-room items commonly provided in hotel guest rooms. The document supports AI assistants, guest services, concierge teams, reservation support, and other downstream systems through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Room amenities are the facilities, equipment, and guest-use items provided within a hotel room to support comfort, convenience, and functionality.

Amenities vary by hotel category, room type, destination, and property standards. This document records stable hospitality concepts rather than hotel-specific inventories.

# Core Information

Typical room amenities may include:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Furniture | Desk, chair, wardrobe, luggage rack |
| Bathroom | Shower, bathtub, hair dryer, towels, toiletries |
| Entertainment | Television, streaming device, radio |
| Connectivity | Wi-Fi, telephone, charging outlets |
| Comfort | Air conditioning, heating, blackout curtains |
| Refreshments | Kettle, coffee maker, tea set, minibar, refrigerator |
| Safety | In-room safe, smoke detector, emergency information |
| Workspace | Desk, task lighting, power outlets |

Actual amenities depend on each property's official standards.
