# Overview

Bed types describe the size, configuration, and arrangement of beds provided in hotel guest rooms.

Although dimensions and naming conventions may vary by country, hotel brand, or regional standards, many bed types are widely recognized throughout the hospitality industry.

This document records stable knowledge about bed classifications without describing individual hotel inventory or availability.

# Core Information

Typical hotel bed types may include:

| Bed Type | General Description |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Single Bed | Designed for one guest |
| Twin Beds | Two separate single beds within one room |
| Double Bed | Bed intended for one or two guests depending on regional standards |
| Queen Bed | Medium-sized bed commonly used for two guests |
| King Bed | Large bed designed for two guests |
| Sofa Bed | Convertible seating that can function as a bed |
| Bunk Bed | Two or more beds stacked vertically, commonly used in family or group accommodations |

Actual bed dimensions may vary by country, manufacturer, or hotel brand.
