# Overview

Family rooms are guest accommodations designed to support families or groups traveling together by providing additional sleeping capacity, flexible layouts, or family-oriented facilities.

The exact configuration of a family room varies by property, destination, and hotel brand. This document records stable hospitality concepts rather than hotel-specific room inventory.
