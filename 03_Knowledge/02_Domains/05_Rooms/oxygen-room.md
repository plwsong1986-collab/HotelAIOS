# Overview

An oxygen room is a guest room equipped with an oxygen enrichment system designed to maintain a higher oxygen concentration than the surrounding environment.

Such rooms are commonly found in high-altitude destinations where reduced atmospheric oxygen may affect guest comfort. The availability, design, operating standards, and oxygen concentration vary by property and local regulations.

This document records general hospitality knowledge and does not describe medical treatment or clinical oxygen therapy.
