# Overview

Couples travel focuses on experiences shared by two travelers.

This guide provides general travel knowledge that helps couples prepare for trips, understand common activity categories, and organize travel plans without recommending specific destinations, businesses, or commercial products.

The guide remains descriptive rather than advisory and is applicable across destinations.

## Shared Travel Experience

Couples commonly travel to:

- Explore destinations together
- Experience local culture
- Visit attractions
- Enjoy outdoor environments
- Discover local dining
- Relax and spend time together
- Participate in recreational activities

Individual travel preferences vary.
