# Overview

Food discovery is an important part of many travel experiences.

This guide organizes general knowledge about exploring local cuisine, understanding regional food culture, and discovering different dining experiences without recommending specific restaurants, businesses, or commercial services.

The guide is intended to provide descriptive travel knowledge applicable across destinations.
