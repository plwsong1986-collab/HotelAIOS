# Core Information

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What types of dining options are commonly available?

Dining options vary by destination and may include restaurants, cafés, street food vendors, food courts, markets, and takeaway establishments.

Detailed classifications remain owned by the Dining Domain.

---

### Do restaurants require reservations?

Reservation requirements vary by restaurant, location, operating hours, and demand.

Official reservation information should be obtained directly from the restaurant where applicable.

---

### Are dietary requirements commonly supported?

Support for dietary requirements depends on the restaurant and available menu options.

Guests should communicate dietary needs directly with the dining establishment.

---

### Are local dining customs different?

Yes.

Dining customs, meal schedules, tipping practices, and table etiquette differ between destinations and cultures.

Destination-specific customs remain outside the scope of this document.

---

### Are food allergies handled the same everywhere?

No.

Procedures and available information vary by restaurant and jurisdiction.

Guests should communicate allergies directly with the dining establishment.

---

### What payment methods are commonly accepted?

Accepted payment methods vary by restaurant and destination.

Official payment information should be confirmed with the dining establishment.
