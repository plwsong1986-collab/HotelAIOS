# Core Information

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is considered a tourist attraction?

A tourist attraction is a place, site, landmark, or experience that visitors commonly travel to see or participate in.

Detailed attraction classifications remain owned by the Attractions Domain.

---

### Do attractions require advance reservations?

Reservation requirements vary by attraction.

Official information should be obtained from the attraction operator or responsible authority.

---

### Are all attractions open throughout the year?

Operating schedules vary according to the attraction, season, weather conditions, maintenance, or local regulations.

---

### Are attractions accessible?

Accessibility varies by attraction, infrastructure, and local conditions.

Official accessibility information should be obtained from the attraction operator where applicable.

---

### How can visitors travel to attractions?

Transportation options depend on the destination and may include public transportation, taxis, rideshare services, shuttle services, private vehicles, cycling, or walking.

Transportation information is maintained by the Transportation Domain.

---

### Are some attractions located in protected areas?

Yes.

Certain attractions may be located within protected natural, cultural, or historical areas where additional visitor requirements may apply.

Protected area concepts remain owned by the Destination Policies Domain.
