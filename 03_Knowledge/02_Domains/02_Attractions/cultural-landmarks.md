# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for cultural landmarks located near the hotel.

It records factual information about culturally significant places that guests may visit during their stay. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for cultural landmark knowledge.

---

# Overview

Cultural landmarks represent the local history, traditions, architecture, and identity of a destination.

This document provides structured information that helps guests understand what cultural landmarks are available nearby without serving as a travel guide or promotional material.

Only stable and verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Heritage landmarks
- Cultural monuments
- Historic public buildings
- Traditional districts
- Cultural heritage sites
- Architectural landmarks

This document does not cover:

- Museums
- Religious sites
- Natural attractions
- Seasonal attractions
- Events or festivals

These topics are maintained in their respective documents.
