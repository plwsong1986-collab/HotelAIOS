# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for historical sites located near the hotel.

It records factual information about places of historical significance that guests may visit during their stay. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for historical site knowledge.

---

# Overview

Historical sites preserve the history, architecture, and heritage of a destination.

This document provides structured and verifiable information to help guests understand available historical sites without serving as a travel guide or promotional material.

Only stable and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Historic buildings
- Archaeological sites
- Historic districts
- Fortifications
- Heritage structures
- Officially recognized historical sites

This document does not cover:

- Museums
- Religious sites
- Cultural landmarks without historical designation
- Natural attractions
- Temporary exhibitions

These topics are maintained in their respective documents.
