# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for nightlife destinations located near the hotel.

It records factual information about evening and nighttime entertainment areas and venues that guests may visit during their stay. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for nightlife knowledge.

---

# Overview

Nightlife includes destinations primarily visited during the evening or nighttime for entertainment, social activities, performances, or scenic experiences.

This document provides structured and verifiable information without serving as an entertainment guide or promotional material.

Only stable and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Entertainment districts
- Bar streets
- Live music venues
- Performance venues
- Rooftop venues
- Night observation points
- Evening waterfront districts
- Other established nightlife destinations

This document does not cover:

- Individual restaurants
- Temporary festivals
- Seasonal events
- Shopping areas
- Hotel-operated entertainment facilities

These topics are maintained in their respective documents.
