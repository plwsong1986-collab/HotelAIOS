# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for shopping areas located near the hotel.

It records factual information about shopping districts, malls, retail streets, and commercial centers that guests may visit during their stay. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for shopping area knowledge.

---

# Overview

Shopping areas are destinations primarily intended for retail, dining, and commercial activities.

This document provides structured and verifiable information about nearby shopping destinations without serving as a shopping guide or promotional material.

Only stable and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Shopping malls
- Shopping centers
- Commercial districts
- Retail streets
- Outlet centers
- Department stores
- Mixed-use retail developments
- Local shopping areas

This document does not cover:

- Individual restaurants
- Markets held as temporary events
- Night markets associated with seasonal festivals
- Hotel retail facilities

These topics are maintained in their respective documents.
