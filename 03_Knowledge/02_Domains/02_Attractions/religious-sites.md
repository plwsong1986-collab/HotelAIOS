# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for religious sites located near the hotel.

It records factual information about places of worship and religious heritage that guests may visit during their stay. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for religious site knowledge.

---

# Overview

Religious sites are places dedicated to worship, spiritual practice, religious history, or pilgrimage.

This document provides structured and verifiable information about nearby religious sites without serving as a travel guide or providing religious interpretation.

Only stable and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Temples
- Churches
- Cathedrals
- Mosques
- Synagogues
- Monasteries
- Shrines
- Other officially recognized places of worship

This document does not cover:

- Museums
- Historical sites whose primary purpose is not religious
- Cultural landmarks without active or historical religious significance
- Religious festivals or events

These topics are maintained in their respective documents.
