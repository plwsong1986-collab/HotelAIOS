# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for parks, gardens, and managed green spaces located near the hotel.

It records factual information about public parks, botanical gardens, landscaped gardens, and other maintained recreational green spaces that guests may visit during their stay.

The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for parks and gardens.

---

# Overview

Parks and gardens are publicly or privately managed green spaces designed for recreation, conservation, education, or landscape appreciation.

This document provides structured and verifiable information without serving as a travel guide or promotional material.

Only stable and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Urban parks
- Public parks
- Botanical gardens
- Landscape gardens
- Arboretums
- Waterfront parks
- Recreation parks
- Managed green spaces

This document does not cover:

- Mountains
- Lakes
- Beaches
- Forests
- Nature reserves
- Geological attractions

These topics are maintained in `natural-attractions.md`.
