# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for family-friendly attractions located near the hotel.

It records factual information about attractions that are generally suitable for families traveling with children. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for family attraction knowledge.

---

# Overview

Family attractions are destinations that provide experiences appropriate for visitors of different ages, particularly children and accompanying adults.

This document organizes attractions based on visitor suitability rather than attraction type.

Only stable and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Zoos
- Aquariums
- Children's museums
- Science centers
- Theme parks
- Water parks
- Interactive learning centers
- Indoor play centers
- Family entertainment centers
- Other attractions designed for or suitable for families

This document does not cover:

- General museums
- Historical sites
- Natural attractions
- Parks and gardens
- Seasonal events

These topics remain owned by their respective documents.
