# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for natural attractions located near the hotel.

It records factual information about naturally occurring landscapes and scenic destinations that guests may visit during their stay. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for natural attraction knowledge.

---

# Overview

Natural attractions are destinations primarily valued for their natural environment, geological features, ecosystems, or scenic beauty.

This document provides structured and verifiable information without serving as a travel guide or promotional material.

Only stable and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Mountains
- Lakes
- Rivers
- Beaches
- Waterfalls
- Forests
- Nature reserves
- Scenic viewpoints
- Coastal areas
- Geological formations

This document does not cover:

- Urban parks
- Botanical gardens
- Historical sites
- Cultural landmarks
- Seasonal events

These topics are maintained in their respective documents.
