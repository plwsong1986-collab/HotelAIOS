# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for seasonal attraction information relevant to destinations near the hotel.

It records factual information about attractions whose visitor experience, accessibility, or significance varies by season. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for seasonal attraction knowledge.

---

# Overview

Seasonal attractions describe when destinations are most suitable to visit based on recurring seasonal conditions.

This document classifies attractions by time rather than by attraction type.

Only stable, recurring, and officially verifiable seasonal information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Seasonal scenic areas
- Flower viewing seasons
- Autumn foliage locations
- Winter landscape destinations
- Cherry blossom viewing areas
- Seasonal wildlife viewing
- Seasonal natural phenomena
- Attractions with recurring seasonal accessibility

This document does not cover:

- Permanent attraction descriptions
- Temporary festivals
- One-time events
- Weather forecasts
- Emergency closures

Permanent attraction information remains in its respective SSOT document.

# Single Source of Truth

This document owns recurring seasonal information related to attractions, including:

- Seasonal characteristics
- Typical visiting periods
- Seasonal accessibility
- Seasonal planning information

It does not own the permanent description of any attraction. Permanent attraction data remains in the corresponding attraction document.

Transportation information remains owned by the Transportation Domain.
