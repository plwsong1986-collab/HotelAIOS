# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for museums located near the hotel.

It records factual information about museums, galleries, exhibition venues, and related cultural institutions that guests may visit during their stay.

The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, and operational teams by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for museum-related knowledge.

---

# Overview

Museums preserve, interpret, and exhibit cultural, artistic, scientific, or historical collections.

This document provides structured and verifiable information about nearby museums without serving as a travel guide or promotional material.

Only stable and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Art museums
- History museums
- Science museums
- Local heritage museums
- Galleries with permanent exhibitions
- Public exhibition institutions

This document does not cover:

- Historical sites
- Cultural landmarks
- Religious sites
- Temporary events
- Commercial exhibitions

These topics are maintained in their respective documents.
