# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for experience review knowledge within the Guest Reviews Domain.

It defines the common concepts, review topics, and descriptive information associated with overall guest experiences throughout a stay. The document supports AI assistants, search, analytics, reporting, and other downstream consumers through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Experience reviews summarize a guest's overall perception of their stay by combining impressions from multiple aspects of the visit.

Unlike reviews focused on a single area, experience reviews provide a broader perspective that may include accommodations, services, facilities, dining, location, and the overall travel experience.

This document defines experience review concepts only and does not describe hotel operations, customer service workflows, or commercial activities.

# Core Information

## Experience Review Topics

Experience reviews commonly discuss:

- Overall satisfaction
- First impressions
- Check-in and departure experiences
- Comfort throughout the stay
- Consistency of service
- Property atmosphere
- Convenience
- Value perception
- Likelihood of returning

The emphasis on individual topics varies between guests.

---

## Common Review Characteristics

Experience reviews are generally:

- Holistic
- Experience-based
- Subjective
- Time-specific
- Descriptive

Each review reflects an individual guest's overall perception of a completed stay.

---

## Frequently Mentioned Themes

Guests may describe:

- Memorable experiences
- Positive highlights
- Areas for improvement
- Overall convenience
- Relaxation
- Hospitality
- Expectations versus experience
- Overall recommendation sentiment

These themes support semantic organization and AI retrieval.

---

## Review Interpretation

Experience reviews should be interpreted by considering:

- Individual expectations
- Purpose of travel
- Length of stay
- Personal preferences
- Visit timing

Reviews should complement, rather than replace, authoritative hotel information.
