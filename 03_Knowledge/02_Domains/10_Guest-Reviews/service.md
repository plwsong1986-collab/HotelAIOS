# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for service review knowledge within the Guest Reviews Domain.

It defines the common concepts, review topics, and descriptive information associated with guest feedback about hotel services and staff interactions. The document supports AI assistants, search, analytics, reporting, and other downstream consumers through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Service reviews describe guest experiences related to services provided during a stay, including interactions with hotel staff and the perceived quality of service delivery.

Reviews commonly summarize subjective opinions regarding responsiveness, professionalism, communication, efficiency, hospitality, and the overall service experience.

This document defines service review concepts only and does not describe operational procedures, employee management, customer support workflows, or quality management processes.

# Core Information

## Service Review Topics

Service reviews commonly discuss:

- Staff friendliness
- Professionalism
- Responsiveness
- Communication
- Efficiency
- Courtesy
- Knowledge
- Problem resolution experiences
- Check-in and check-out service
- Overall service experience

The emphasis on individual topics varies by guest and property.

---

## Common Review Characteristics

Service reviews are generally:

- Experience-based
- Subjective
- Time-specific
- Descriptive
- Interaction-focused

Individual reviews represent personal experiences rather than objective evaluations of service quality.

---

## Frequently Mentioned Themes

Guests may describe:

- Hospitality
- Attentiveness
- Helpfulness
- Timeliness
- Professional conduct
- Communication quality
- Consistency
- Overall satisfaction

These themes support semantic organization and AI retrieval.

---

## Review Interpretation

Service reviews should be interpreted by considering:

- Individual expectations
- Nature of guest interactions
- Visit timing
- Service context
- Personal preferences

Reviews should complement, rather than replace, authoritative hotel information.
