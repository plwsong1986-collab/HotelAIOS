# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for guest suggestion knowledge within the Guest Reviews Domain.

It defines the common concepts, structure, and classification of suggestions voluntarily provided by guests as part of their feedback. The document supports AI assistants, search, analytics, reporting, and other downstream consumers through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Guest suggestions are recommendations, ideas, or observations submitted by guests to improve future guest experiences.

Unlike experience reviews, which primarily describe past experiences, suggestions focus on potential improvements from the guest's perspective.

This document defines guest suggestion concepts only and does not describe operational improvement processes, service recovery procedures, or internal decision-making.

# Core Information

## Definition

A guest suggestion is voluntary feedback proposing an improvement, enhancement, or alternative that may improve future guest experiences.

Suggestions may accompany reviews or be submitted independently.

---

## Common Suggestion Categories

Suggestions commonly relate to:

- Accommodation
- Facilities
- Dining
- Guest services
- Technology
- Accessibility
- Transportation
- Wayfinding
- Amenities
- Overall guest experience

Categories may vary between properties and review platforms.

---

## Common Characteristics

Guest suggestions are generally:

- Experience-based
- Improvement-oriented
- Subjective
- Descriptive
- Non-binding

Suggestions represent individual perspectives and should not be interpreted as operational requirements.

---

## Frequently Mentioned Themes

Guests may suggest improvements related to:

- Convenience
- Comfort
- Efficiency
- Communication
- Facility enhancements
- Service availability
- Information clarity
- Accessibility

These themes support semantic organization and AI retrieval.

---

## Suggestion Interpretation

Guest suggestions should be interpreted by considering:

- Individual expectations
- Personal travel preferences
- Visit context
- Property characteristics
- Overall guest experience

Suggestions provide additional context but do not represent official hotel commitments.
