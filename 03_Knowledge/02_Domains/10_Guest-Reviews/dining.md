# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for dining review knowledge within the Guest Reviews Domain.

It defines the common concepts, review topics, and descriptive information associated with guest feedback about dining experiences. The document supports AI assistants, search, analytics, reporting, and other downstream consumers through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Dining reviews describe guest experiences related to restaurants, cafés, breakfast services, bars, room service, or other food and beverage offerings.

Reviews commonly summarize subjective opinions regarding food quality, service quality, cleanliness, atmosphere, value perception, and overall dining experiences.

This document defines dining review concepts only and does not describe restaurant operations, food preparation, pricing, or service management.

# Core Information

## Dining Review Topics

Dining reviews commonly discuss:

- Food quality
- Beverage quality
- Taste
- Portion size
- Menu variety
- Breakfast experience
- Restaurant atmosphere
- Cleanliness
- Staff interactions
- Overall dining experience

The relevance of individual topics varies by property.

---

## Common Review Characteristics

Dining reviews are generally:

- Experience-based
- Subjective
- Time-specific
- Descriptive
- Independent between guests

Individual reviews represent personal experiences rather than objective measurements.

---

## Frequently Mentioned Themes

Guests may describe:

- Food freshness
- Flavor
- Presentation
- Service efficiency
- Dining environment
- Waiting time
- Value perception
- Overall satisfaction

These themes support semantic organization and AI retrieval.

---

## Review Interpretation

Dining reviews should be interpreted by considering:

- Individual preferences
- Dietary expectations
- Meal period
- Visit timing
- Personal expectations

Reviews should complement, rather than replace, authoritative hotel and restaurant information.
