# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for facilities review knowledge within the Guest Reviews Domain.

It defines the common concepts, review topics, and descriptive information associated with guest feedback about hotel facilities. The document supports AI assistants, search, analytics, reporting, and other downstream consumers through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Facilities reviews describe guest experiences related to shared hotel amenities and physical facilities.

Reviews commonly summarize subjective opinions regarding facility quality, cleanliness, maintenance, availability, convenience, and overall usability.

This document defines facilities review concepts only and does not describe facility operations, maintenance procedures, or operational management.

# Core Information

## Facilities Review Topics

Facilities reviews commonly discuss:

- Swimming pools
- Fitness centers
- Spa facilities
- Business facilities
- Meeting spaces
- Public areas
- Parking facilities
- Elevators
- Wi-Fi in common areas
- Recreational amenities

The facilities discussed vary by property.

---

## Common Review Characteristics

Facilities reviews are generally:

- Experience-based
- Subjective
- Time-specific
- Descriptive
- Independent between guests

Individual reviews represent personal experiences rather than objective assessments.

---

## Frequently Mentioned Themes

Guests may describe:

- Cleanliness
- Availability
- Comfort
- Maintenance condition
- Convenience
- Accessibility
- Crowding
- Overall satisfaction

These themes support semantic organization and AI retrieval.

---

## Review Interpretation

Facilities reviews should be interpreted by considering:

- Individual expectations
- Facility availability during the visit
- Visit timing
- Intended use
- Personal preferences

Reviews should complement, rather than replace, authoritative hotel information.
