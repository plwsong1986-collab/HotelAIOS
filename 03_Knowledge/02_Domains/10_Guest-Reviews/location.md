# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for location review knowledge within the Guest Reviews Domain.

It defines the common concepts, review topics, and descriptive information associated with guest feedback about a property's location. The document supports AI assistants, search, analytics, reporting, and other downstream consumers through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Location reviews describe guest experiences and opinions regarding the geographical position of a property and its surrounding environment.

Reviews commonly summarize subjective perceptions of convenience, accessibility, nearby attractions, transportation access, neighborhood characteristics, and overall location suitability.

This document defines location review concepts only and does not describe destination information, transportation systems, mapping services, or operational planning.

# Core Information

## Location Review Topics

Location reviews commonly discuss:

- Accessibility
- Transportation convenience
- Walking convenience
- Nearby attractions
- Nearby dining
- Shopping proximity
- Neighborhood environment
- Safety perception
- Scenic surroundings
- Overall location convenience

The emphasis on individual topics varies between guests and properties.

---

## Common Review Characteristics

Location reviews are generally:

- Experience-based
- Subjective
- Time-specific
- Descriptive
- Context-dependent

Individual reviews represent personal experiences rather than authoritative geographic information.

Guests may describe:

## Review Interpretation

Location reviews should be interpreted by considering:

- Individual travel purposes
- Transportation preferences
- Visit timing
- Personal expectations
- Familiarity with the destination

Reviews should complement, rather than replace, authoritative destination and location information.
