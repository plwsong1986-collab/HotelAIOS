# Overview

Accommodation reviews describe guest experiences associated with lodging facilities and physical guest rooms.

Reviews commonly summarize subjective opinions regarding comfort, cleanliness, functionality, maintenance, and overall accommodation quality.

This document defines accommodation review concepts only and does not describe hotel operations, maintenance procedures, or service workflows.

# Core Information

## Accommodation Review Topics

Accommodation reviews commonly discuss:

- Room cleanliness
- Bed comfort
- Room size
- Noise levels
- Interior condition
- Temperature comfort
- Bathroom condition
- Lighting
- Furniture
- Overall room experience

The relevance of individual topics varies by property.

---

## Common Review Characteristics

Accommodation reviews are generally:

- Experience-based
- Subjective
- Time-specific
- Descriptive
- Independent between guests

Individual reviews represent personal experiences rather than objective quality measurements.

---

## Frequently Mentioned Themes

Guests may describe:

- Comfort
- Cleanliness
- Quietness
- Spaciousness
- Maintenance condition
- Sleep quality
- Convenience
- Overall satisfaction

These themes support semantic organization and AI retrieval.

---

## Review Interpretation

Accommodation reviews should be interpreted by considering:

- Individual preferences
- Visit timing
- Length of stay
- Room category
- Personal expectations

Reviews should complement, rather than replace, authoritative hotel information.
