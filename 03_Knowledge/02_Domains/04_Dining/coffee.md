# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for coffee-related knowledge within the Dining Domain.

It defines factual, implementation-independent information about coffee beverages, coffee service, and coffee venues commonly encountered by hotel guests. The document supports AI assistants, guest services, concierge teams, and other downstream systems through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Coffee is one of the most widely consumed beverages worldwide and is commonly available in hotels, cafés, restaurants, and takeaway outlets.

This document records stable knowledge related to coffee rather than information about specific businesses or hotel operations.
