# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for local food knowledge within the Dining Domain.

It defines factual, implementation-independent information about regional cuisine, signature dishes, ingredients, and food specialties that guests may encounter during their stay. The document supports AI assistants, guest services, concierge teams, and other downstream systems through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Local food represents the characteristic cuisine of a destination and reflects its regional ingredients, cooking methods, and culinary traditions.

This document records stable information about local dishes and food specialties. It does not include restaurant recommendations, cultural history, or subjective opinions.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Regional cuisine
- Signature dishes
- Traditional foods
- Local specialties
- Common ingredients
- Typical cooking methods
- Seasonal specialties

This document does not cover:

- Individual restaurants
- Food festivals
- Dining etiquette
- Cultural traditions
- Restaurant reviews
- Hotel menus

These topics are maintained by their respective documents.
