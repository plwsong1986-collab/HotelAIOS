# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for breakfast-related knowledge within the Dining Domain.

It defines factual information about breakfast dining, enabling AI assistants, guest services, concierge teams, and other downstream systems to answer guest questions consistently through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

# Overview

Breakfast is the first main meal of the day and is commonly served by hotels, restaurants, cafés, and other food establishments.

This document records stable, implementation-independent knowledge related to breakfast services and dining options. It does not include hotel-specific operational policies or promotional content.
