# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for tea-related knowledge within the Dining Domain.

It defines factual, implementation-independent information about tea beverages, tea service, and tea culture as it relates to guest dining. The document supports AI assistants, guest services, concierge teams, and other downstream systems through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Tea is a widely consumed beverage served in hotels, restaurants, cafés, tea houses, and other dining establishments around the world.

This document records stable knowledge about tea beverages and tea service. It does not include information about specific businesses, pricing, or promotional activities.
