# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for snack-related knowledge within the Dining Domain.

It defines factual, implementation-independent information about snacks, light meals, and small food items commonly available to hotel guests. The document supports AI assistants, guest services, concierge teams, and other downstream systems through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Snacks are light food items typically consumed between regular meals or as quick dining options.

This document records stable knowledge about snack categories, serving styles, and guest planning information. It does not include restaurant listings, pricing, or promotional content.

---

This document covers:

- Light meals

This document does not cover:
