# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for drink-related knowledge within the Dining Domain.

It defines factual, implementation-independent information about beverages other than coffee and tea, supporting AI assistants, guest services, concierge teams, and other downstream systems through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Drinks include a wide range of non-food beverages served in hotels, restaurants, cafés, bars, and other dining establishments.

This document records stable knowledge about beverage categories, service characteristics, and guest planning information. It does not include information about specific venues, brands, pricing, or promotions.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Soft drinks
- Fruit juices
- Mineral water
- Sparkling water
- Smoothies
- Milk-based beverages
- Alcoholic beverages
- Mocktails
- Cocktails
- Beverage service

This document does not cover:

- Coffee beverages
- Tea beverages
- Individual bars
- Restaurants
- Hotel pricing
- Promotional offers

These topics are maintained by their respective documents.
