# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for restaurant information relevant to hotel guests.

It records factual information about restaurants that guests may visit during their stay. The document supports AI assistants, websites, guest services, concierge operations, and other downstream systems by maintaining a single source of truth (SSOT) for restaurant knowledge.

---

# Overview

Restaurants are dining establishments that primarily provide prepared meals for on-site dining, takeaway, or other officially supported dining services.

This document records stable and verifiable information about restaurants without providing reviews, rankings, or personalized recommendations.

Only factual and officially verifiable information should be maintained.

---

# Scope

This document covers:

- Independent restaurants
- Chain restaurants
- Hotel-operated restaurants
- Specialty restaurants
- Buffet restaurants
- Family restaurants
- Seafood restaurants
- Steak houses
- International restaurants
- Other permanent restaurant establishments

This document does not cover:

- Cafés
- Bars
- Street food vendors
- Temporary food markets
- Food festivals
- Restaurant reservation policies
- Dining etiquette

These topics are maintained in their respective documents.
