This document is the authoritative source for tea culture knowledge within the Culture Domain.

It defines the scope, organization, and terminology of tea-related cultural traditions, heritage, and customs to support AI assistants, semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), ontology development, and visitor information through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Tea culture encompasses the historical, social, and cultural traditions associated with the cultivation, preparation, serving, and appreciation of tea within a destination.

Tea traditions often reflect regional identity, historical development, hospitality practices, and cultural heritage. This document provides descriptive cultural knowledge only and does not provide commercial recommendations, product reviews, or food and beverage operational guidance.
