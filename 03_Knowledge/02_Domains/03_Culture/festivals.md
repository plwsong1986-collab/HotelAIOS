# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for festival knowledge within the Culture Domain.

It defines the scope, organization, and terminology of traditional, cultural, and community festivals to support AI assistants, semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), ontology development, and visitor information through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Festivals are recurring cultural events that reflect the history, traditions, beliefs, and community identity of a destination.

Festival knowledge helps visitors understand the cultural significance of seasonal celebrations and traditional observances. This document provides descriptive cultural information only and does not publish event schedules, ticketing information, or operational guidance.
