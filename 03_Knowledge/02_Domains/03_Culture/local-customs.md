# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for local customs knowledge within the Culture Domain.

It defines the scope, organization, and terminology of local customs, etiquette, and traditional social practices to support AI assistants, semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), ontology development, and visitor information through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Local customs represent the traditional social behaviors, etiquette, practices, and community conventions that characterize daily life within a destination.

Understanding local customs helps visitors interact respectfully with local communities and better appreciate cultural traditions. This document provides descriptive cultural knowledge only and does not prescribe visitor behavior or replace official regulations.
