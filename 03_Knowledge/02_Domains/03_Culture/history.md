# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for historical knowledge within the Culture Domain.

It defines the scope, organization, and terminology of destination history to support AI assistants, semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), ontology development, and visitor information through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

# Overview

History provides cultural and historical context that helps explain the development, identity, and heritage of a destination.

Historical knowledge describes significant periods, events, influences, and cultural evolution that contribute to understanding a destination. It provides background information only and does not replace academic historical research or official historical records.
