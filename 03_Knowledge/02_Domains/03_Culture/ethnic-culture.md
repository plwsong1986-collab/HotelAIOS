# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for ethnic culture knowledge within the Culture Domain.

It defines the scope, organization, and terminology of ethnic diversity and cultural heritage to support AI assistants, semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), ontology development, and visitor information through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Ethnic culture describes the traditions, identities, heritage, and cultural characteristics associated with the ethnic communities of a destination.

The document provides structured cultural knowledge intended to help visitors understand cultural diversity and historical influences. It is descriptive rather than analytical and does not replace academic research, demographic studies, or government publications.
