# Purpose

This document is the authoritative source for local language knowledge within the Culture Domain.

It defines the scope, organization, and terminology of languages, dialects, linguistic heritage, and commonly used expressions to support AI assistants, semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), ontology development, and visitor information through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Local language reflects the linguistic identity, history, and cultural heritage of a destination. It includes official languages, regional dialects, commonly spoken languages, and culturally significant expressions that help explain how people communicate within the local community.

This document provides descriptive linguistic and cultural knowledge only. It does not function as a phrasebook, translation service, language-learning guide, or pronunciation manual.
