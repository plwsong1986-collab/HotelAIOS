# Purpose

This document defines the Culture Domain within the HotelAIOS Knowledge Module.

It establishes the scope, ownership, and organizational structure of culture-related knowledge that supports AI assistants, semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), ontology development, and guest information services through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

The Culture Domain provides structured knowledge describing the cultural characteristics of a destination.

It includes historical background, local customs, ethnic diversity, festivals, traditional arts, language, tea culture, and other cultural topics that help visitors better understand local identity and heritage.

This domain organizes descriptive cultural knowledge only. It does not provide travel itineraries, commercial recommendations, historical research, or operational procedures.

---

# Scope

This domain includes:

- Cultural knowledge
- Historical context
- Local customs and traditions
- Ethnic culture
- Festivals and celebrations
- Performing arts
- Traditional crafts
- Tea culture
- Local language
- Cultural architecture and knowledge organization

This domain does not include:

- Tourist attractions
- Transportation
- Dining
- Hotel operations
- Commercial recommendations
- Travel planning
- Government regulations
- Academic historical research

Authoritative ownership of these topics remains with their respective domains.

# Domain Relationships

The Culture Domain is closely related to:

- Attractions
- Dining
- Destination Policies

- Travel Guides
- Guest Reviews

The domain may reference information maintained by other domains but does not assume ownership of their authoritative knowledge.

---

# Design Principles

The Culture Domain follows these principles:

- Single Source of Truth (SSOT)
- Clear ownership boundaries
- Implementation independence
- AI-ready structure
- RAG-ready organization
- Ontology-ready terminology
- Semantic consistency
- Reusable knowledge components

Culture information should remain descriptive, factual, and implementation-independent.

---

# Single Source of Truth

This domain owns:

- Cultural concepts
- Cultural terminology
- Historical context summaries
- Customs and traditions knowledge
- Festival knowledge
- Cultural classification
- Language-related visitor knowledge
- Cultural organization

This domain does not own:

- Attraction information
- Transportation information
- Hotel services
- Commercial content
- Government regulations
- Operational procedures
- Marketing content

Authoritative ownership remains with the corresponding domains.
