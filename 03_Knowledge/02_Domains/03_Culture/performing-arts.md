This document is the authoritative source for performing arts knowledge within the Culture Domain.

It defines the scope, organization, and terminology of traditional and contemporary performing arts to support AI assistants, semantic search, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), ontology development, and visitor information through a Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

---

# Overview

Performing arts represent the music, dance, theatre, storytelling, and other live artistic expressions that form part of a destination's cultural heritage.

This document provides structured cultural knowledge that helps explain the role of performing arts in preserving traditions, expressing cultural identity, and supporting community heritage. It does not provide event schedules, venue information, ticketing, or commercial recommendations.
