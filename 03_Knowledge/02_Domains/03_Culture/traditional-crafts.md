# Overview

Traditional crafts represent skills, techniques, materials, and artistic practices that have been preserved and transmitted across generations as part of a destination's cultural heritage.

Traditional craftsmanship reflects regional identity, historical development, community traditions, and cultural continuity. This document provides descriptive cultural knowledge only and does not provide commercial recommendations, retail information, pricing, or artisan business guidance.
