# Overview

Dogs are among the most commonly accommodated companion animals in the hospitality industry.

Hotels that permit pets frequently include dogs within their accepted companion animal categories, subject to the property's official pet policies.

This document records stable hospitality knowledge regarding dogs without describing property-specific operational rules.
