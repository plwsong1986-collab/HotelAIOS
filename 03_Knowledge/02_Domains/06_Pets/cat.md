# Overview

Cats are among the companion animals commonly accommodated by pet-friendly hotels.

Hotels that accept pets may permit cats according to their official pet policies. Acceptance conditions, accommodation requirements, and operational practices vary between individual properties.

This document records stable hospitality knowledge regarding cats without describing hotel-specific operational rules.
