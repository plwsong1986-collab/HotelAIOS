# Purpose

This document defines the business continuity standards for HotelAIOS.

Business Continuity establishes the operational framework required to maintain critical business functions during service disruptions, infrastructure failures, cyber incidents, natural disasters, and other unexpected events while minimizing business impact and ensuring timely service recovery.

---

# Objectives

The Business Continuity should:

- Ensure continuous business operations
- Minimize operational disruption
- Protect critical business services
- Improve organizational resilience
- Support rapid service recovery
- Standardize continuity planning

# Continuity Strategy

The continuity strategy should define:

- Business continuity objectives
- Recovery priorities
- Operational resilience goals
- Critical business functions
- Recovery approaches
- Resource planning

The strategy should align with organizational risk management policies.

---

# Business Impact Analysis

Business impact analysis should identify:

- Critical business processes
- Service dependencies
- Operational risks
- Financial impact
- Customer impact
- Regulatory impact

Impact analysis should guide recovery priorities.

---

# Critical Services

Critical services should include:

- Reservation management
- OTA synchronization
- Payment processing
- Identity and access management
- AI platform services
- Administrative operations

Critical services should receive the highest recovery priority.

---

# Continuity Planning

Continuity planning should define:

- Emergency procedures
- Service continuity plans
- Operational responsibilities
- Resource allocation
- Alternative operating procedures
- Recovery coordination

Plans should be documented and regularly reviewed.

---

# Disaster Response

Disaster response should support:

- Incident activation
- Emergency communication
- Operational coordination
- Infrastructure recovery
- Service restoration
- Business stabilization

Response activities should follow predefined operational procedures.

---

# Recovery Coordination

Recovery coordination should include:

- Recovery leadership
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Progress tracking
- Resource management
- Stakeholder communication
- Operational validation

Recovery activities should remain coordinated until normal operations resume.

---

# Continuity Testing

Business continuity testing should include:

- Tabletop exercises
- Recovery simulations
- Disaster recovery drills
- Communication testing
- Service restoration validation
- Lessons learned

Testing should be scheduled regularly to verify operational readiness.

---

# Governance

Business continuity governance should define:

- Continuity ownership
- Recovery responsibilities
- Review procedures
- Compliance requirements
- Audit activities
- Continuous improvement

Governance should ensure continuity plans remain effective and current.

---

# Business Continuity Principles

Business Continuity should:

- Be resilient
- Be documented
- Be repeatable
- Be regularly tested
- Support continuous improvement
- Follow Documentation First principles
