# Operations

The Operations module defines how HotelAIOS operates in production.

It standardizes operational processes, monitoring, incident response, deployment, maintenance, business continuity, security operations, and service governance to ensure stable, reliable, and scalable hotel platform operations.

---

# Objectives

The Operations module provides:

- Operational architecture
- Service operations
- Monitoring standards
- Incident management
- Deployment management
- Backup and recovery
- Business continuity
- Capacity management
- Operational governance

# Scope

This module covers:

- Platform operations
- Infrastructure operations
- Application operations
- Operational procedures
- Production support
- Disaster recovery
- Operational governance

This module does not define business logic, software implementation, or development standards.

# Design Principles

Operations should:

- Be standardized
- Be automated
