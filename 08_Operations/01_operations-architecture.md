# Purpose

This document defines the operational architecture for HotelAIOS.

Operations Architecture establishes the organizational structure, operational domains, service responsibilities, operational workflows, and management principles required to ensure reliable, scalable, secure, and continuously available platform operations.

---

# Objectives

The Operations Architecture should:

- Define the operational architecture
- Standardize operational responsibilities
- Support reliable service delivery
- Improve operational efficiency
- Enable scalable platform management
- Establish operational governance

---

# Operational Domains

Operations should be organized into the following domains:

- Platform Operations
- Infrastructure Operations
- Application Operations
- Security Operations
- Data Operations
- Network Operations
- Business Operations

Each domain should have clearly defined responsibilities.

---

# Service Operations

Service operations should ensure:

- Service availability
- Service reliability
- Service continuity
- Operational stability
- Performance management
- Incident coordination

Operational procedures should be standardized across all services.

---

# Infrastructure Operations

Infrastructure operations should manage:

- Compute resources
- Storage resources
- Networking
- Virtualization
- Cloud infrastructure
- Platform dependencies

Infrastructure should support high availability and scalability.

---

# Platform Operations

Platform operations should support:

- System administration
- Configuration management
- Environment management
- Platform maintenance
- Operational automation
- Platform optimization

Platform operations should minimize manual intervention whenever possible.

---

# Operational Processes

Operational processes should include:

- Daily operations
- Maintenance procedures
- Change execution
- Incident handling
- Problem resolution
- Service review

Processes should be documented and continuously improved.

---

# Operational Monitoring

Operations should continuously monitor:

- Platform health
- Service availability
- Infrastructure status
- Resource utilization
- Operational risks
- Business impact

Monitoring should provide real-time operational visibility.

---

# Governance

Operational governance should define:

- Operational policies
- Roles and responsibilities
- Service ownership
- Operational standards
- Review procedures
- Continuous improvement practices

Governance should ensure consistent operational execution.

---

# Architecture Principles

Operations Architecture should:

- Be standardized
- Be resilient
- Be scalable
- Be observable
- Support automation
- Follow Documentation First principles
