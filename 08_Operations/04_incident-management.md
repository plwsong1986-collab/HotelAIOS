# Purpose

This document defines the incident management standards for HotelAIOS.

Incident Management establishes a standardized process for identifying, assessing, responding to, resolving, and reviewing operational incidents to minimize business impact and restore services as quickly as possible.

---

# Objectives

The Incident Management should:

- Standardize incident handling
- Minimize service disruption
- Accelerate incident resolution
- Improve operational coordination
- Reduce business impact
- Enable continuous operational improvement

# Incident Classification

Incidents should be classified according to:

- Severity
- Business impact
- Service affected
- Operational priority
- Customer impact
- Security relevance

Classification should determine response priorities and escalation procedures.

---

# Incident Lifecycle

Every incident should follow a standardized lifecycle:

- Detection
- Identification
- Assessment
- Assignment
- Response
- Resolution
- Recovery
- Closure
- Review

Each stage should be documented and traceable.

---

# Detection

Incident detection may originate from:

- Monitoring systems
- Automated alerts
- User reports
- Operational teams
- Security monitoring
- External service providers

Detection should occur as early as possible.

---

# Response

Incident response should include:

- Initial assessment
- Incident ownership
- Impact analysis
- Containment actions
- Escalation when required
- Stakeholder notification

Response activities should prioritize service restoration.

---

# Resolution

Incident resolution should:

- Restore affected services
- Validate service health
- Confirm operational stability
- Record corrective actions
- Update operational documentation

Resolution should eliminate immediate operational impact.

---

# Communication

Incident communication should support:

- Internal notifications
- Management updates
- Customer communications
- Status reporting
- Resolution announcements
- Operational documentation

Communication should remain timely, accurate, and consistent.

---

# Post-Incident Review

Every significant incident should include:

- Root cause analysis
- Timeline review
- Impact assessment
- Lessons learned
- Preventive actions
- Process improvements

Reviews should drive continuous operational improvement.

---

# Governance

Incident governance should define:

- Incident ownership
- Escalation policies
- Severity definitions
- Communication standards
- Review procedures
- Continuous improvement practices

Governance should ensure consistent incident management across the platform.

---

# Incident Management Principles

Incident Management should:

- Be standardized
- Be measurable
- Be transparent
- Be traceable
- Support automation
- Follow Documentation First principles
