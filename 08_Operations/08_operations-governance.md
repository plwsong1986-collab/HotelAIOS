# Purpose

This document defines the operational governance framework for HotelAIOS.

Operations Governance establishes the policies, organizational responsibilities, operational standards, review mechanisms, compliance requirements, and continuous improvement processes required to ensure consistent, secure, efficient, and accountable platform operations.

---

# Objectives

The Operations Governance should:

- Establish operational governance standards
- Define operational roles and responsibilities
- Standardize operational policies
- Ensure operational compliance
- Improve operational quality
- Enable continuous operational improvement

# Governance Framework

The governance framework should define:

- Governance objectives
- Governance principles
- Decision authority
- Operational ownership
- Accountability model
- Review mechanisms

Governance should align operational execution with organizational objectives.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

Operational governance should clearly define responsibilities for:

- Platform Engineering
- Infrastructure Operations
- Security Operations
- Application Operations
- Business Operations
- Executive Management

Every operational activity should have a clearly assigned owner.

---

# Operational Policies

Operational policies should define:

- Change management
- Deployment management
- Incident management
- Backup policies
- Recovery policies
- Service maintenance

Policies should be documented, approved, and regularly reviewed.

---

# Operational Standards

Operational standards should establish:

- Documentation standards
- Monitoring standards
- Service availability requirements
- Performance expectations
- Security requirements
- Operational procedures

Standards should be consistently applied across the platform.

---

# Compliance Management

Operational compliance should support:

- Internal policies
- Regulatory requirements
- Security standards
- Audit readiness
- Risk management
- Evidence collection

Compliance activities should be continuously monitored.

---

# Operational Reviews

Operational reviews should include:

- Service performance reviews
- Incident reviews
- Capacity reviews
- Risk assessments
- Operational audits
- Governance evaluations

Review outcomes should generate measurable improvement actions.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement should include:

- Process optimization
- Operational automation
- Documentation updates
- Knowledge sharing
- Lessons learned
- Performance optimization

Improvement initiatives should be prioritized based on operational value and business impact.

---

# Governance Metrics

Operational governance should measure:

- Service availability
- Incident frequency
- Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
- Mean Time to Recover (MTTR)
- Deployment success rate
- Change success rate
- SLA compliance
- Operational efficiency

Metrics should support evidence-based operational decisions.

---

# Governance Principles

Operations Governance should:

- Be standardized
- Be transparent
- Be measurable
- Be accountable
- Support continuous improvement
- Follow Documentation First principles
