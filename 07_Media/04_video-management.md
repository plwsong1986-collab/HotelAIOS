# Purpose

This document defines the video management standards for HotelAIOS.

Video management ensures promotional videos, property showcases, brand content, educational materials, and operational videos remain centrally managed, consistently maintained, optimized for delivery, and governed throughout their lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

The Video Management should:

- Standardize video management
- Maintain video quality
- Support scalable delivery
- Improve operational efficiency
- Enable centralized governance
- Ensure consistent user experience

# Video Categories

Videos should be organized into categories such as:

- Property overview
- Room showcase
- Facility presentation
- Destination experience
- Brand promotion
- Marketing campaign
- Educational content
- Operational training

Categories should remain standardized across all platforms.

---

# Video Standards

Videos should define:

- Resolution
- Aspect ratio
- Duration guidelines
- File format
- Naming convention
- Copyright information
- Publication status

Only approved videos should be published.

---

# Video Metadata

Each video should maintain metadata including:

- Video identifier
- Title
- Description
- Category
- Language
- Duration
- Thumbnail
- Version
- Ownership
- Usage permissions

Metadata should support efficient discovery and management.

---

# Video Processing

Video processing should support:

- Format conversion
- Resolution generation
- Thumbnail creation
- Subtitle integration
- Metadata preservation
- Quality validation

Processing should maintain approved production quality.

---

# Video Optimization

Video optimization should:

- Reduce file size
- Improve streaming performance
- Generate adaptive formats
- Support multiple resolutions
- Preserve viewing quality

Optimization should improve delivery efficiency without compromising approved standards.

---

# Video Distribution

Videos may be distributed to:

- Website
- OTA platforms
- Mobile applications
- AI services
- Marketing campaigns
- Internal operations

Only approved video versions should be distributed.

---

# Monitoring

Video monitoring should:

- Track video availability
- Monitor playback performance
- Detect delivery failures
- Measure usage statistics
- Support operational reviews

Monitoring should ensure reliable video delivery.

---

# Governance

Video governance should define:

- Video ownership
- Review procedures
- Approval workflow
- Publication standards
- Archive policy
- Retirement process

Governance should maintain long-term quality and consistency.

---

# Management Principles

Video management should:

- Be centralized
- Be scalable
- Be reusable
- Be traceable
- Support automation
- Follow Documentation First principles
