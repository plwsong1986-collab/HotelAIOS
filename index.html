Welcome to Sanlitian

A peaceful stay near the heart of Lijiang

Sanlitian Oxygen Boutique Guesthouse is located near Lijiang Ancient Town, within convenient walking distance of local streets, cultural attractions, markets, restaurants, and traditional architecture.

Our guesthouse combines comfortable accommodation, thoughtful service, oxygen support, local culture, and a traditional Chinese courtyard environment.

We also invite guests to discover Yunnan through local tea and coffee experiences, creating opportunities to slow down, learn, and connect with the region's everyday culture.
