# Purpose

This document defines the Business Intelligence (BI) framework for HotelAIOS.

Business Intelligence establishes the architecture, analytical capabilities, reporting standards, data exploration methods, decision support mechanisms, governance, and operational practices required to transform platform data into actionable business insights across the HotelAIOS ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Business Intelligence framework should:

- Standardize business intelligence capabilities
- Support data-driven decision making
- Improve operational visibility
- Enable self-service analytics
- Ensure analytical consistency
- Support continuous business improvement

# BI Principles

Business Intelligence should follow these principles:

- Business Driven
- Single Source of Truth (SSOT)
- Data Quality First
- Standardized Metrics
- Secure Access
- Scalable Architecture
- Documentation First
- Continuous Improvement

Business Intelligence should provide trusted and consistent insights across the organization.

---

# Data Sources

Business Intelligence may integrate data from:

- Core platform services
- Reservation systems
- OTA integrations
- Customer interactions
- Financial systems
- Operational systems
- AI services
- External business systems

All data sources should be documented, validated, and governed.

---

# Data Modeling

Business Intelligence should define:

- Business entities
- Dimensions
- Facts
- Relationships
- Aggregation rules
- Semantic models

Data models should provide consistent analytical interpretations.

---

# Analytical Capabilities

Business Intelligence should support:

- Historical analysis
- Trend analysis
- Comparative analysis
- Performance analysis
- Forecast support
- Exception analysis

Analytical capabilities should support operational, tactical, and strategic decision making.

---

# Reporting Services

Business Intelligence reporting should include:

- Executive reports
- Operational reports
- Scheduled reports
- Interactive dashboards
- Export capabilities
- Ad hoc reporting

Reporting services should provide timely and reliable business information.

---

# Self-Service Analytics

Self-service analytics should support:

- Dataset discovery
- Data filtering
- Interactive exploration
- Standard metrics
- Visualization tools
- Controlled data access

Self-service capabilities should empower business users while maintaining governance.

# Data Governance

Business Intelligence governance should define:

- Data ownership
- Metric ownership
- Access policies
- Quality standards
- Documentation requirements
- Compliance controls

Governance should ensure reliable analytical outcomes.

# Monitoring and Quality

Business Intelligence monitoring should include:

- Data freshness
- Data quality
- Processing status
- Report availability
- Query performance
- User adoption metrics

Monitoring should support reliable BI operations.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Business Intelligence should continuously improve through:

- User feedback
- KPI reviews
- Performance optimization
- Data quality improvements
- Technology evaluation
- Process refinement

Improvement activities should enhance analytical value over time.

# Business Intelligence Principles

Business Intelligence should:

- Be business focused
- Be consistent
- Be scalable
