# Purpose

This document defines the analytics governance framework for HotelAIOS.

Analytics Governance establishes the organizational structure, ownership model, policies, standards, review processes, data governance, security controls, operational responsibilities, and continuous improvement mechanisms required to ensure that analytics capabilities remain accurate, secure, scalable, and consistent across the HotelAIOS platform.

---

# Objectives

The Analytics Governance should:

- Standardize analytics governance
- Ensure data consistency
- Improve analytics quality
- Strengthen security and compliance
- Support scalable analytics operations
- Enable continuous improvement

# Governance Principles

Analytics governance should follow these principles:

- Single Source of Truth (SSOT)
- Documentation First
- Business Driven
- Data Quality First
- Security by Design
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Continuous Improvement

Governance should ensure trusted analytics throughout the platform.

---

# Organizational Responsibilities

Analytics governance should define responsibilities for:

- Technical Leadership
- Platform Engineering
- Data Team
- Analytics Team
- Product Management
- Operations Team
- Security Team

Every analytics asset should have clearly assigned ownership.

---

# Data Governance

Analytics data governance should define:

- Data ownership
- Data quality standards
- Metadata management
- Data lineage
- Data retention
- Data access policies
- Data validation

Data governance should ensure consistent and reliable analytical information.

---

# KPI Governance

KPI governance should include:

- KPI ownership
- KPI approval
- Definition management
- Calculation standards
- Target management
- Periodic review

KPI governance should maintain consistent business measurements.

---

# Reporting Governance

Reporting governance should define:

- Report ownership
- Dashboard ownership
- Publication standards
- Review procedures
- Version management
- Documentation maintenance

Reporting governance should ensure consistent analytical communication.

---

# Security and Compliance

Analytics governance should enforce:

- Authentication
- Authorization
- Role-based access control
- Audit logging
- Privacy protection
- Regulatory compliance
- Data encryption

Security controls should protect analytical assets throughout their lifecycle.

---

# Quality Management

Analytics quality management should include:

- Data quality monitoring
- KPI validation
- Report verification
- Dashboard review
- Visualization consistency
- Periodic quality audits

Quality management should continuously improve analytical reliability.

---

# Lifecycle Management

Analytics lifecycle management should include:

- Planning
- Design
- Development
- Validation
- Deployment
- Maintenance
- Version management
- Retirement

Lifecycle processes should ensure sustainable analytics operations.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement should include:

- Governance reviews
- KPI optimization
- Reporting enhancements
- User feedback
- Technology evaluation
- Process improvement

Improvement activities should strengthen long-term analytical capabilities.

---

# Analytics Governance Principles

Analytics Governance should:

- Be standardized
- Be measurable
- Be secure
- Be scalable
- Be maintainable
- Follow Documentation First principles
