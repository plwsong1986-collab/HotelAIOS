# Purpose

This document defines the KPI framework for HotelAIOS.

The KPI Framework establishes standardized business metrics, measurement principles, calculation methodologies, ownership, reporting standards, governance, and continuous improvement mechanisms required to measure business performance consistently across the HotelAIOS platform.

---

# Objectives

The KPI Framework should:

- Standardize KPI definitions
- Improve business visibility
- Support data-driven decision making
- Ensure measurement consistency
- Enable operational optimization
- Support continuous improvement

# KPI Principles

KPI management should follow these principles:

- Business Driven
- Measurable
- Consistent
- Accurate
- Actionable
- Transparent
- Documentation First
- Continuous Improvement

KPIs should measure meaningful business outcomes rather than system activity alone.

---

# KPI Categories

The platform should support KPIs across multiple categories, including:

- Business performance
- Revenue performance
- Occupancy performance
- Customer experience
- Operational efficiency
- Marketing performance
- AI effectiveness
- Platform reliability
- Security operations
- Financial performance

Each KPI category should align with defined business objectives.

---

# KPI Definitions

Each KPI should define:

- KPI name
- Business purpose
- Business owner
- Metric definition
- Formula
- Unit of measurement
- Target value
- Threshold values
- Reporting frequency

Definitions should remain consistent across all reports and dashboards.

---

# Calculation Standards

KPI calculations should define:

- Calculation formula
- Source systems
- Data validation
- Aggregation rules
- Time periods
- Historical comparison
- Exception handling

Calculation methods should remain stable and reproducible.

---

# Data Sources

KPI data may originate from:

- Core platform services
- Reservation systems
- OTA integrations
- AI services
- CRM systems
- Financial systems
- Operational systems
- Analytics platform

All KPI data sources should be documented and governed.

---

# Reporting Standards

KPI reporting should include:

- Standard dashboards
- Scheduled reports
- Executive summaries
- Operational reports
- Trend analysis
- Historical comparisons

Reporting should provide consistent visibility into business performance.

---

# KPI Ownership

Each KPI should define ownership for:

- Business accountability
- Technical implementation
- Data quality
- Reporting accuracy
- Review responsibility
- Continuous improvement

Every KPI should have clearly assigned ownership.

---

# KPI Review

KPI reviews should include:

- Target evaluation
- Trend analysis
- Data quality assessment
- Business relevance
- Calculation validation
- Improvement planning

KPIs should be reviewed regularly to ensure continued business value.

---

# Governance

KPI governance should define:

- KPI standards
- Approval process
- Version management
- Documentation maintenance
- Audit requirements
- Continuous improvement

Governance should ensure consistent KPI management across the platform.

---

# KPI Framework Principles

The KPI Framework should:

- Be measurable
- Be consistent
- Be transparent
- Be scalable
- Be maintainable
- Follow Documentation First principles
