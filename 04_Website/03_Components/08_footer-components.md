# Purpose

This document defines reusable footer components used throughout the website.

Footer components provide standardized access to supporting information, legal resources, secondary navigation, and business details while maintaining consistency across all website pages.

---

# Objectives

The Footer Components should:

- Standardize footer layouts
- Improve access to supporting information
- Support secondary navigation
- Ensure accessibility
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Component Structure

```text
Footer Components

├── Footer Navigation
│
├── Contact Information
│
├── Business Information
│
├── Social Links
│
├── Newsletter Signup
│
├── Legal Links
│
├── Copyright Notice
│
└── Language & Region Selector
```

---

# Component Definitions

## Footer Navigation

Footer navigation should:

- Provide links to important pages
- Organize links into logical groups
- Remain consistent across the website

---

## Contact Information

Contact information should:

- Display primary communication channels
- Present business contact details clearly
- Remain accurate and up to date

---

## Business Information

Business information should:

- Display hotel identity
- Present address where applicable
- Include essential operational information

---

## Social Links

Social links should:

- Provide access to official social platforms
- Display approved platform icons
- Open external destinations clearly

---

## Newsletter Signup

Newsletter signup should:

- Allow users to subscribe to updates
- Collect only required information
- Confirm successful subscription

---

## Legal Links

Legal links should provide access to:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Cookie Policy
- Additional legal documentation where applicable

---

## Copyright Notice

The copyright notice should:

- Display ownership information
- Reflect the current publication year
- Follow organizational branding standards

---

## Language & Region Selector

The language and region selector should:

- Allow supported language selection
- Support regional variations where applicable
- Maintain consistent behavior throughout the website

---

# Accessibility Requirements

Footer components should:

- Support keyboard navigation
- Use semantic footer elements
- Provide descriptive link labels
- Maintain sufficient color contrast
- Support screen readers
