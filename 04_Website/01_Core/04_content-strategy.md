# Purpose

This document defines the content strategy for the HotelAIOS website.

It establishes principles, governance, ownership boundaries, and organizational standards that ensure all website content is consistent, maintainable, user-centered, and aligned with the HotelAIOS Knowledge Module.

This document is the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for website content strategy.

---

# Overview

The Website Content Strategy defines how content should be planned, organized, presented, and maintained.

The website presents knowledge owned by the Knowledge Module but does not duplicate ownership.

Content should always be accurate, reusable, scalable, and easy to maintain.

---

# Scope

This document includes:

- Content organization
- Content hierarchy
- Content ownership
- Content governance
- Content lifecycle
- Content consistency
- Editorial principles

This document does not include:

- Business knowledge
- Individual page content
- UI implementation
- SEO implementation
- Frontend implementation

These remain owned by their corresponding modules.

---

# Content Principles

Website content should be:

- Accurate
- Consistent
- User-centered
- Accessible
- Scannable
- Reusable
- Searchable
- AI Ready
- SEO Friendly
- Easy to maintain

Knowledge should never be duplicated unnecessarily.

---

# Content Hierarchy

Content should follow a consistent hierarchy:

- Page
- Section
- Block
- Component
- Element

Each level should have a clearly defined responsibility.

---

# Content Ownership

Business knowledge is owned by the Knowledge Module.

The Website owns:

- Presentation
- Content layout
- Information organization
- User-facing structure

Website content should reference authoritative knowledge sources whenever appropriate.

---

# Editorial Standards

Content should:

- Use consistent terminology
- Follow brand guidelines
- Maintain a clear tone of voice
- Avoid duplicated information
- Support multilingual localization
- Follow accessibility guidelines

Editorial consistency should be maintained across the entire website.

---

# Content Lifecycle

Content typically progresses through:

- Draft
- Review
- Approved
- Published
- Maintained
- Archived

Lifecycle management should support long-term governance.

---

# Relationship with Knowledge

The Website consumes knowledge from the Knowledge Module.

Knowledge remains the Single Source of Truth for business information.

The Website should present knowledge without redefining or duplicating ownership.

---

# Relationship with SEO

SEO optimizes discoverability.

Content Strategy governs the structure and quality of content.

SEO should complement—not replace—content strategy.
