# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Cancellation Policy page on the website.

The Cancellation Policy page explains how guests may cancel or modify reservations, outlines applicable conditions, and helps guests understand potential fees and refund eligibility before making changes to their booking.

---

# Objectives

The Cancellation Policy page should:

- Explain cancellation policies clearly
- Present modification rules
- Describe refund eligibility
- Build guest confidence
- Maintain a consistent booking experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Cancellation Policy

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Cancellation Rules
│
├── Modification Rules
│
├── Refund Information
│
├── Contact Information
│
└── Related Booking Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the cancellation policy
- Encourage guests to review reservation conditions
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Cancellation Rules

Explain cancellation information including:

- Eligible reservations
- Cancellation deadlines
- Non-refundable reservations
- Cancellation process
- Reservation status updates

Only officially supported cancellation rules should be presented.

---

## Modification Rules

Describe reservation modification policies including:

- Date changes
- Room changes
- Guest information updates
- Rate plan changes
- Availability limitations

Modification options should match the booking system capabilities.

---

## Refund Information

Provide general information about refunds, including:

- Refund eligibility
- Processing timeline
- Payment method considerations
- Non-refundable conditions
- Contact for refund inquiries

Specific refund processing belongs to the booking system and financial operations.

---

## Contact Information

Provide guests with methods to obtain assistance, including:

- Hotel contact information
- Reservation support
- Customer service
- Frequently asked questions

Support information should remain consistent across the website.

---

## Related Booking Pages

Provide links to:

- Manage Booking
- Booking Confirmation
- Booking FAQ
- Contact

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the booking experience.
