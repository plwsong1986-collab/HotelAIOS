# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Guest Information page on the website.

The Guest Information page allows guests to provide the personal and reservation details required to complete a booking while ensuring a secure, simple, and trustworthy reservation experience.

---

# Objectives

The Guest Information page should:

- Collect required guest information
- Explain why information is requested
- Minimize unnecessary data entry
- Build guest confidence
- Maintain a consistent booking workflow

---

```text
Guest Information
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of guest information collection
- Reassure guests about data privacy
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Reservation Summary

Display a summary including:

- Selected room
- Selected rate plan
- Stay dates
- Number of guests
- Estimated total price (when available)

Reservation details should be provided by the booking system.

---

## Guest Information Form

The form may include fields such as:

- Guest name
- Email address
- Telephone number
- Country or region
- Estimated arrival time
- Additional guest information (when required)

Only officially required information should be collected.

---

## Special Requests

Guests may submit optional requests such as:

- Early check-in request
- Late check-out request
- Bed preference
- Accessibility request
- Celebration request
- Other stay preferences

Submission of a request does not guarantee availability unless confirmed.

---

## Privacy Notice

Provide a brief explanation covering:

- Information collection
- Data usage
- Privacy protection
- Terms acknowledgement
- Privacy Policy link

Privacy information should remain consistent with official policies.

---

## Related Booking Pages

Provide links to:

- Rate Plans
- Payment
- Booking Confirmation
- Booking FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the booking journey.

# User Experience Rules

The Guest Information page should:

- Keep forms simple and easy to complete
- Clearly distinguish required and optional fields
- Minimize typing where possible
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Prepare guests for payment

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel booking keywords
- Guest reservation keywords
- Hotel reservation process keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
