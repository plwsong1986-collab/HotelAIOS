# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Manage Booking page on the website.

The Manage Booking page enables guests to view, update, or cancel an existing reservation through a secure and straightforward self-service experience while following the hotel's reservation policies.

---

# Objectives

The Manage Booking page should:

- Provide reservation management
- Support booking modifications
- Support reservation cancellation
- Present reservation status clearly
- Maintain a consistent booking experience

---

```text
Manage Booking

```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of reservation management
- Introduce available self-service options
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Reservation Lookup

Guests may locate a reservation using:

- Reservation number
- Guest name
- Email address
- Other supported verification information

Lookup methods should match the booking system.

---

## Reservation Details

Display reservation information such as:

- Reservation status
- Guest information
- Stay dates
- Selected room
- Selected rate plan
- Payment status

Reservation data should be supplied by the booking system.

---

## Available Actions

Depending on reservation status and hotel policies, guests may:

- View reservation
- Update guest information
- Modify stay dates
- Change room selection (if supported)
- Cancel reservation
- Download confirmation
- Contact the hotel

Only officially supported actions should be displayed.

---

## Policy Information

Explain relevant policies including:

- Modification policy
- Cancellation policy
- Refund policy (when applicable)
- Reservation conditions
- Contact information

Policies should remain consistent with official hotel documentation.

---

## Related Booking Pages

Provide links to:

- Booking Confirmation
- Booking FAQ
- Contact
- Guest Services

Navigation should remain consistent throughout reservation management.
