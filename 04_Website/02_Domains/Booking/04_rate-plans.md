# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Rate Plans page on the website.

The Rate Plans page helps guests understand the available booking options for a selected room, compare included benefits, and choose the reservation that best matches their travel needs.

---

# Objectives

The Rate Plans page should:

- Present available rate plans clearly
- Help guests compare booking options
- Explain included benefits
- Support informed booking decisions
- Maintain a consistent booking experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Rate Plans

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Available Rate Plans
│
├── Rate Comparison
│
├── Selected Rate Summary
│
└── Related Booking Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of rate plans
- Help guests understand available booking options
- Reflect the hotel's booking experience

---

## Available Rate Plans

Each rate plan may include:

- Rate plan name
- Short description
- Included benefits
- Meal inclusion
- Cancellation policy summary
- Payment option
- Starting price (when available)

Rate information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Rate Comparison

Guests should be able to compare rate plans using information such as:

- Included services
- Breakfast inclusion
- Cancellation flexibility
- Payment requirements
- Special offers
- Member benefits (when applicable)

Comparison information should remain consistent across all rate plans.

---

## Selected Rate Summary

After a rate plan is selected, provide a summary including:

- Selected room
- Selected rate plan
- Stay dates
- Guest information
- Link to Guest Information

Pricing and availability should be supplied by the booking system.

---

## Related Booking Pages

Provide links to:

- Booking Overview
- Room Selection
- Guest Information
- Booking FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the booking journey.
