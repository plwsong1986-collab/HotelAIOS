# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Booking FAQ page on the website.

The Booking FAQ page provides answers to common reservation questions, helping guests quickly understand the booking process, reservation policies, payment options, and post-booking services before contacting the hotel.

---

# Objectives

The Booking FAQ page should:

- Answer common booking questions
- Reduce guest uncertainty
- Improve self-service support
- Reduce customer service requests
- Maintain a consistent booking experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Booking FAQ

├── Reservation Questions
│
├── Payment Questions
│
├── Modification & Cancellation
│
├── Arrival Questions

│

```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the FAQ
- Help guests quickly locate answers
- Reflect the hotel's service standards

---

## Reservation Questions

Provide answers to common topics such as:

- How to make a reservation
- How availability works
- Reservation confirmation
- Booking reference
- Group reservations

Answers should remain consistent with official booking policies.

---

## Payment Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Accepted payment methods
- Payment timing
- Deposits
- Taxes and fees
- Payment security

Payment information should remain consistent with the booking system.

---

## Modification & Cancellation

Provide answers covering:

- How to modify a reservation
- How to cancel a reservation
- Cancellation deadlines
- Refund eligibility
- Reservation policies

Detailed policies should reference the official cancellation documentation.

---

## Arrival Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Check-in requirements
- Arrival time
- Late arrival
- Early check-in requests
- Special requests

Operational procedures should remain consistent with hotel policies.

---

## Contact Support

Provide support options including:

- Reservation assistance
- Hotel contact information
- Customer service
- Additional help resources

Support information should remain consistent across the website.

---

## Related Booking Pages

Provide links to:

- Booking Overview
- Manage Booking
- Cancellation Policy
- Contact
- Guest Services

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the booking experience.
