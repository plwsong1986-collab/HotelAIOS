# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Payment page on the website.

The Payment page enables guests to securely complete their reservation by reviewing booking details, selecting an available payment method, and confirming payment before receiving their booking confirmation.

---

# Objectives

The Payment page should:

- Present payment information clearly
- Support secure payment completion
- Explain available payment methods
- Build guest confidence
- Maintain a consistent booking workflow

---

```text
Payment

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Reservation Summary

├── Payment Methods
│

│
├── Payment Confirmation
│

```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the payment process
- Reassure guests about payment security
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Reservation Summary

Display a summary including:

- Selected room
- Selected rate plan
- Stay dates
- Guest information
- Total amount
- Taxes and fees (when applicable)

Reservation details should be supplied by the booking system.

---

## Payment Methods

Present officially supported payment methods, such as:

- Credit card
- Debit card
- Digital wallet
- Bank transfer (if supported)
- Pay at property (if supported)

Only supported payment methods should be displayed.

---

## Payment Information

Explain information related to payment, including:

- Payment authorization
- Billing information
- Currency
- Secure payment processing
- Payment terms

Sensitive payment processing belongs to the booking system.

---

## Payment Confirmation

Before completing payment, provide confirmation of:

- Reservation details
- Total amount
- Selected payment method
- Agreement to booking terms
- Agreement to privacy policy

Guests should confirm details before continuing.

---

## Related Booking Pages

Provide links to:

- Guest Information
- Booking Confirmation
- Booking FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the booking journey.
