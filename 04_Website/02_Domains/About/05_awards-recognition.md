# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Awards & Recognition page on the website.

The Awards & Recognition page showcases the hotel's achievements, certifications, industry recognition, and notable accomplishments, helping visitors understand the organization's reputation and commitment to excellence.

---

# Objectives

The Awards & Recognition page should:

- Present verified awards
- Highlight industry recognition
- Build organizational credibility
- Reinforce brand reputation
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Awards & Recognition

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Industry Awards
│
├── Certifications
│
├── Professional Recognition
│
├── Achievement Timeline
│
└── Related About Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the page
- Highlight the organization's pursuit of excellence
- Reflect the hotel's brand identity

---

## Industry Awards

Present officially recognized awards, including:

- Hospitality awards
- Service excellence awards
- Tourism awards
- Regional recognition
- International recognition

Only verified awards should be presented.

---

## Certifications

Introduce applicable certifications, including:

- Quality certifications
- Sustainability certifications
- Safety certifications
- Accessibility certifications
- Other officially recognized credentials

Certification details should remain accurate and up to date.

---

## Professional Recognition

Summarize recognition received from:

- Industry organizations
- Hospitality associations
- Tourism authorities
- Professional institutions
- Independent rating organizations

Only official recognition should be included.

---

## Achievement Timeline

Present significant achievements in chronological order, including:

- Major awards
- Certification milestones
- Industry recognition
- Organizational accomplishments

Timeline entries should remain factual and verifiable.

---

## Related About Pages

Provide links to:

- About Overview
- Hotel Story
- Brand & Values
- Sustainability
- Careers
- Press & Media
- About FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the About domain.
