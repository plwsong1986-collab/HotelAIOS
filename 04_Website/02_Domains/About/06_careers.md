# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Careers page on the website.

The Careers page introduces employment opportunities, workplace culture, employee development, and recruitment information, helping prospective candidates understand the organization and available career opportunities.

---

# Objectives

The Careers page should:

- Introduce career opportunities
- Present workplace culture
- Support recruitment efforts
- Strengthen employer branding
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Careers

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Why Join Us
│
├── Workplace Culture
│
├── Career Opportunities
│
├── Recruitment Process
│
├── Employee Development
│
└── Related About Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Welcome prospective candidates
- Introduce the organization's employment philosophy
- Reflect the hotel's employer brand

---

## Why Join Us

Present key reasons to work with the organization, including:

- Professional growth
- Team environment
- Guest-focused culture
- Career development
- Long-term opportunities

Only officially approved employer branding content should be presented.

---

## Workplace Culture

Provide an overview of:

- Organizational culture
- Team collaboration
- Diversity and inclusion
- Professional ethics
- Employee wellbeing

Content should align with official corporate policies.

---

## Career Opportunities

Explain available employment opportunities, including:

- Operational positions
- Management positions
- Internship programs
- Graduate opportunities
- Seasonal positions (if applicable)

Job listings should remain current and be maintained through official recruitment channels.

---

## Recruitment Process

Describe the general recruitment process, including:

- Application
- Resume review
- Interviews
- Selection
- Onboarding

Detailed recruitment procedures should remain within official HR documentation.

---

## Employee Development

Introduce available development opportunities, including:

- Training programs
- Career progression
- Leadership development
- Continuous learning
- Performance support

Development information should remain consistent with HR policies.

---

## Related About Pages

Provide links to:

- About Overview
- Hotel Story
- Brand & Values
- Sustainability
- Awards & Recognition
- Press & Media
- About FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the About domain.

# User Experience Rules

The Careers page should:

- Present opportunities clearly
- Use inclusive and professional language
- Make navigation straightforward
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Guide candidates to official application channels

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel careers keywords
- Hospitality jobs keywords
- Hotel employment keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

---

# Maintenance

Review this page when:

- Recruitment policies change
- Career opportunities change
- Employer branding changes
- HR processes change
- Website structure changes
