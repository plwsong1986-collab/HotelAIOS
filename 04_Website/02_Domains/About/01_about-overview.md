# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the About Overview page on the website.

The About Overview page introduces the hotel organization, its identity, mission, and corporate background while serving as the entry point to all About-related content.

---

# Objectives

The About Overview page should:

- Introduce the hotel organization
- Present the brand identity
- Build credibility and trust
- Guide visitors to corporate information
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
About

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Hotel Overview
│
├── Mission & Vision
│
├── Core Values
│
├── Corporate Highlights
│
└── Related About Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel organization
- Introduce the About section
- Reflect the hotel's brand identity

---

## Hotel Overview

Provide a high-level overview including:

- Hotel profile
- Brand positioning
- Hospitality philosophy
- Guest commitment

Only officially approved corporate information should be presented.

---

## Mission & Vision

Introduce:

- Mission statement
- Vision statement
- Long-term direction
- Commitment to guests

Statements should remain consistent with official brand documentation.

---

## Core Values

Summarize the organization's core values, including topics such as:

- Guest experience
- Service excellence
- Integrity
- Innovation
- Sustainability

Detailed value descriptions should be provided on dedicated pages.

---

## Corporate Highlights

Present key organizational highlights, such as:

- Years of operation
- Major milestones
- Brand achievements
- Industry recognition

Only verified corporate information should be presented.

---

## Related About Pages

Provide links to:

- Hotel Story
- Brand & Values
- Sustainability
- Awards & Recognition
- Careers
- Press & Media
- About FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the About domain.

The About Overview page should:

- Present information clearly
- Use concise language
- Build visitor confidence
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Guide visitors to detailed About pages

The page should support:

- Hotel about keywords

- Hospitality brand keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
