# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the About FAQ page on the website.

The About FAQ page provides answers to common questions about the hotel organization, brand, sustainability initiatives, careers, media relations, and corporate information, helping visitors quickly find official information.

---

# Objectives

The About FAQ page should:

- Answer common corporate questions
- Improve visitor self-service
- Reduce repetitive inquiries
- Reinforce official messaging
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
About FAQ

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Company Questions
│
├── Brand Questions
│
├── Sustainability Questions
│
├── Careers Questions
│
├── Media Questions
│
├── Additional Support
│
└── Related About Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the FAQ
- Help visitors locate answers quickly
- Reflect the hotel's professional communication standards

---

## Company Questions

Provide answers covering topics such as:

- Hotel background
- Company history
- Mission
- Vision
- Organizational information

Answers should remain consistent with official corporate information.

---

## Brand Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Brand identity
- Core values
- Hospitality philosophy
- Guest commitment
- Brand positioning

Brand information should remain consistent with approved messaging.

---

## Sustainability Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Environmental initiatives
- Social responsibility
- Sustainability commitments
- Certifications
- Responsible operations

Only verified sustainability information should be presented.

---

## Careers Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Employment opportunities
- Recruitment process
- Workplace culture
- Employee development
- Application guidance

Recruitment information should remain consistent with HR policies.

---

## Media Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Press inquiries
- Media resources
- Brand assets
- Press releases
- Corporate communications

Media information should direct visitors to official communication channels.

---

## Additional Support

Provide support resources including:

- Corporate Contact
- Media Contact
- Human Resources
- General Enquiries

Support options should direct visitors to official communication channels.

---

## Related About Pages

Provide links to:

- About Overview
- Hotel Story
- Brand & Values
- Sustainability
- Awards & Recognition
- Careers
- Press & Media

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the About domain.

The About FAQ page should:

- Organize questions by topic
- Use concise answers
- Keep information easy to scan
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Guide visitors to related resources when appropriate
