# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Hotel Story page on the website.

The Hotel Story page introduces the hotel's history, heritage, milestones, and evolution, helping visitors understand the organization's background and long-term commitment to hospitality.

---

# Objectives

The Hotel Story page should:

- Present the hotel's history
- Share the organization's heritage
- Build brand authenticity
- Strengthen visitor trust
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Hotel Story

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Origins
│
├── Growth & Milestones
│
├── Hospitality Philosophy
│
├── Today
│
└── Related About Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Introduce the hotel's story
- Explain the importance of its heritage
- Reflect the hotel's brand identity

---

## Origins

Provide an overview of:

- Hotel founding
- Original vision
- Early development
- Historical background

Only officially approved historical information should be presented.

---

## Growth & Milestones

Present significant milestones, including:

- Property expansion
- Major renovations
- Brand development
- Important achievements
- Organizational growth

Milestones should be presented in chronological order where appropriate.

---

## Hospitality Philosophy

Explain how the hotel's history has shaped:

- Guest service philosophy
- Operational values
- Commitment to quality
- Long-term vision

Detailed brand values should remain on dedicated pages.

---

## Today

Summarize the current organization, including:

- Present-day operations
- Commitment to guests
- Future direction
- Ongoing development

Information should remain consistent with official corporate messaging.

---

## Related About Pages

Provide links to:

- About Overview
- Brand & Values
- Sustainability
- Awards & Recognition
- Careers
- Press & Media
- About FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the About domain.
