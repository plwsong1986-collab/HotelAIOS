# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Brand & Values page on the website.

The Brand & Values page introduces the hotel's brand identity, mission, vision, and core values, helping visitors understand the principles that guide the organization's culture, guest experience, and decision-making.

---

# Objectives

The Brand & Values page should:

- Present the hotel brand
- Explain the organization's mission and vision
- Communicate core values
- Strengthen brand consistency
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Introduce the hotel's brand
- Explain the purpose of the page
- Reflect the organization's identity

---

## Brand Identity

Provide an overview of:

- Brand positioning
- Brand personality
- Hospitality philosophy
- Guest experience principles

Only officially approved brand messaging should be presented.

---

## Mission

Present the organization's mission statement, explaining:

- Purpose
- Guest commitment
- Service philosophy

Mission statements should remain consistent with official corporate documentation.

---

## Vision

Present the organization's vision statement, including:

- Long-term direction
- Organizational aspirations
- Future goals

Vision statements should remain consistent across all communication channels.

---

## Core Values

Introduce the organization's core values, such as:

- Service Excellence
- Integrity
- Respect
- Innovation
- Sustainability
- Teamwork

Detailed operational policies should remain in internal documentation.

---

## Brand Promise

Summarize the commitments made to guests, including:

- Quality
- Consistency
- Hospitality
- Trust
- Memorable experiences

Brand promises should align with official marketing and corporate communications.

---

## Related About Pages

Provide links to:

- About Overview
- Hotel Story
- Sustainability
- Awards & Recognition
- Careers
- Press & Media
- About FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the About domain.
