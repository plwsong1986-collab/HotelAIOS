# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Sustainability page on the website.

The Sustainability page introduces the hotel's environmental, social, and governance initiatives, helping visitors understand the organization's long-term commitment to responsible hospitality.

---

# Objectives

The Sustainability page should:

- Present sustainability initiatives
- Communicate environmental responsibility
- Highlight social commitments
- Build stakeholder trust
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Sustainability

│
├── Sustainability Commitment
│
├── Environmental Initiatives

├── Social Responsibility
│
├── Governance & Standards

├── Future Goals
│

```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the hotel's sustainability philosophy
- Introduce responsible hospitality practices
- Reflect the organization's long-term commitment

---

## Sustainability Commitment

Provide an overview of:

- Sustainability strategy
- Corporate commitment
- Responsible operations
- Continuous improvement

Only officially approved corporate information should be presented.

---

## Environmental Initiatives

Present initiatives such as:

- Energy efficiency
- Water conservation
- Waste reduction
- Recycling
- Sustainable sourcing

Only officially supported environmental programs should be described.

---

## Social Responsibility

Describe commitments including:

- Employee wellbeing
- Community engagement
- Local partnerships
- Diversity and inclusion
- Ethical business practices

Content should align with official corporate policies.

---

## Governance & Standards

Summarize applicable governance topics, including:

- Corporate governance
- Sustainability standards
- Compliance
- Certifications (if applicable)
- Internal accountability

Only verified information should be presented.

---

## Future Goals

Provide a high-level overview of future sustainability objectives, including:

- Continuous improvement
- Innovation
- Long-term environmental goals
- Community development

Detailed plans should remain in official corporate documentation.

---

## Related About Pages

Provide links to:

- About Overview
- Hotel Story
- Brand & Values
- Awards & Recognition
- Careers
- Press & Media
- About FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the About domain.
