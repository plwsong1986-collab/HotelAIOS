# About

**Project:** HotelAIOS  
**Module:** Website  
**Layer:** Domains  
**Domain:** About

---

# Purpose

The About domain defines all website pages that introduce the hotel, its identity, values, history, people, and corporate information.

These pages help guests, partners, investors, media, and potential employees understand the organization beyond its products and services.

---

# Scope

This domain includes:

1. About Overview
2. Hotel Story
3. Brand & Values
4. Sustainability
5. Awards & Recognition
6. Careers
7. Press & Media
8. About FAQ

# Objectives

The About domain should:

- Present the hotel's identity
- Build guest trust
- Communicate brand values
- Highlight organizational achievements
- Support employer branding
- Provide official corporate information

---

# Audience

Primary audiences include:

- Prospective guests
- Returning guests
- Business partners
- Media
- Investors
- Job applicants
- Local community

---

# Content Principles

All pages should:

- Use official corporate information
- Maintain consistent brand messaging
- Avoid duplicated content
- Link to supporting knowledge documents
- Follow Website documentation standards
