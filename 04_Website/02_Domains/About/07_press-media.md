# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Press & Media page on the website.

The Press & Media page provides official information for journalists, media organizations, partners, and public relations professionals, serving as the primary source for corporate news, media resources, and press contacts.

---

# Objectives

The Press & Media page should:

- Provide official media information
- Support press inquiries
- Present company news
- Protect brand consistency
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Press & Media

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Company News
│
├── Media Resources
│
├── Brand Assets
│
├── Press Contact
│
└── Related About Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the Press & Media page
- Welcome media professionals
- Reflect the organization's official communication standards

---

## Company News

Provide access to official announcements, including:

- Press releases
- Company news
- Business updates
- Major announcements
- Corporate milestones

Only officially approved communications should be published.

---

## Media Resources

Provide resources including:

- Company overview
- Executive information
- Fact sheets
- Corporate background
- Media kits (if available)

Resources should remain current and officially maintained.

---

## Brand Assets

Present available brand materials, including:

- Logos
- Brand guidelines
- Approved imagery
- Corporate descriptions
- Usage policies

Only officially approved assets should be distributed.

---

## Press Contact

Provide official communication channels for:

- Media inquiries
- Interview requests
- Press partnerships
- Public relations
- Corporate communications

Contact information should remain accurate and regularly maintained.

---

## Related About Pages

Provide links to:

- About Overview
- Hotel Story
- Brand & Values
- Sustainability
- Awards & Recognition
- Careers
- About FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the About domain.

# User Experience Rules

The Press & Media page should:

- Present information professionally
- Make media resources easy to locate
- Use consistent corporate messaging
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Direct media inquiries through official channels

- Hotel press keywords
- Hotel media keywords
- Hospitality news keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
