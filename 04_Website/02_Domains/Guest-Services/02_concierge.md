# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Concierge page on the website.

The Concierge page introduces concierge services available to guests, helping them discover personalized assistance, local recommendations, and travel support before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Concierge page should:

- Introduce concierge services
- Explain available guest assistance
- Help guests discover local experiences
- Support personalized service requests
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

```text
Concierge

├── Page Introduction

├── Concierge Services

├── Local Recommendations

│
└── Related Guest Services Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of concierge services
- Introduce personalized guest assistance
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Concierge Services

Present available concierge services, including:

- Local recommendations
- Restaurant reservations
- Activity bookings
- Transportation arrangements
- Special occasion assistance

Only officially supported concierge services should be presented.

---

## Local Recommendations

Introduce areas where concierge assistance may be available, such as:

- Attractions
- Dining
- Shopping
- Entertainment
- Cultural experiences

Detailed destination information belongs to the Knowledge module.

---

## Service Requests

Explain how guests may request concierge assistance through supported channels, including:

- Front Desk
- Concierge Desk
- Telephone
- Mobile application (if supported)
- Guest portal (if supported)

Request procedures should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Related Guest Services Pages

Provide links to:

- Guest Services Overview
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Airport Transfer
- Accessibility Services
- Guest Services FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Guest Services domain.
