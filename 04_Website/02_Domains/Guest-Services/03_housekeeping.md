# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Housekeeping page on the website.

The Housekeeping page introduces housekeeping services available to guests, helping them understand room care, cleaning schedules, and available housekeeping assistance throughout their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Housekeeping page should:

- Introduce housekeeping services
- Explain room cleaning options
- Support guest housekeeping requests
- Improve guest comfort
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of housekeeping services
- Introduce room care options
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Housekeeping Services

Present available housekeeping services, including:

- Daily room cleaning
- Turndown service (if available)
- Linen replacement
- Towel replacement
- Room replenishment

Only officially supported housekeeping services should be presented.

---

## Cleaning Schedule

Provide general information regarding:

- Standard cleaning hours
- Service availability
- Do Not Disturb procedures
- Housekeeping access
- Service frequency

Operational schedules should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Service Requests

Explain how guests may request housekeeping assistance through supported channels, including:

- Front Desk
- Housekeeping department
- Telephone
- Mobile application (if supported)
- Guest portal (if supported)

Request procedures should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Related Guest Services Pages

Provide links to:

- Guest Services Overview
- Concierge
- Laundry
- Airport Transfer
- Accessibility Services
- Guest Services FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Guest Services domain.
