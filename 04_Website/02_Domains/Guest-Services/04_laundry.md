# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Laundry page on the website.

The Laundry page introduces laundry and garment care services available to guests, helping them understand available options, service requests, and turnaround expectations during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Laundry page should:

- Introduce laundry services
- Explain garment care options
- Support laundry service requests
- Improve guest convenience
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of laundry services
- Introduce garment care options
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Laundry Services

Present available laundry services, including:

- Washing
- Dry cleaning (if available)
- Pressing
- Ironing
- Express service (if available)

Only officially supported laundry services should be presented.

---

## Service Process

Provide general information regarding:

- Collection procedures
- Service schedules
- Estimated turnaround time
- Garment handling
- Delivery procedures

Operational procedures should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Service Requests

Explain how guests may request laundry services through supported channels, including:

- Front Desk
- Housekeeping
- Telephone
- Mobile application (if supported)
- Guest portal (if supported)

Request procedures should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Related Guest Services Pages

Provide links to:

- Guest Services Overview
- Concierge
- Housekeeping
- Airport Transfer
- Accessibility Services
- Guest Services FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Guest Services domain.
