# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Accessibility Services page on the website.

The Accessibility Services page introduces accessibility features, guest assistance, and inclusive services available at the hotel, helping guests understand available accommodations before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Accessibility Services page should:

- Introduce accessibility services
- Explain available accessibility features
- Support accessibility service requests
- Improve travel confidence
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of accessibility services
- Introduce the hotel's commitment to accessibility
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Accessibility Features

Present available accessibility features, including:

- Accessible guest rooms
- Step-free access
- Elevators
- Accessible parking
- Accessible public areas
- Accessible restrooms

Only officially supported accessibility features should be presented.

---

## Guest Assistance

Introduce available assistance services, including:

- Arrival assistance
- Luggage assistance
- Mobility assistance
- Communication assistance
- Service animal accommodation (if supported)

Assistance should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Service Requests

Explain how guests may request accessibility services through supported channels, including:

- Reservations
- Front Desk
- Guest Services
- Telephone
- Mobile application (if supported)
- Guest portal (if supported)

Request procedures should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Related Guest Services Pages

Provide links to:

- Guest Services Overview
- Concierge
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Airport Transfer
- Guest Services FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Guest Services domain.
