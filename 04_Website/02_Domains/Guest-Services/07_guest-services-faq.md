# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Guest Services FAQ page on the website.

The Guest Services FAQ page provides answers to common questions about hotel services, helping guests quickly understand available assistance, service requests, operating procedures, and guest support before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Guest Services FAQ page should:

- Answer common guest service questions
- Improve self-service support
- Reduce guest uncertainty
- Reduce service inquiries
- Maintain a consistent website experience

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the FAQ
- Help guests quickly locate answers
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Concierge Questions

Provide answers covering topics such as:

- Concierge services
- Local recommendations
- Restaurant reservations
- Activity bookings
- Transportation assistance

Answers should remain consistent with official service information.

---

## Housekeeping Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Room cleaning schedules
- Linen replacement
- Towel replacement
- Do Not Disturb
- Housekeeping requests

Operational information should remain consistent with hotel procedures.

---

## Laundry Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Laundry services
- Dry cleaning
- Turnaround time
- Express service
- Garment care

Laundry information should reflect officially supported services.

---

## Airport Transfer Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Airport shuttle availability
- Private transfers
- Booking requirements
- Pickup arrangements
- Flight information

Transfer information should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Accessibility Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Accessible rooms
- Accessibility features
- Mobility assistance
- Service requests
- Accessible facilities

Accessibility information should remain consistent with official hotel services.

---

## Additional Support

Provide support resources including:

- Guest Services
- Concierge
- Front Desk
- Contact Information

Support options should direct guests to official communication channels.

---

## Related Guest Services Pages

Provide links to:

- Guest Services Overview
- Concierge
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Airport Transfer
- Accessibility Services

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Guest Services domain.
