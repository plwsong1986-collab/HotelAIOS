# Guest Services

**Project:** HotelAIOS  
**Module:** Website  
**Layer:** Domains  
**Domain:** Guest Services  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This domain defines all website pages related to guest services provided before, during, and after a guest's stay.

It introduces available services, explains how guests can request assistance, and provides a consistent service experience throughout the website.

---

# Scope

This domain includes:

- Guest Services Overview
- Concierge
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Airport Transfer
- Accessibility Services
- Guest Services FAQ

Operational procedures belong to the Knowledge module.

---

# Objectives

The Guest Services domain should:

- Introduce available guest services
- Help guests understand service offerings
- Improve guest convenience
- Support self-service information discovery
- Maintain consistent navigation

# Navigation

The Guest Services domain should be accessible from:

- Global Navigation
- Website Footer
- Booking Confirmation
- Contact
- Guest Account (if supported)

Every page should provide navigation to related guest service resources.

---

# Content Principles

Content should:

- Be accurate
- Be easy to understand
- Use consistent terminology
- Reflect current hotel operations
- Avoid duplicated content

Authoritative operational information belongs to the Knowledge module.

---

# User Experience

The Guest Services domain should help guests:

- Discover available services
- Request assistance
- Prepare for arrival
- Enhance their stay
- Quickly locate service information
