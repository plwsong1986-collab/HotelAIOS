# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Airport Transfer page on the website.

The Airport Transfer page introduces airport transportation services available to guests, helping them understand transfer options, booking procedures, and arrival guidance before and after their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Airport Transfer page should:

- Introduce airport transfer services
- Explain available transportation options
- Support transfer reservations
- Improve arrival convenience
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of airport transfer services
- Introduce available transportation options
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Transfer Services

Present available airport transfer services, including:

- Airport shuttle (if available)
- Private transfer
- Shared transfer (if available)
- Luxury transportation (if available)
- Accessible transportation (if available)

Only officially supported transfer services should be presented.

---

## Booking Process

Provide general information regarding:

- Reservation requirements
- Advance booking recommendations
- Pickup scheduling
- Confirmation process
- Service availability

Operational procedures should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Arrival Information

Provide guidance including:

- Airport pickup locations
- Driver identification
- Flight information requirements
- Arrival instructions
- Contact information during travel

Detailed operational instructions should remain consistent with official hotel procedures.

---

## Service Requests

Explain how guests may request airport transfer services through supported channels, including:

- Reservations
- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Telephone
- Mobile application (if supported)
- Guest portal (if supported)

Request procedures should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Related Guest Services Pages

Provide links to:

- Guest Services Overview
- Concierge
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Accessibility Services
- Guest Services FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Guest Services domain.
