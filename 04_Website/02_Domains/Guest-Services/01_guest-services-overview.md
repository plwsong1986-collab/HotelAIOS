# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Guest Services Overview page on the website.

The Guest Services Overview page introduces the hotel's available guest services, helping guests understand the support available before arrival, during their stay, and after departure.

---

# Objectives

The Guest Services Overview page should:

- Introduce available guest services
- Help guests discover service offerings
- Improve guest convenience
- Support self-service information discovery
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Guest Services Overview

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Service Categories
│
├── Featured Services
│
├── Service Requests
│
└── Related Guest Services Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the guest services section
- Introduce available hotel services
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Service Categories

Introduce major service categories, including:

- Concierge
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Airport Transfer
- Accessibility Services

Each category should link to its dedicated page.

---

## Featured Services

Highlight commonly used services, such as:

- Local recommendations
- Room assistance
- Laundry services
- Airport transportation
- Accessibility support

Only officially supported services should be presented.

---

## Service Requests

Explain how guests can request services through supported channels, including:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Telephone
- Mobile application (if supported)
- Guest portal (if supported)

Request procedures should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Related Guest Services Pages

Provide links to:

- Concierge
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Airport Transfer
- Accessibility Services
- Guest Services FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Guest Services domain.
