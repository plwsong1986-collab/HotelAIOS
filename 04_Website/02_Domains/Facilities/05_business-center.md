# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Business Center page on the website.

The Business Center page introduces the hotel's business facilities and services, helping business travelers understand the available resources for work, communication, and productivity during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Business Center page should:

- Present business facilities clearly
- Introduce available business services
- Explain access and usage information
- Support business travel needs
- Encourage facility utilization

---

# Page Structure

```text
Business Center

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Facility Overview
│
├── Business Services
│
├── Equipment & Amenities
│
├── Visitor Information
│
└── Related Facilities
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the business center
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce the available business resources

---

## Facility Overview

The overview may include:

- Workspace environment
- Business support capabilities
- Available work areas
- Guest experience
- Professional atmosphere

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Business Services

Services may include:

- Printing
- Copying
- Scanning
- Internet access
- Document services
- Administrative assistance

Only officially available services should be presented.

---

## Equipment & Amenities

Equipment and amenities may include:

- Desktop computers
- Workstations
- Printers
- Meeting tables
- Charging stations
- Office supplies

Only confirmed equipment should be listed.

---

## Visitor Information

Provide information such as:

- Opening hours
- Guest eligibility
- Access procedures
- Usage policies
- Service availability

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Meeting & Events
- Accessibility
- Facility Guide
- Contact Information

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

The Business Center page should:

- Present information clearly
- Use professional imagery

- Help guests quickly understand available business resources

- Business center keywords
