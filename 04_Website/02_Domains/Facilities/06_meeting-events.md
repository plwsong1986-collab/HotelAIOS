# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Meeting & Events page on the website.

The Meeting & Events page introduces the hotel's meeting venues and event facilities, helping organizers evaluate available spaces and services before making an inquiry or reservation.

---

# Objectives

The Meeting & Events page should:

- Present meeting and event facilities
- Introduce available venue types
- Explain supporting services
- Encourage event inquiries
- Support business and social event planning

---

```text
Meeting & Events

├── Venue Overview

```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's event capabilities
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce available meeting and event solutions

---

## Venue Overview

The overview may include:

- Venue collection
- Event capabilities
- Flexible layouts
- Business and social events
- Guest experience

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Event Spaces

Event spaces may include:

- Meeting rooms
- Conference rooms
- Ballrooms
- Boardrooms
- Outdoor venues
- Multi-purpose spaces

Only confirmed venues should be presented.

---

## Meeting Services

Services may include:

- Event planning
- Audio-visual equipment
- High-speed internet
- Catering
- Technical support
- On-site coordination

Only officially available services should be listed.

---

## Booking Information

Provide information such as:

- Inquiry process
- Reservation procedures
- Venue availability
- Contact information
- Event consultation

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Business Center
- Dining
- Accessibility
- Facility Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
