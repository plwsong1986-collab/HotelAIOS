# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Facility Guide page on the website.

The Facility Guide serves as a centralized reference for guests, providing practical information about accessing, using, and enjoying hotel facilities throughout their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Facility Guide should:

- Centralize facility information
- Help guests locate facilities quickly
- Explain facility access and usage
- Improve the overall guest experience
- Reduce operational inquiries

---

# Page Structure

```text
Facility Guide

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Facility Directory
│
├── Access Information
│
├── Usage Guidelines
│
├── Frequently Asked Questions
│
└── Support Information
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the guide
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Help guests navigate available facilities

---

## Facility Directory

The directory should organize facilities into logical categories, such as:

- Recreation
- Wellness
- Business
- Family
- Transportation
- Accessibility

Each entry should link to its corresponding facility page.

---

## Access Information

Provide information such as:

- Facility locations
- Opening hours
- Access requirements
- Reservation requirements
- Guest eligibility

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Usage Guidelines

Usage guidance may include:

- Appropriate guest behavior
- Dress requirements
- Safety reminders
- Age restrictions
- Capacity limitations
- Facility-specific instructions

Operational policies must reference official Operations documentation.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Questions may address:

- Facility availability
- Reservation procedures
- Equipment usage
- Accessibility support
- Family facilities
- Contact methods

Answers should remain concise and reference authoritative sources where applicable.

---

## Support Information

Provide official support channels, including:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Guest Services
- Contact information
- Operating hours

Only officially supported contact methods should be presented.
