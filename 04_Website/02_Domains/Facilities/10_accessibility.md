# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Accessibility page on the website.

The Accessibility page introduces the hotel's accessibility features and services, helping guests understand how the property supports visitors with different mobility and accessibility needs.

---

# Objectives

The Accessibility page should:

- Present accessibility features clearly
- Explain accessible facilities and services
- Support travel planning for all guests
- Improve confidence before arrival
- Promote an inclusive guest experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's commitment to accessibility
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce available accessibility support

---

## Accessibility Overview

The overview may include:

- Accessible design
- Inclusive guest experience
- Mobility support
- Barrier-free access
- Property accessibility

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Accessible Facilities

Facilities may include:

- Accessible guest rooms
- Step-free entrances
- Elevators
- Accessible parking
- Accessible restrooms
- Accessible public areas

Only confirmed accessibility features should be presented.

---

## Guest Services

Services may include:

- Accessibility assistance
- Luggage support
- Mobility assistance
- Service animal accommodations
- Communication support
- Special assistance requests

Only officially available services should be listed.

---

## Visitor Information

Provide information such as:

- Reservation recommendations
- Accessibility contacts
- Arrival guidance
- Assistance request procedures
- Facility availability

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Parking
- Transportation
- Rooms
- Facility Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
