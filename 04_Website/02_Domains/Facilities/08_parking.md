# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Parking page on the website.

The Parking page introduces the hotel's parking facilities and provides guests with the information required to plan vehicle access before arrival.

---

# Objectives

The Parking page should:

- Present available parking options
- Explain parking access procedures
- Provide essential visitor information
- Support arrival planning
- Reduce guest uncertainty before check-in

---

```text
Parking

├── Parking Overview
│
├── Parking Options

│

│

```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's parking services
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce available parking options

---

## Parking Overview

The overview may include:

- Parking availability
- Parking location
- Guest convenience
- Vehicle access
- General parking experience

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Parking Options

Parking options may include:

- Self-parking
- Valet parking
- Covered parking
- Outdoor parking
- Accessible parking
- Electric vehicle charging

Only confirmed services should be presented.

---

## Access Information

Provide information such as:

- Entrance location
- Arrival instructions
- Height or vehicle restrictions
- Operating hours
- Access procedures

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Parking Policies

Parking policies may include:

- Guest eligibility
- Parking availability
- Vehicle limitations
- Overnight parking
- Security information
- Special parking instructions

All policies must reference official Operations documentation.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Transportation
- Accessibility
- Facility Guide
- Contact Information

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

The Parking page should:

- Help guests prepare before arrival
