# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Fitness Center page on the website.

The Fitness Center page introduces the hotel's fitness facilities and provides guests with the information needed to plan their workouts during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Fitness Center page should:

- Present the fitness experience
- Introduce available equipment and amenities
- Explain access and usage information
- Encourage healthy lifestyles during travel
- Support guest trip planning

---

# Page Structure

```text
Fitness Center

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Facility Overview
│
├── Equipment & Amenities
│
├── Usage Information
│
├── Guest Guidelines
│
└── Related Facilities
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the fitness center
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce the wellness experience

---

## Facility Overview

The overview may include:

- Facility description
- Workout environment
- Indoor location
- Fitness experience
- Available workout spaces

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Equipment & Amenities

Equipment and amenities may include:

- Cardio equipment
- Strength training equipment
- Free weights
- Stretching area
- Towels
- Water station
- Lockers
- Changing rooms

Only confirmed facilities should be presented.

---

## Usage Information

Provide information such as:

- Opening hours
- Guest eligibility
- Age requirements
- Access methods
- Capacity limitations

Operational information must remain consistent with official policies.

---

## Guest Guidelines

Where applicable, include:

- Appropriate workout attire
- Equipment usage rules
- Hygiene expectations
- Safety reminders
- Personal belongings policy

Guest guidelines should reference official Operations documentation.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Swimming Pool
- Spa & Wellness
- Facility Guide
- Accessibility

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
