# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Swimming Pool page on the website.

The Swimming Pool page introduces the hotel's pool facilities and provides guests with essential information before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Swimming Pool page should:

- Present the swimming pool experience
- Explain available facilities
- Provide practical visitor information
- Support trip planning
- Encourage facility usage

---

```text
Swimming Pool

├── Pool Overview
│
├── Features & Amenities

├── Usage Information
│
├── Safety Guidelines
│

```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the swimming pool experience
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce the facility's primary features

---

## Pool Overview

The overview may include:

- Pool type
- General environment
- Indoor or outdoor location
- Guest experience
- Scenic highlights

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Features & Amenities

Facilities may include:

- Children's pool
- Infinity pool
- Heated pool
- Whirlpool
- Poolside lounge
- Towels
- Changing rooms
- Showers

Only confirmed amenities should be presented.

---

## Usage Information

Provide information such as:

- Opening hours
- Guest eligibility
- Capacity limitations
- Seasonal availability
- Access requirements

Operational information must remain consistent with official policies.

---

## Safety Guidelines

Where applicable, include:

- Supervision requirements
- Children's safety
- Appropriate swimwear
- Pool rules
- Emergency procedures

Safety information should reference official Operations documentation.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Spa & Wellness
- Fitness Center
- Kids Facilities
- Facility Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
