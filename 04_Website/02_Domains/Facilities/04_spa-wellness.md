# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Spa & Wellness page on the website.

The Spa & Wellness page introduces the hotel's wellness facilities and services, helping guests discover opportunities for relaxation, rejuvenation, and personal well-being before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Spa & Wellness page should:

- Present the wellness experience
- Introduce available spa services
- Explain facility information
- Encourage advance reservations
- Support guest trip planning

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the wellness philosophy
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce the spa experience

---

## Wellness Overview

The overview may include:

- Spa environment
- Wellness concept
- Relaxation experience
- Signature wellness features
- Guest experience

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Treatments & Services

Services may include:

- Massage treatments
- Facial treatments
- Body treatments
- Beauty services
- Wellness programs
- Couples treatments

Only confirmed services should be presented.

---

## Wellness Facilities

Facilities may include:

- Treatment rooms
- Sauna
- Steam room
- Relaxation lounge
- Hot tub
- Wellness reception

Operational details should remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Visitor Information

Provide information such as:

- Opening hours
- Reservation requirements
- Guest eligibility
- Arrival recommendations
- Dress guidelines
- Cancellation policy

Policy information must reference official Operations documentation.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Swimming Pool
- Fitness Center
- Facility Guide
- Accessibility

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
