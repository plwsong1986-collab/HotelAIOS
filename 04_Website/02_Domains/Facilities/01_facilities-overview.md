# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Facilities Overview page on the website.

The Facilities Overview page introduces the hotel's facilities and helps guests quickly understand the amenities and services available during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Facilities Overview page should:

- Present all major hotel facilities
- Provide a clear overview of available amenities
- Help guests discover relevant services
- Support trip planning before arrival
- Guide users to detailed facility pages

---

# Page Structure

```text
Facilities Overview

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Facility Categories
│
├── Featured Facilities
│
├── Facility Highlights
│
└── Quick Navigation
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Welcome guests to the hotel's facilities
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Explain the variety of available amenities

---

## Facility Categories

Facilities may be grouped into categories such as:

- Recreation
- Wellness
- Business
- Family
- Transportation
- Accessibility

Each category should link to its corresponding detail page.

---

## Featured Facilities

Featured facilities may include:

- Swimming pool
- Fitness center
- Spa
- Business center
- Meeting rooms
- Kids facilities

Only facilities available at the property should be presented.

---

## Facility Highlights

Highlights may include:

- Signature amenities
- Guest favorites
- Recently updated facilities
- Premium services
- Seasonal offerings

Descriptions should emphasize guest value rather than operational details.

---

## Quick Navigation

Provide navigation to:

- Individual facility pages
- Dining
- Rooms
- Experiences
- Contact information

Navigation should remain consistent with the website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Facilities Overview page should:

- Be easy to scan
- Present facilities consistently
- Use high-quality photography
- Support desktop and mobile devices
- Encourage exploration of detailed facility pages

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel facilities keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
- Search-friendly descriptions

- Facilities change
