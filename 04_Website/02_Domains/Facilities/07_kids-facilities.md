# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Kids Facilities page on the website.

The Kids Facilities page introduces family-friendly amenities and services, helping parents understand the facilities available for children before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Kids Facilities page should:

- Present children's facilities clearly
- Highlight family-friendly amenities
- Explain access and usage information
- Support family travel planning
- Encourage facility participation

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the family-friendly environment
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce children's facilities and services

---

## Facility Overview

The overview may include:

- Children's activity spaces
- Indoor and outdoor facilities
- Family-oriented environment
- Age suitability
- Guest experience

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Play Areas

Play facilities may include:

- Indoor playground
- Outdoor playground
- Children's activity room
- Games area
- Creative activity space
- Family recreation areas

Only confirmed facilities should be presented.

---

## Family Services

Family services may include:

- Children's programs
- Family activities
- Kids amenities
- Baby equipment
- Child-friendly dining support
- Family assistance services

Only officially available services should be listed.

---

## Visitor Information

Provide information such as:

- Opening hours
- Age recommendations
- Parent supervision requirements
- Access procedures
- Safety guidelines

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Swimming Pool
- Dining
- Accessibility
- Facility Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
