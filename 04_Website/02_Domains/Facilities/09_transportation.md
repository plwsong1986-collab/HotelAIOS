# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Transportation page on the website.

The Transportation page introduces the hotel's transportation options and helps guests plan their arrival, departure, and local travel efficiently.

---

# Objectives

The Transportation page should:

- Present available transportation services
- Explain arrival and departure options
- Support guest travel planning
- Improve transportation accessibility
- Reduce uncertainty before travel

---

# Page Structure

```text
Transportation

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Transportation Overview
│
├── Available Services
│
├── Arrival & Departure
│
├── Travel Information
│
└── Related Facilities
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's transportation services
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce available travel options

---

## Transportation Overview

The overview may include:

- Transportation convenience
- Property accessibility
- Nearby transportation hubs
- Guest travel experience
- Local mobility

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Available Services

Transportation services may include:

- Airport transfer
- Shuttle service
- Taxi assistance
- Car rental support
- Public transportation guidance
- Bicycle services

Only officially available services should be presented.

---

## Arrival & Departure

Provide information such as:

- Airport access
- Railway access
- Local transportation
- Pick-up procedures
- Drop-off locations
- Reservation requirements

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Travel Information

Travel information may include:

- Recommended transportation methods
- Estimated travel guidance
- Accessibility considerations
- Luggage assistance
- Contact information

Only verified information should be published.

---

## Related Facilities

Provide links to:

- Parking
- Accessibility
- Facility Guide
- Contact Information

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Transportation page should:

- Present transportation information clearly
- Support quick trip planning
- Keep arrival information easy to locate
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Link to related guest services

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel transportation keywords
- Airport transfer keywords
- Shuttle service keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
