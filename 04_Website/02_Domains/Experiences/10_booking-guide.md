# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Experiences Booking Guide page on the website.

The Booking Guide explains how guests can discover, reserve, modify, and prepare for experience bookings, providing a consistent booking journey across all experience types.

---

# Objectives

The Booking Guide should:

- Explain the booking process clearly
- Help guests prepare before booking
- Present reservation policies consistently
- Reduce booking uncertainty
- Improve the overall booking experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain how guests can book experiences
- Encourage advance reservations where appropriate
- Reflect the hotel brand

---

## Booking Process

The booking process should describe:

1. Browse available experiences
2. Review experience details
3. Select preferred date and time
4. Submit a reservation request
5. Receive booking confirmation
6. Participate in the experience

The process should remain consistent with the official reservation workflow.

---

## Reservation Methods

Supported reservation methods may include:

- Website booking
- Mobile booking
- Front desk reservation
- Concierge assistance
- Guest service support

Only officially supported reservation channels should be presented.

---

## Booking Policies

Policy information may include:

- Reservation requirements
- Modification policy
- Cancellation policy
- Capacity limitations
- Availability conditions

Policy content must remain consistent with operational documentation.

---

## Preparation Information

Preparation guidance may include:

- Arrival recommendations
- Meeting locations
- Required equipment
- Recommended clothing
- Weather considerations

Preparation information should help guests participate successfully.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions may include:

- How to book an experience
- When reservations open
- Whether modifications are allowed
- Whether cancellations are permitted
- What guests should bring

Answers should remain concise and reference official policies.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Activities
- Seasonal Experiences
- Family Experiences
- Wellness Experiences
- Cultural Experiences
- Adventure Experiences

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
