# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Cultural Experiences page on the website.

The Cultural Experiences page introduces local traditions, arts, heritage, and authentic cultural activities that allow guests to better understand the destination during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Cultural Experiences page should:

- Present cultural experiences clearly
- Highlight authentic local traditions
- Encourage meaningful destination exploration
- Support cultural learning
- Enhance the overall travel experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Cultural Experiences

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Experience Categories
│
├── Featured Experiences
│
├── Participation Information
│
└── Related Experiences
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the destination's cultural character
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Encourage guests to experience local culture

---

## Experience Categories

Experiences may be organized into categories such as:

- Heritage Sites
- Museums
- Local Arts
- Traditional Performances
- Festivals
- Cultural Workshops

Each category should link to detailed information where available.

---

## Featured Experiences

Each featured experience may include:

- Experience name
- Short description
- Cultural significance
- Key highlights
- Typical duration
- Reservation availability

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Participation Information

Provide information such as:

- Activity schedules
- Reservation methods
- Visitor recommendations
- Dress considerations
- Meeting locations

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Local Attractions
- Tours & Excursions
- Seasonal Experiences
- Adventure Experiences
- Booking Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
