# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Wellness Experiences page on the website.

The Wellness Experiences page introduces wellness-focused activities and programs that help guests relax, recharge, and maintain a healthy lifestyle during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Wellness Experiences page should:

- Present wellness experiences clearly
- Highlight health and relaxation activities
- Support guest well-being
- Encourage participation in wellness programs
- Enhance the overall stay experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Wellness Experiences

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Experience Categories
│
├── Featured Experiences
│
├── Participation Information
│
└── Related Experiences
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's wellness philosophy
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Encourage guests to prioritize well-being during their stay

---

## Experience Categories

Experiences may be organized into categories such as:

- Fitness Programs
- Yoga & Meditation
- Spa Experiences
- Mindfulness Activities
- Outdoor Wellness
- Personal Wellness Programs

Each category should link to detailed information where available.

---

## Featured Experiences

Each featured experience may include:

- Experience name
- Short description
- Wellness focus
- Key highlights
- Typical duration
- Reservation availability

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Participation Information

Provide information such as:

- Activity schedules
- Reservation methods
- Participation requirements
- Recommended attire
- Meeting locations

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Activities
- Family Experiences
- Seasonal Experiences
- Adventure Experiences
- Booking Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Wellness Experiences page should:

- Present information clearly
- Use calming, high-quality photography
- Make participation information easy to locate
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Encourage guests to explore wellness offerings

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Wellness experience keywords
- Hotel wellness keywords
- Relaxation activities keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

---

# Maintenance

Review this page when:

- Wellness programs change
- Activity schedules change
- Reservation procedures change
- Participation requirements change
- Images are updated
