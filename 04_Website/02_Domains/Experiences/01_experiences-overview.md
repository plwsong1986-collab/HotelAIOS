# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Experiences Overview page on the website.

The Experiences Overview page introduces the hotel's experience offerings and helps guests discover activities, attractions, and curated experiences before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Experiences Overview page should:

- Present available experiences clearly
- Inspire guests to explore activities
- Support itinerary planning
- Increase participation in hotel experiences
- Guide users to detailed experience pages

---

# Page Structure

```text
Experiences Overview

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Experience Categories
│
├── Featured Experiences
│
├── Experience Highlights
│
└── Quick Navigation
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Welcome guests to the hotel's experiences
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce the range of available activities

---

## Experience Categories

Experiences may be grouped into categories such as:

- Local Attractions
- Tours & Excursions
- Activities
- Seasonal Experiences
- Family Experiences
- Wellness Experiences
- Cultural Experiences
- Adventure Experiences

Each category should link to its corresponding detail page.

---

## Featured Experiences

Featured experiences may include:

- Signature hotel activities
- Popular guest experiences
- Exclusive offerings
- Seasonal recommendations
- Local highlights

Only officially available experiences should be presented.

---

## Experience Highlights

Highlights may include:

- Guest favorites
- Limited-time experiences
- Curated itineraries
- Exclusive partnerships
- Recommended activities

Descriptions should emphasize guest value and memorable experiences.

---

## Quick Navigation

Provide navigation to:

- Individual experience pages
- Dining
- Facilities
- Rooms
- Contact information

Navigation should remain consistent with the website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Experiences Overview page should:

- Be easy to scan
- Present experiences consistently
- Use high-quality photography
- Support desktop and mobile devices
- Encourage exploration of detailed experience pages

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel experiences keywords
- Local activities keywords
- Travel experience keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

# Maintenance

Review this page when:

- Experience offerings change
- Seasonal activities change
- Partner information changes
- Images are updated
