# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Family Experiences page on the website.

The Family Experiences page introduces activities and services designed for families, helping guests discover enjoyable experiences for children and adults before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Family Experiences page should:

- Present family-friendly experiences clearly
- Highlight activities suitable for different age groups
- Support family travel planning
- Encourage participation in shared experiences
- Enhance the overall family stay

---

# Page Structure

```text
Family Experiences

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Experience Categories
│
├── Featured Experiences
│
├── Participation Information
│
└── Related Experiences
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's family-friendly experience offerings
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Encourage families to explore activities together

---

## Experience Categories

Experiences may be organized into categories such as:

- Children's Activities
- Parent & Child Activities
- Family Entertainment
- Educational Experiences
- Outdoor Experiences
- Indoor Experiences

Each category should link to detailed information where available.

---

## Featured Experiences

Each featured experience may include:

- Experience name
- Short description
- Age suitability
- Key highlights
- Typical duration
- Reservation availability

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Participation Information

Provide information such as:

- Activity schedules
- Reservation methods
- Age recommendations
- Parent supervision requirements
- Meeting locations

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Activities
- Seasonal Experiences
- Wellness Experiences
- Adventure Experiences
- Booking Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Family Experiences page should:

- Present information clearly
- Use welcoming, family-oriented photography
- Make participation requirements easy to locate
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Help families plan memorable experiences together

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Family hotel keywords
- Family activities keywords
- Kids activities keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

- Experience offerings change
