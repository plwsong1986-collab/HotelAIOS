# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Tours & Excursions page on the website.

The Tours & Excursions page introduces guided tours and excursion opportunities available to hotel guests, helping them discover organized experiences before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Tours & Excursions page should:

- Present available tours clearly
- Introduce organized excursion experiences
- Support itinerary planning
- Encourage advance bookings
- Help guests explore the destination with confidence

---

# Page Structure

```text
Tours & Excursions

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Tour Categories
│
├── Featured Tours
│
├── Booking Information
│
└── Related Experiences
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Introduce the hotel's tour offerings
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Encourage guests to discover the destination

---

## Tour Categories

Tours may be organized into categories such as:

- City Tours
- Nature Tours
- Cultural Tours
- Historical Tours
- Food Tours
- Adventure Excursions

Each category should link to detailed information where available.

---

## Featured Tours

Each featured tour may include:

- Tour name
- Short description
- Key highlights
- Estimated duration
- Recommended guest type
- Reservation availability

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Booking Information

Provide information such as:

- Reservation methods
- Advance booking recommendations
- Meeting locations
- Departure schedules
- Availability information

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Local Attractions
- Activities
- Cultural Experiences
- Booking Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.
