# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Adventure Experiences page on the website.

The Adventure Experiences page introduces outdoor and adventure activities available to hotel guests, helping them discover exciting experiences while maintaining clear expectations for participation and safety.

---

# Objectives

The Adventure Experiences page should:

- Present adventure experiences clearly
- Highlight outdoor and active experiences
- Support activity planning
- Encourage responsible participation
- Enhance the destination experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Adventure Experiences

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Experience Categories
│
├── Featured Experiences
│
├── Participation Information
│
├── Safety Information
│
└── Related Experiences
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the destination's adventure opportunities
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Encourage guests to explore responsibly

---

## Experience Categories

Experiences may be organized into categories such as:

- Hiking
- Cycling
- Water Activities
- Wildlife Experiences
- Outdoor Adventures
- Guided Adventure Tours

Each category should link to detailed information where available.

---

## Featured Experiences

Each featured experience may include:

- Experience name
- Short description
- Activity highlights
- Typical duration
- Recommended participant profile
- Reservation availability

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Participation Information

Provide information such as:

- Activity schedules
- Reservation methods
- Equipment requirements
- Participant recommendations
- Meeting locations

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Safety Information

Safety information may include:

- Physical activity recommendations
- Weather considerations
- Equipment guidance
- Age restrictions
- Safety requirements

Only officially approved safety guidance should be presented.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Activities
- Local Attractions
- Tours & Excursions
- Seasonal Experiences
- Booking Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Adventure Experiences page should:

- Present information consistently
- Clearly distinguish activity requirements
- Use high-quality photography
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Encourage responsible participation

The page should support:

- Adventure experiences keywords
- Outdoor activities keywords
- Adventure travel keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

- Adventure offerings change
