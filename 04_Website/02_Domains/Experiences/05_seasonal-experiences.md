# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Seasonal Experiences page on the website.

The Seasonal Experiences page introduces limited-time activities, celebrations, and destination-specific experiences that vary throughout the year, helping guests discover timely opportunities during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Seasonal Experiences page should:

- Present seasonal experiences clearly
- Highlight limited-time activities
- Support seasonal trip planning
- Encourage participation during special periods
- Keep seasonal information current

---

# Page Structure

```text
Seasonal Experiences

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Seasonal Categories
│
├── Featured Experiences
│
├── Availability Information
│
└── Related Experiences
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's seasonal offerings
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Encourage guests to explore experiences available during their visit

---

## Seasonal Categories

Experiences may be organized into categories such as:

- Spring Experiences
- Summer Experiences
- Autumn Experiences
- Winter Experiences
- Holiday Celebrations
- Local Festivals

Each category should link to detailed information where available.

---

## Featured Experiences

Each featured experience may include:

- Experience name
- Short description
- Seasonal highlights
- Availability period
- Recommended participants
- Reservation availability

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Availability Information

Provide information such as:

- Operating period
- Reservation recommendations
- Capacity limitations
- Weather considerations
- Schedule updates

Operational information must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Activities
- Local Attractions
- Tours & Excursions
- Family Experiences
- Booking Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Seasonal Experiences page should:

- Clearly identify seasonal availability
- Present information consistently
- Use high-quality seasonal photography
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Encourage timely reservations where applicable

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Seasonal experiences keywords
- Holiday activities keywords
- Seasonal travel keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

---

# Maintenance

Review this page when:

- Seasonal offerings change
- Event schedules change
- Reservation policies change
- Availability periods change
- Images are updated
