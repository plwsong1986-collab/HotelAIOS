# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Local Attractions page on the website.

The Local Attractions page introduces nearby points of interest and helps guests discover destinations that complement their hotel stay.

---

# Objectives

The Local Attractions page should:

- Present nearby attractions clearly
- Help guests plan local visits
- Highlight destination experiences
- Support itinerary planning
- Encourage exploration beyond the hotel

---

# Page Structure

```text
Local Attractions

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Attraction Categories
│
├── Featured Attractions
│
├── Visitor Information
│
└── Related Experiences
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Introduce the surrounding destination
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Encourage guests to explore the local area

---

## Attraction Categories

Attractions may be organized into categories such as:

- Landmarks
- Nature
- Museums
- Shopping
- Entertainment
- Family Attractions
- Cultural Sites

Each category should link to detailed information where available.

---

## Featured Attractions

Each featured attraction may include:

- Attraction name
- Short description
- Primary highlights
- Approximate travel distance
- Recommended transportation
- Link to additional information

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Visitor Information

Visitor information may include:

- Recommended visiting times
- Typical visit duration
- Accessibility information
- Transportation options
- Travel recommendations

Only verified information should be published.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Tours & Excursions
- Activities
- Cultural Experiences
- Booking Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Local Attractions page should:

- Present attractions consistently
- Support quick trip planning
- Use high-quality destination photography
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Encourage exploration through internal links

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Local attractions keywords
- Nearby places keywords
- Destination guide keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

Review this page when:

- Attraction information changes
