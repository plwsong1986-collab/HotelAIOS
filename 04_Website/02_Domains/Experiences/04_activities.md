# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Activities page on the website.

The Activities page introduces recreational and lifestyle activities available to hotel guests, helping them discover enjoyable experiences before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Activities page should:

- Present available activities clearly
- Introduce recreational opportunities
- Support guest itinerary planning
- Encourage participation
- Enhance the overall stay experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Activities

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Activity Categories
│
├── Featured Activities
│
├── Participation Information
│
└── Related Experiences
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Introduce the hotel's activity offerings
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Encourage guests to participate throughout their stay

---

## Activity Categories

Activities may be organized into categories such as:

- Outdoor Activities
- Indoor Activities
- Sports
- Wellness Activities
- Family Activities
- Cultural Activities

Each category should link to detailed information where available.

---

## Featured Activities

Each featured activity may include:

- Activity name
- Short description
- Key highlights
- Recommended participants
- Typical duration
- Reservation availability

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Participation Information

Provide information such as:

- Activity schedules
- Reservation methods
- Participant requirements
- Equipment availability
- Meeting locations

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Experiences

Provide links to:

- Local Attractions
- Tours & Excursions
- Family Experiences
- Adventure Experiences
- Booking Guide

Navigation should remain consistent with the overall website architecture.

# User Experience Rules

The Activities page should:

- Present activities consistently
- Make participation information easy to find
- Use high-quality photography
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Encourage discovery through internal navigation

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel activities keywords
- Guest activities keywords
- Recreation keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

---

# Maintenance

Review this page when:

- Activity offerings change
- Schedules change
- Reservation procedures change
- Participation requirements change
- Images are updated
