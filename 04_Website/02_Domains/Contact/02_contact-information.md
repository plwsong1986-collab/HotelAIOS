# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Contact Information page on the website.

The Contact Information page provides guests with official contact details for the hotel and its service departments, ensuring communication is clear, reliable, and easily accessible.

---

# Objectives

The Contact Information page should:

- Present official contact details
- Help guests reach the correct department
- Support pre-arrival and in-stay communication
- Improve guest confidence
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Contact Information

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Primary Contact Details
│
├── Department Contacts
│
├── Business Hours
│
├── Communication Guidelines
│
└── Related Contact Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the contact information page
- Encourage guests to use official contact channels
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Primary Contact Details

Present official hotel contact information, including:

- Hotel name
- Telephone number
- Email address
- Website
- Physical address

Only officially approved contact details should be displayed.

---

## Department Contacts

Provide contact information for departments such as:

- Front Desk
- Reservations
- Guest Services
- Events
- Sales
- General Inquiries

Department availability should reflect operational information.

---

## Business Hours

Provide operating hours where applicable, including:

- Front Desk
- Reservation Office
- Guest Services
- Administrative Office

Hours should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Communication Guidelines

Explain communication expectations, including:

- Preferred contact methods
- Typical response times
- Emergency contact guidance
- Language support (if applicable)

Communication guidance should reflect actual service capabilities.

---

## Related Contact Pages

Provide links to:

- Contact Overview
- Location & Directions
- Transportation
- Parking
- Contact FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Contact domain.
