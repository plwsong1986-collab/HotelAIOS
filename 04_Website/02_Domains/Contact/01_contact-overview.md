# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Contact Overview page on the website.

The Contact Overview page introduces all official communication channels, helping guests quickly find the appropriate way to contact the hotel before, during, or after their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Contact Overview page should:

- Introduce available contact channels
- Help guests reach the appropriate department
- Support guest communication
- Improve accessibility
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Contact Overview

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Contact Channels
│
├── Service Departments
│
├── Quick Actions
│
└── Related Contact Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the contact section
- Encourage guests to use official communication channels
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Contact Channels

Introduce available contact methods, such as:

- Telephone
- Email
- Contact form
- Live chat (if supported)
- Social media (if supported)

Only officially supported communication channels should be presented.

---

## Service Departments

Provide an overview of available departments, including:

- Front Desk
- Reservations
- Guest Services
- Events
- Sales
- General Inquiries

Detailed department information belongs to dedicated contact pages.

---

## Quick Actions

Provide shortcuts to common guest tasks, including:

- Make a reservation
- Manage a booking
- Request assistance
- Find directions
- View frequently asked questions

Actions should remain consistent with website navigation.

---

## Related Contact Pages

Provide links to:

- Contact Information
- Location & Directions
- Transportation
- Parking
- Contact FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Contact domain.

# User Experience Rules

The Contact Overview page should:

- Make contact options easy to find
- Use clear navigation
- Present official communication channels only
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Reduce guest effort when seeking assistance

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel contact keywords
- Contact hotel keywords
- Hotel customer service keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
