# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Parking page on the website.

The Parking page provides guests with clear information about parking facilities, vehicle access, parking policies, and related services to help them prepare for arrival.

---

# Objectives

The Parking page should:

- Present parking facilities clearly
- Explain parking availability
- Introduce parking services
- Help guests prepare before arrival
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the parking page
- Help guests prepare for vehicle arrival
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Parking Facilities

Present available parking facilities, including:

- On-site parking
- Indoor parking
- Outdoor parking
- Accessible parking
- Electric vehicle charging (if available)

Only officially supported facilities should be presented.

---

## Parking Policies

Provide general parking information, including:

- Parking availability
- Guest eligibility
- Parking hours
- Vehicle size limitations
- Parking fees (if applicable)

Official operational policies should remain authoritative.

---

## Vehicle Services

Introduce available vehicle-related services, such as:

- Valet parking (if available)
- Self-parking
- Electric vehicle charging
- Bicycle parking
- Motorcycle parking

Only officially supported services should be displayed.

---

## Parking Guidance

Provide practical guidance including:

- Parking entrance
- Arrival instructions
- Height restrictions
- Navigation recommendations
- Safety reminders

Detailed navigation should reference official mapping services where applicable.

---

## Related Contact Pages

Provide links to:

- Contact Overview
- Contact Information
- Location & Directions
- Transportation
- Contact FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Contact domain.
