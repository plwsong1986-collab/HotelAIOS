# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Location & Directions page on the website.

The Location & Directions page helps guests locate the hotel, understand nearby landmarks, and choose the most appropriate route before arriving.

---

# Objectives

The Location & Directions page should:

- Present the hotel's location clearly
- Help guests plan their journey
- Introduce nearby landmarks
- Improve arrival confidence
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Location & Directions

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Hotel Location
│
├── Interactive Map
│
├── Nearby Landmarks
│
├── Directions
│
└── Related Contact Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the location page
- Help guests prepare for arrival
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Hotel Location

Present official location information including:

- Hotel address
- City
- State or Province
- Postal code
- Country
- Geographic coordinates (if supported)

Only officially approved location information should be displayed.

---

## Interactive Map

Provide map functionality including:

- Hotel location
- Zoom controls
- Route planning
- Nearby attractions
- Map provider attribution

Map functionality should be supplied by supported mapping services.

---

## Nearby Landmarks

Introduce nearby locations such as:

- Airports
- Railway stations
- City center
- Shopping districts
- Tourist attractions
- Business districts

Descriptions should remain concise and factual.

---

## Directions

Provide general travel guidance for:

- Driving
- Walking
- Public transportation
- Taxi or ride-sharing
- Airport transfers

Detailed navigation should be provided by mapping services.

---

## Related Contact Pages

Provide links to:

- Contact Overview
- Contact Information
- Transportation
- Parking
- Contact FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Contact domain.

- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Help guests reach the hotel efficiently
