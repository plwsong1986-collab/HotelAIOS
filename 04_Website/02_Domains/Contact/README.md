# Contact

**Project:** HotelAIOS  
**Module:** Website  
**Layer:** Domains  
**Domain:** Contact  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This domain defines all website pages related to guest communication and contact channels.

It provides clear, accessible, and trustworthy ways for guests to contact the hotel before, during, and after their stay.

---

# Scope

This domain includes:

- Contact Overview
- Contact Information
- Location & Directions
- Transportation
- Parking
- Frequently Asked Questions

Operational workflows belong to the Knowledge module.

---

# Objectives

The Contact domain should:

- Make hotel contact information easy to find
- Help guests reach the appropriate department
- Support trip planning
- Improve guest confidence
- Maintain consistent navigation

---

# Information Architecture

```text
Contact

├── Contact Overview
├── Contact Information
├── Location & Directions
├── Transportation
├── Parking
└── Contact FAQ
```

---

# Navigation

The Contact domain should be accessible from:

- Global Navigation
- Website Footer
- Booking Confirmation
- Guest Services
- Help Pages

Every page should provide navigation to related contact resources.

---

# Content Principles

Content should:

- Be accurate
- Be easy to understand
- Use consistent terminology
- Reflect current operational information
- Avoid duplicated content

Authoritative operational details belong to the Knowledge module.

---

# User Experience

The Contact domain should help guests:

- Contact the hotel quickly
- Find directions
- Plan transportation
- Locate parking
- Get answers before arrival
