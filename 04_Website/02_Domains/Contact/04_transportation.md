# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Transportation page on the website.

The Transportation page helps guests understand the available transportation options for reaching the hotel and traveling to nearby destinations before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Transportation page should:

- Present available transportation options
- Help guests plan their journey
- Explain transportation services
- Improve arrival convenience
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the transportation page
- Help guests choose appropriate travel options
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Arrival Transportation

Introduce transportation options for arriving at the hotel, including:

- Airport transportation
- Railway transportation
- Public transportation
- Taxi services
- Ride-sharing services

Only officially supported transportation information should be presented.

---

## Local Transportation

Provide information about local travel options, including:

- Bus routes
- Metro or subway
- Tram services
- Taxi availability
- Bicycle rental (if supported)

Operational details should remain consistent with local transportation providers.

---

## Hotel Transportation Services

Present hotel-supported transportation services such as:

- Airport shuttle (if available)
- Scheduled shuttle services
- Private transfer arrangements
- Concierge transportation assistance

Only officially available hotel services should be displayed.

---

## Travel Information

Provide general travel guidance including:

- Estimated travel times
- Peak traffic considerations
- Accessibility information
- Travel recommendations
- Seasonal transportation considerations

Detailed routing should be provided by mapping or transportation services.

---

## Related Contact Pages

Provide links to:

- Contact Overview
- Contact Information
- Location & Directions
- Parking
- Contact FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Contact domain.
