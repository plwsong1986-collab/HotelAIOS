# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Contact FAQ page on the website.

The Contact FAQ page provides answers to common questions about contacting the hotel, finding the property, transportation, parking, and guest communication before, during, and after a stay.

---

# Objectives

The Contact FAQ page should:

- Answer common contact questions
- Reduce guest uncertainty
- Improve self-service support
- Reduce customer service requests
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the FAQ
- Help guests quickly locate answers
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Contact Questions

Provide answers covering topics such as:

- How to contact the hotel
- Department contact information
- Business hours
- Emergency contact guidance
- Response expectations

Answers should remain consistent with official operational information.

---

## Location Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Hotel address
- Nearby landmarks
- Map availability
- Arrival directions
- Navigation assistance

Location information should remain consistent with official hotel information.

---

## Transportation Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Airport transportation
- Public transportation
- Taxi services
- Ride-sharing services
- Hotel transportation services

Transportation information should reflect officially supported services.

---

## Parking Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Parking availability
- Parking fees
- Valet parking
- Electric vehicle charging
- Accessible parking

Parking information should remain consistent with hotel operations.

---

## Additional Support

Provide support resources including:

- Contact Information
- Reservation assistance
- Guest Services
- Frequently requested resources

Support options should direct guests to official communication channels.

---

## Related Contact Pages

Provide links to:

- Contact Overview
- Contact Information
- Location & Directions
- Transportation
- Parking

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Contact domain.
