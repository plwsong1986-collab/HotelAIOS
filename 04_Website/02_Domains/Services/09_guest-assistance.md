# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Guest Assistance page on the website.

The Guest Assistance page introduces the hotel's general guest support services, helping guests understand how to obtain assistance before arrival, during their stay, and after departure.

---

# Objectives

The Guest Assistance page should:

- Present guest support services clearly
- Explain how guests can request assistance
- Improve guest confidence and convenience
- Support a consistent service experience
- Maintain clear website navigation

---

# Page Structure

```text
Guest Assistance

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Assistance Services
│
├── Support Channels
│
├── Service Availability
│
└── Related Services
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's commitment to guest support
- Explain the purpose of guest assistance services
- Reflect the hotel's hospitality philosophy

---

## Assistance Services

Available assistance may include:

- General guest inquiries
- Special requests
- Lost and found
- Accessibility assistance
- Travel support
- Stay-related assistance

Only officially supported services should be presented.

---

## Support Channels

Support channels may include:

- Front desk
- Concierge
- Telephone
- Guest application
- Email support

Detailed operational procedures belong to the Knowledge module.

---

## Service Availability

Provide high-level information such as:

- Availability hours
- Contact methods
- Expected response channels
- Service scope
- Escalation when appropriate

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Transportation
- Business Services
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.

# User Experience Rules

The Guest Assistance page should:

- Present support options clearly
- Help guests quickly identify the appropriate service
- Avoid unnecessary operational complexity
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Improve guest confidence when requesting assistance
