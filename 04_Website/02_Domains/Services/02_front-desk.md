# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Front Desk page on the website.

The Front Desk page introduces reception services available throughout the guest journey, helping guests understand how the front desk can assist before arrival, during the stay, and at departure.

---

# Objectives

The Front Desk page should:

- Present front desk services clearly
- Explain how guests can obtain assistance
- Set appropriate service expectations
- Support guest convenience
- Maintain consistent website presentation

---

# Page Structure

```text
Front Desk

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Available Services
│
├── Service Availability
│
├── How to Request Assistance
│
└── Related Services
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the role of the front desk
- Explain how the team supports guests
- Reflect the hotel's service philosophy

---

## Available Services

Available services may include:

- Check-in assistance
- Check-out assistance
- Guest information
- Room inquiries
- Key replacement
- General guest support

Only officially supported services should be presented.

---

## Service Availability

Provide high-level information such as:

- Service hours
- Service location
- Languages supported
- Contact methods
- Response expectations

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## How to Request Assistance

Guests may request assistance through channels such as:

- Front desk visit
- Telephone
- Guest application
- Concierge referral

Detailed operating procedures belong to the Knowledge module.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Concierge
- Guest Assistance
- Room Service
- Transportation
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.

# User Experience Rules

The Front Desk page should:

- Make service information easy to understand
- Clearly describe available assistance
- Avoid operational complexity
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Help guests quickly find support

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel front desk keywords
- Hotel reception keywords
- Guest services keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

# Maintenance

Review this page when:

- Front desk services change
- Service hours change
- Contact methods change
- Operational procedures change
- Website navigation changes
