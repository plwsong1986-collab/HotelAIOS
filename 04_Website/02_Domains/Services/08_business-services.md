# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Business Services page on the website.

The Business Services page introduces facilities and support services designed for business travelers, helping guests understand available resources for meetings, remote work, and business activities during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Business Services page should:

- Present business services clearly
- Explain available business support
- Help guests prepare for work-related needs
- Encourage use of business facilities
- Maintain consistent website presentation

---

# Page Structure

```text
Business Services

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Available Services
│
├── Business Facilities
│
├── Service Availability
│
└── Related Services
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's business support services
- Explain how the hotel assists business travelers
- Reflect the hotel's professional service standards

---

## Available Services

Available services may include:

- Printing
- Copying
- Scanning
- Document assistance
- Internet access
- Administrative support

Only officially supported services should be presented.

---

## Business Facilities

Business facilities may include:

- Business center
- Meeting rooms
- Conference facilities
- Private workspaces
- Co-working areas

Each facility should link to its dedicated page where applicable.

---

## Service Availability

Provide high-level information such as:

- Operating hours
- Reservation requirements
- Service locations
- Contact methods
- Availability conditions

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Transportation
- Meeting & Events
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.
