# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Transportation page on the website.

The Transportation page introduces transportation services available to guests, helping them understand available travel options before arrival, during their stay, and upon departure.

---

# Objectives

The Transportation page should:

- Present transportation services clearly
- Explain available travel options
- Support guest travel planning
- Improve arrival and departure convenience
- Maintain consistent website presentation

---

# Page Structure

```text
Transportation

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Transportation Services
│
├── Service Availability
│
├── Booking Information
│
└── Related Services
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's transportation services
- Explain the convenience provided to guests
- Reflect the hotel's hospitality philosophy

---

## Transportation Services

Available services may include:

- Airport transfer
- Shuttle service
- Taxi assistance
- Private vehicle service
- Car rental assistance
- Local transportation information

Only officially supported services should be presented.

---

## Service Availability

Provide high-level information such as:

- Operating hours
- Service coverage
- Reservation requirements
- Pickup and drop-off locations
- Availability conditions

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Booking Information

Provide a general overview of how guests may arrange transportation, including:

- Advance reservation
- Front desk assistance
- Concierge assistance
- Guest application
- Contact methods

Detailed operational procedures belong to the Knowledge module.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Guest Assistance
- Experiences
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.
