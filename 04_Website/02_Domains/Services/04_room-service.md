# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Room Service page on the website.

The Room Service page introduces in-room dining services, helping guests understand how meals and beverages can be ordered and delivered during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Room Service page should:

- Present room service clearly
- Explain available ordering methods
- Set appropriate service expectations
- Encourage convenient in-room dining
- Maintain consistent website presentation

---

# Page Structure

```text
Room Service

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Service Overview
│
├── Menu Categories
│
├── Ordering Information
│
├── Delivery Information
│
└── Related Services
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the convenience of room service
- Reflect the hotel's hospitality philosophy
- Encourage guests to enjoy in-room dining

---

## Service Overview

Provide a high-level overview of room service, including:

- In-room dining
- Beverage service
- Breakfast service
- Special meal options
- Guest convenience

Detailed menu information belongs to the Dining domain or Knowledge module.

---

## Menu Categories

Menu categories may include:

- Breakfast
- All-day Dining
- Snacks
- Desserts
- Beverages
- Children's Menu

Each category should link to detailed menu information where available.

---

## Ordering Information

Provide high-level information such as:

- Ordering methods
- Ordering hours
- Confirmation process
- Special requests
- Guest communication

Operational procedures must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Delivery Information

Provide information such as:

- Delivery process
- Estimated delivery expectations
- Service availability
- Delivery locations
- Contact channels

Only officially approved operational information should be presented.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Dining
- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Guest Assistance
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.
