# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Laundry page on the website.

The Laundry page introduces laundry and garment care services, helping guests understand available laundry options, service availability, and how to request laundry assistance during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Laundry page should:

- Present laundry services clearly
- Explain available garment care options
- Help guests understand service availability
- Support guest convenience during extended stays
- Maintain consistent website presentation

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's laundry services
- Explain the convenience of professional garment care
- Reflect the hotel's service philosophy

---

## Laundry Services

Available services may include:

- Standard laundry
- Dry cleaning
- Pressing and ironing
- Express laundry
- Garment care
- Special fabric handling

Only officially supported services should be presented.

---

## Service Availability

Provide high-level information such as:

- Service hours
- Collection schedule
- Delivery schedule
- Express service availability
- Service coverage

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Laundry Process

Provide a general overview of the service process, including:

1. Submit a laundry request
2. Prepare garments for collection
3. Laundry collection
4. Cleaning and garment care
5. Delivery back to the guest room

Detailed operational procedures belong to the Knowledge module.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Housekeeping
- Front Desk
- Guest Assistance
- Room Service
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.
