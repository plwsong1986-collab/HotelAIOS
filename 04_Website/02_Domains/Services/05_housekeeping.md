# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Housekeeping page on the website.

The Housekeeping page introduces room cleaning and housekeeping services, helping guests understand available housekeeping options and how to request assistance during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Housekeeping page should:

- Present housekeeping services clearly
- Explain available cleaning options
- Help guests understand service availability
- Support guest comfort throughout the stay
- Maintain consistent website presentation

---

```text
Housekeeping

├── Page Introduction

├── Housekeeping Services
│
├── Service Availability

├── Special Requests
│

```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the housekeeping service philosophy
- Explain the role of housekeeping in enhancing guest comfort
- Reflect the hotel's commitment to cleanliness and hospitality

---

## Housekeeping Services

Available services may include:

- Daily room cleaning
- Towel replacement
- Linen replacement
- Evening turndown service
- Additional amenities
- Extra housekeeping requests

Only officially supported services should be presented.

---

## Service Availability

Provide high-level information such as:

- Regular housekeeping schedule
- Service hours
- Service frequency
- Guest availability considerations
- Service notifications

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Special Requests

Guests may request services such as:

- Additional towels
- Extra bedding
- Additional toiletries
- Room refresh
- Special housekeeping assistance

Detailed operating procedures belong to the Knowledge module.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Front Desk
- Laundry
- Guest Assistance
- Room Service
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.
