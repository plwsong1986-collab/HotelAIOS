# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Concierge page on the website.

The Concierge page introduces personalized guest assistance services, helping guests discover destination recommendations, travel support, reservations, and other concierge services before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Concierge page should:

- Present concierge services clearly
- Explain how guests can request assistance
- Highlight personalized guest support
- Encourage guests to make use of concierge expertise
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the role of the concierge
- Present the hotel's personalized service philosophy
- Encourage guests to seek local recommendations and assistance

---

## Concierge Services

Available services may include:

- Local recommendations
- Restaurant reservations
- Attraction reservations
- Transportation arrangements
- Event information
- Special guest requests

Only officially supported services should be presented.

---

## Personalized Assistance

Describe the types of assistance the concierge can provide, such as:

- Customized itineraries
- Local travel advice
- Celebration arrangements
- Activity recommendations
- Guest preference support

Detailed operational procedures belong to the Knowledge module.

---

## How to Contact the Concierge

Provide high-level information about available contact channels, including:

- Concierge desk
- Telephone
- Guest application
- Front desk referral

Operational details must remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Front Desk
- Transportation
- Guest Assistance
- Experiences
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.
