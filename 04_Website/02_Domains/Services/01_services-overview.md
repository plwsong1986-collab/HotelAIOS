# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Services Overview page on the website.

The Services Overview page introduces the hotel's service offerings, helping guests quickly understand available services and navigate to detailed service information before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Services Overview page should:

- Introduce all major hotel services
- Help guests discover available assistance
- Provide clear navigation to service pages
- Support guest planning before arrival
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Services Overview

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Service Categories
│
├── Featured Services
│
├── How to Access Services
│
└── Related Services
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's service philosophy
- Explain the purpose of the Services section
- Encourage guests to explore available services

---

## Service Categories

The overview may organize services into categories such as:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Room Service
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Transportation
- Business Services
- Guest Assistance

Each category should link to its dedicated page.

---

## Featured Services

Featured services may include:

- Service name
- Short description
- Primary benefits
- Availability summary
- Link to detailed information

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## How to Access Services

Provide a high-level overview of available service channels, such as:

- Front desk
- Telephone
- Mobile services
- Guest application
- Concierge

Detailed operational procedures belong to the Service Guide.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Room Service
- Guest Assistance
- Service Guide

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.

# User Experience Rules

The Services Overview page should:

- Present services clearly
- Use consistent navigation
- Minimize information overload
- Support desktop and mobile browsing
- Help guests quickly locate desired services

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Hotel services keywords
- Guest services keywords
- Hotel assistance keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links

# Maintenance

Review this page when:

- New services are introduced
- Existing services change
- Navigation changes
- Operational information changes
- Featured services are updated
