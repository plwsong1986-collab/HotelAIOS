# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Service Guide page on the website.

The Service Guide explains how guests can access hotel services, what service channels are available, and where to find additional assistance throughout their stay. It provides a central entry point for service-related information across the website.

---

# Objectives

The Service Guide should:

- Explain how guests access hotel services
- Present available service channels
- Help guests locate appropriate assistance
- Improve service accessibility
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the Service Guide
- Introduce available guest service channels
- Reflect the hotel's service philosophy

---

## Service Channels

Describe the available service channels, which may include:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Telephone
- Guest Application
- Email Support

Only officially supported communication channels should be presented.

---

## Service Availability

Provide high-level information such as:

- General service hours
- Department availability
- Response expectations
- Service limitations
- Contact availability

Detailed operational information belongs to the Knowledge module.

---

## Request Process

Provide a general overview of how guests may request services:

1. Identify the required service
2. Select an appropriate contact channel
3. Submit the request
4. Receive confirmation when applicable
5. Service fulfillment

Operational workflows should remain consistent with official documentation.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions may include:

- How to request hotel services
- Which department to contact
- How to modify a request
- Available communication methods
- Where to obtain additional assistance

Answers should remain concise and reference official policies where appropriate.

---

## Related Services

Provide links to:

- Front Desk
- Concierge
- Room Service
- Housekeeping
- Laundry
- Transportation
- Business Services
- Guest Assistance

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the website.
