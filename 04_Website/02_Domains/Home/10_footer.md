# Purpose

This document defines the structure and content requirements for the website footer.

The footer provides supporting navigation, essential information, trust elements, and secondary user access points.

---

# Objectives

The Footer should:

- Provide important supporting links
- Improve website accessibility
- Support user navigation
- Display required legal information
- Maintain brand consistency

---

# Structure

```text
Footer

├── Brand Information
│
├── Navigation Links
│
├── Contact Information
│
├── Legal Information
│
└── Social Media
```

---

# Content Components

## Brand Information

May include:

- Hotel name
- Logo
- Short brand statement
- Brand-related information

Brand content should reference the Brand module.

---

## Navigation Links

Footer navigation may include:

```text
Rooms
Dining
Facilities
Experiences
Offers
About
Contact
FAQ
Booking
```

Links should follow the official website hierarchy.

---

## Contact Information

May include:

- Address
- Phone number
- Email
- Contact channels
- Location information

All factual information should reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Legal Information

May include:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Cookie Policy
- Accessibility Statement

Legal content should reference the Legal module when available.

---

## Social Media

May include:

- Official social media links
- Community channels
- Brand platforms

All social media references should use official accounts only.

---

# Footer Principles

The Footer should:

- Remain consistent across pages
- Avoid unnecessary duplication
- Provide clear navigation
- Support user trust
- Maintain clean presentation

---

# Content Ownership

| Content | Owner |
|---------|-------|
| Footer structure | Website |
| Brand information | Brand |
| Hotel information | Knowledge |
| Legal information | Legal |
| Social media assets | Media |

---

# User Experience Rules

The Footer should:

- Be easy to scan
- Work across all devices
- Provide important information without clutter
- Maintain accessibility standards

---

# Maintenance

Review the Footer when:

- Website structure changes
- Contact information changes
- Legal requirements change
- Social media channels change
