# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Special Offers section on the homepage.

The section introduces current promotions, packages, and value-added opportunities to encourage guest engagement and bookings.

---

# Objectives

The Special Offers section should:

- Present available promotions clearly
- Encourage direct booking
- Highlight seasonal opportunities
- Improve guest conversion
- Support marketing campaigns

---

# Structure

```text
Special Offers Section

├── Section Introduction
│
├── Featured Offers
│
├── Offer Highlights
│
└── Call-to-Action
```

---

# Content Components

## Section Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the value of available offers
- Match the brand tone
- Encourage guests to explore opportunities

---

## Featured Offers

Featured offers may include:

- Seasonal packages
- Accommodation packages
- Dining packages
- Experience packages
- Special events
- Limited-time promotions

---

## Offer Highlights

Each offer may include:

- Offer name
- Main image
- Description
- Included benefits
- Valid period
- Terms and conditions
- Booking requirements
- Link to booking page

All offer information must be verified before publication.

---

## Call-to-Action

The section may provide actions such as:

- View Offers
- Book Now
- Learn More
- Check Availability

---

# Content Ownership

| Content | Owner |

---

# User Experience Rules

The Special Offers section should:

- Clearly communicate value
- Avoid misleading promotions
- Display important conditions
- Maintain visual consistency
- Provide simple booking paths

---

# SEO Considerations

The section should support:

- Offer-related keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
- Search-friendly descriptions

---

# Maintenance

Review this section when:

- New offers are created
- Promotions expire
- Campaign strategies change
- Booking conditions change

Expired offers should be archived according to website maintenance rules.
