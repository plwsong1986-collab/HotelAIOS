# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Dining section on the homepage.

The section introduces the hotel's dining experience and guides guests toward detailed dining information.

---

# Objectives

The Dining section should:

- Showcase the hotel's dining experience
- Present key dining options
- Communicate food and beverage value
- Encourage guests to explore dining services

---

# Structure

```text
Dining Section

├── Section Introduction
│
├── Featured Dining Options
│
├── Dining Highlights
│
└── Call-to-Action
```

---

# Content Components

## Section Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the overall dining concept
- Reflect the brand atmosphere
- Communicate the guest experience

---

## Featured Dining Options

Featured dining content may include:

- Restaurants
- Breakfast
- Coffee
- Tea
- Bars
- Special dining experiences

---

## Dining Highlights

Each dining highlight may include:

- Dining name
- Main image
- Short description
- Signature features
- Opening information
- Reservation options
- Link to detailed page

All factual dining information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Call-to-Action

The section may provide actions such as:

- Explore Dining
- View Menu
- Make Reservation
- Discover More

---

# Content Ownership

| Content | Owner |
|---------|-------|
| Dining facts | Knowledge |
| Brand presentation | Brand |
| Page layout | Website |
| Food imagery | Media |

---

# User Experience Rules

The Dining section should:

- Present dining options clearly
- Use attractive visual presentation
- Highlight guest benefits
- Avoid unnecessary information overload
- Provide clear next steps

---

# SEO Considerations

The section should support:

- Descriptive headings
- Internal links
- Relevant dining keywords
- Accessible images

---

# Maintenance

Review this section when:

- New dining options are introduced
- Menus change
- Dining services change
- Visual assets are updated
