# Purpose

This document defines the overall structure and purpose of the HotelAIOS homepage.

The homepage acts as the primary digital entrance for guests and provides a clear overview of the hotel's identity, offerings, and guest experience.

---

# Page Objectives

The homepage should:

- Introduce the hotel brand
- Communicate the primary guest value
- Guide visitors to important information
- Build trust
- Encourage exploration
- Support direct booking

---

# Page Structure

The homepage follows this content hierarchy:

```text
Homepage

├── Hero Section
│
├── Hotel Introduction
│
├── Featured Rooms
│
├── Facilities Overview
│
├── Dining Overview
│
├── Experiences Overview
│
├── Special Offers
│
├── Guest Reviews
│
└── Booking Call-to-Action
```

---

# User Intent

Homepage visitors typically seek:

- Understanding what the hotel offers
- Evaluating whether the hotel matches their needs
- Exploring rooms and services
- Finding booking options
- Confirming trust and credibility

---

# Content Relationship

Homepage content should reference:

| Content | Source |
|---------|--------|
| Brand story | Brand |
| Hotel information | Knowledge |
| Room information | Knowledge |
| Services | Knowledge |
| Experiences | Knowledge |
| Reviews | Website / Review sources |

---

# Content Rules

The homepage should:

- Present accurate information
- Avoid excessive text
- Prioritize important guest decisions
- Maintain visual balance
- Use consistent terminology
- Link to detailed pages when necessary

---

# SEO Considerations

The homepage should provide:

- Clear title
- Accurate meta description
- Primary keywords
- Structured content hierarchy
- Internal links to important pages

---

# Maintenance

Review the homepage when:

- Major brand changes occur
- New services are introduced
- Important website sections change
- User behavior indicates improvement opportunities
