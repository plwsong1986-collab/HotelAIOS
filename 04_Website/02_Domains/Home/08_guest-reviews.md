# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Guest Reviews section on the homepage.

The section presents guest feedback and trust signals to help visitors understand the guest experience.

---

# Objectives

The Guest Reviews section should:

- Build guest trust
- Present authentic experiences
- Support booking decisions
- Highlight service quality
- Strengthen brand credibility

---

# Structure

```text
Guest Reviews Section

├── Section Introduction
│
├── Review Highlights
│
├── Guest Feedback Display
│
└── Trust Indicators
```

---

# Content Components

## Section Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of guest feedback
- Maintain brand tone
- Encourage visitors to learn from guest experiences

---

## Review Highlights

Review highlights may include:

- Guest comments
- Ratings
- Experience summaries
- Common positive themes
- Service feedback

---

## Guest Feedback Display

Each review item may include:

- Guest feedback content
- Source platform
- Date (when applicable)
- Rating information
- Related experience category

All review information should be accurate and traceable to the original source.

---

## Trust Indicators

Trust indicators may include:

- Review ratings
- Guest satisfaction indicators
- Recognition or awards
- Verified feedback sources

# User Experience Rules

The Guest Reviews section should:

- Display authentic feedback
- Avoid misleading presentation
- Maintain readability
- Provide appropriate context
- Support user confidence

---

# SEO Considerations

The section should support:

- Structured review data where applicable
- Relevant page content
- Internal links
- Trust signals

---

# Maintenance

Review this section when:

- New reviews are collected
- Review platforms change
- Guest feedback trends change
- Display strategy changes

Review content should be updated while maintaining authenticity.
