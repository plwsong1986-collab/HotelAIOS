# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Experiences section on the homepage.

The section introduces memorable guest experiences and helps visitors understand the unique value of staying at the hotel.

---

# Objectives

The Experiences section should:

- Showcase meaningful guest experiences
- Communicate the hotel's unique character
- Encourage deeper exploration
- Support guest decision-making
- Connect visitors with local and hotel experiences

---

# Structure

```text
Experiences Section

├── Section Introduction
│
├── Featured Experiences
│
├── Experience Highlights
│
└── Call-to-Action
```

---

# Content Components

## Section Introduction

The introduction should:

- Communicate the overall experience concept
- Reflect the brand personality
- Create emotional connection
- Encourage exploration

---

## Featured Experiences

Featured experiences may include:

- Cultural experiences
- Local attractions
- Wellness activities
- Seasonal activities
- Signature hotel experiences

---

## Experience Highlights

Each experience highlight may include:

- Experience name
- Main image
- Short description
- Key features
- Availability information
- Participation requirements
- Link to detailed page

All factual experience information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Call-to-Action

The section may provide actions such as:

- Discover Experiences
- Explore Local Area
- Learn More
- Plan Your Stay

---

# Content Ownership

| Content | Owner |
|---------|-------|
| Experience facts | Knowledge |
| Brand storytelling | Brand |
| Page layout | Website |
| Visual assets | Media |

---

# User Experience Rules

The Experiences section should:

- Create emotional engagement
- Present experiences clearly
- Balance inspiration and information
- Support different guest interests
- Provide clear exploration paths

---

# SEO Considerations

The section should support:

- Experience-related keywords
- Internal links
- Structured headings
- Accessible images

---

# Maintenance

Review this section when:

- New experiences are introduced
- Seasonal activities change
- Local information changes
- Visual assets are updated
