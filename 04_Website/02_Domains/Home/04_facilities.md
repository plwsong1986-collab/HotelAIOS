# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Facilities section on the homepage.

The section introduces key hotel facilities and helps guests understand the environment and available resources.

---

# Objectives

The Facilities section should:

- Showcase important hotel facilities
- Communicate guest benefits
- Build confidence before booking
- Guide users toward detailed facility information

---

# Structure

```text
Facilities Section

├── Section Introduction
│
├── Featured Facilities
│
├── Facility Highlights
│
└── Call-to-Action
```

---

# Content Components

## Section Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the overall facility experience
- Reflect the hotel brand tone
- Explain the value of available facilities

---

## Featured Facilities

Featured facilities may include:

- Accommodation facilities
- Dining facilities
- Leisure facilities
- Guest service facilities
- Special property features

---

## Facility Highlights

Each facility highlight may include:

- Facility name
- Main image
- Short description
- Guest benefits
- Availability information
- Link to detailed page

All factual facility information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Call-to-Action

The section may provide actions such as:

- Explore Facilities
- View Details
- Discover More

---

| Content | Owner |

# User Experience Rules

The Facilities section should:

- Highlight the most valuable facilities first
- Maintain consistent visual presentation
- Avoid overwhelming users with excessive details
- Provide clear paths to deeper information

---

# SEO Considerations

The section should support:

- Descriptive headings
- Internal links
- Accessible images
- Relevant facility terminology

---

# Maintenance

Review this section when:

- New facilities are added
- Existing facilities change
- Images are updated
- Guest needs change
