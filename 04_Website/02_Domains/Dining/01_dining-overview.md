# Purpose

This document defines the overall presentation strategy for the Dining overview page.

The page introduces the hotel's dining experience and helps guests discover restaurants, beverages, and specialty dining services.

---

# Objectives

The Dining Overview page should:

- Introduce the hotel's dining experience
- Present available dining options
- Highlight signature food and beverage offerings
- Encourage reservations where applicable
- Guide guests to detailed dining pages

---

# Page Structure

```text
Dining Overview

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Featured Restaurants
│
├── Breakfast
│
├── Beverages
│
├── Special Dining
│
└── Reservation Call-to-Action
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the overall dining philosophy
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce the dining experience

---

## Featured Restaurants

Present each restaurant with:

- Restaurant name
- Short description
- Cuisine
- Main image
- Link to detail page

Restaurant information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Breakfast

Provide an overview of:

- Breakfast service
- Dining style
- Service period
- Key highlights

Detailed information should link to the Breakfast page.

---

## Beverages

Introduce beverage services such as:

- Coffee
- Tea
- Juice
- Cocktails
- Bar services

Detailed information should link to the Beverage page.

---

## Special Dining

Highlight experiences such as:

- Seasonal menus
- Chef's specials
- Private dining
- Event dining

---

## Reservation Call-to-Action

Provide clear actions such as:

- View Restaurants
- Reserve a Table
- Explore Dining
- Contact the Restaurant
