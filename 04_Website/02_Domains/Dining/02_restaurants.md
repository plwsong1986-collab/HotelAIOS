# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for restaurant pages on the website.

The Restaurants page introduces the hotel's dining venues and helps guests explore available dining experiences before making a reservation.

---

# Objectives

The Restaurants page should:

- Present each restaurant clearly
- Highlight each restaurant's unique experience
- Help guests compare dining options
- Support reservation decisions
- Guide users to detailed dining information

---

# Page Structure

```text
Restaurants

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Restaurant List
│
├── Restaurant Detail Cards
│
├── Dining Highlights
│
└── Reservation Call-to-Action
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's dining collection
- Reflect the brand tone
- Help guests understand available dining options

---

## Restaurant List

Each restaurant entry may include:

- Restaurant name
- Cuisine
- Short description
- Main image
- Link to detail page

All restaurant information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Restaurant Detail Cards

Each card may include:

- Signature dishes
- Dining atmosphere
- Opening hours
- Reservation availability
- Guest suitability
- Location within the property

---

## Dining Highlights

Highlights may include:

- Signature cuisine
- Seasonal menus
- Chef recommendations
- Local specialties
- Private dining availability

---

## Reservation Call-to-Action

Provide actions such as:

- View Restaurant
- Reserve a Table
- View Menu
- Contact Restaurant

# User Experience Rules

The Restaurants page should:

- Present restaurants consistently
- Support quick comparison
- Use high-quality photography
- Maintain clear navigation
- Provide direct reservation access where available

---

# SEO Considerations

The page should support:

- Restaurant-related keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
- Search-friendly descriptions

# Maintenance

Review this page when:

- Restaurants change
- Menus change
- Operating hours change
- Reservation policies change
- Images are updated
