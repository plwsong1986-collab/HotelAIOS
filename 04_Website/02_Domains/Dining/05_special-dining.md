# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Special Dining page on the website.

The page introduces unique dining experiences that extend beyond regular restaurant services and helps guests discover memorable culinary opportunities.

---

# Objectives

The Special Dining page should:

- Showcase unique dining experiences
- Highlight exclusive culinary offerings
- Support guest engagement
- Encourage reservations
- Differentiate the hotel's dining experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Special Dining

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Featured Experiences
│
├── Seasonal Events
│
├── Private Dining
│
├── Chef Specials
│
└── Reservation Information
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the concept of special dining
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce available dining experiences

---

## Featured Experiences

Featured experiences may include:

- Signature dining events
- Tasting menus
- Outdoor dining
- Destination dining
- Cultural dining experiences

All factual information must reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Seasonal Events

Where applicable, present:

- Holiday dining
- Seasonal menus
- Festival events
- Limited-time experiences

Seasonal information should remain current.

---

## Private Dining

Private dining information may include:

- Private rooms
- Group dining
- Celebration packages
- Business dining
- Reservation requirements

---

## Chef Specials

Chef specials may include:

- Signature dishes
- Chef's recommendations
- Limited-time creations
- Local specialties

Descriptions should focus on the guest experience rather than operational details.

---

## Reservation Information

Provide information such as:

- Reservation process
- Availability
- Advance booking requirements
- Contact information
