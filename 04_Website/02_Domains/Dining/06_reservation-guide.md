# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for dining reservations on the website.

The Reservation Guide helps guests understand how to reserve dining experiences and what to expect before arriving.

---

# Objectives

The Reservation Guide should:

- Explain the reservation process
- Reduce guest uncertainty
- Improve reservation completion
- Present reservation policies clearly
- Support a consistent booking experience

# Content Components

## Reservation Overview

Provide a brief introduction explaining:

- Which dining venues accept reservations
- When reservations are recommended
- Benefits of reserving in advance

---

## Reservation Methods

Reservation methods may include:

- Website booking
- Telephone
- Email
- Front Desk
- Concierge

Only officially supported reservation channels should be listed.

---

## Reservation Process

The reservation process should explain:

```text
Choose Restaurant
        ↓
Select Date
        ↓
Select Time
        ↓
Enter Guest Information
        ↓
Confirm Reservation
        ↓
Receive Confirmation
```

The process should remain simple and easy to understand.

---

## Reservation Policies

Reservation policies may include:

- Advance booking requirements
- Cancellation policy
- Modification policy
- Late arrival policy
- No-show policy
- Group reservation policy

All policy information must reference the official Operations or Legal documentation.

---

## Arrival Information

Provide guidance such as:

- Arrival recommendations
- Check-in procedure
- Waiting policy
- Seating arrangements
- Special requests

---

## Contact Information

Provide official contact information for reservation assistance, including:

- Telephone
- Email
- Front Desk
- Operating hours

All contact information must reference the Knowledge module.
