# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Beverages page on the website.

The Beverages page introduces the hotel's beverage offerings and helps guests discover available drinks before and during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Beverages page should:

- Present beverage options clearly
- Highlight signature drinks
- Introduce beverage experiences
- Support dining decisions
- Guide guests to related dining services

---

# Page Structure

```text
Beverages

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Coffee
│
├── Tea
│
├── Signature Drinks
│
├── Seasonal Selections
│
└── Service Information
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the hotel's beverage experience
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Introduce available drink categories

---

## Coffee

Coffee information may include:

- Coffee selection
- Brewing styles
- Signature coffee
- Service availability

Detailed coffee information should reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Tea

Tea information may include:

- Tea varieties
- Local teas
- Herbal teas
- Premium selections

Only confirmed offerings should be presented.

---

## Signature Drinks

Highlight beverages such as:

- House specialties
- Seasonal creations
- Local specialties
- Non-alcoholic beverages
- Fresh juices

Descriptions should emphasize the guest experience rather than technical details.

---

## Seasonal Selections

Where applicable, present:

- Limited-time beverages
- Seasonal menus
- Holiday specialties
- Event-exclusive drinks

Seasonal content should be reviewed regularly.

---

## Service Information

Provide information such as:

- Service hours
- Dining location
- Availability
- Reservation requirements (if applicable)

The Beverages page should:

- Present drink categories clearly
- Use high-quality photography
- Support quick browsing
- Maintain consistent terminology
- Link to related dining pages

The page should support:

- Beverage-related keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
- Search-friendly descriptions

- Beverage offerings change
