# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Breakfast page on the website.

The Breakfast page introduces the hotel's breakfast experience and provides guests with the information needed before or during their stay.

---

# Objectives

The Breakfast page should:

- Present the breakfast experience clearly
- Highlight signature breakfast offerings
- Explain service information
- Support guest planning
- Encourage dining participation

---

# Page Structure

```text
Breakfast

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Breakfast Experience
│
├── Menu Highlights
│
├── Service Information
│
├── Dietary Options
│
└── Reservation Information
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Present the breakfast concept
- Reflect the hotel brand
- Set guest expectations

---

## Breakfast Experience

Describe the overall experience, including:

- Dining atmosphere
- Service style
- Signature features
- Guest experience

---

## Menu Highlights

Menu highlights may include:

- Local specialties
- International selections
- Bakery items
- Fresh fruit
- Hot dishes
- Beverages

Detailed menu information should reference the Knowledge module.

---

## Service Information

Service information may include:

- Serving hours
- Dining location
- Buffet or à la carte service
- Guest eligibility

---

## Dietary Options

Where applicable, describe available options such as:

- Vegetarian
- Vegan
- Gluten-free
- Children's selections
- Allergy considerations

Only confirmed offerings should be presented.

---

## Reservation Information

Where applicable, provide:

- Reservation requirements
- Walk-in availability
- Included breakfast policies
- Contact information

The Breakfast page should:

- Present information clearly

- Support mobile browsing
- Link to related dining pages

- Breakfast-related keywords
- Structured headings
- Internal links
- Search-friendly descriptions
