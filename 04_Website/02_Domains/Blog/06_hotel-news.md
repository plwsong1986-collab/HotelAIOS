# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Hotel News page on the website.

The Hotel News page publishes official hotel announcements, operational updates, promotions, events, partnerships, and other organizational news, ensuring guests receive timely and reliable information directly from the hotel.

---

# Objectives

The Hotel News page should:

- Publish official hotel news
- Communicate organizational updates
- Improve guest awareness
- Strengthen brand credibility
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Hotel News

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Featured News
│
├── Latest News
│
├── News Categories
│
├── Related Articles
│
└── Related Blog Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of Hotel News
- Introduce official hotel announcements
- Reflect the hotel's editorial standards

---

## Featured News

Present selected announcements, including:

- Major hotel updates
- Property improvements
- Special events
- Partnerships
- Important guest notices

Featured news should follow editorial priorities.

---

## Latest News

Display recent announcements, including:

- Publication date
- News title
- Summary
- Featured image
- News category

News should be presented in reverse chronological order.

---

## News Categories

Organize news into logical groups, including:

- Hotel Updates
- Events
- Promotions
- Partnerships
- Community Activities
- Service Announcements

Categories should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.

---

## Related Articles

Recommend content based on:

- Similar news topics
- Related hotel updates
- Destination content
- Editorial recommendations

Recommendations should encourage continued reading.

---

## Related Blog Pages

Provide links to:

- Blog Overview
- Categories
- Article Detail
- Destination Guides
- Travel Tips
- Search & Archive
- Blog FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.
