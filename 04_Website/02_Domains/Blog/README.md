# Blog

**Project:** HotelAIOS  
**Module:** Website  
**Layer:** Domains  
**Domain:** Blog

---

# Purpose

The Blog domain defines all website pages related to articles, news, travel inspiration, destination guides, hotel updates, and editorial content.

These pages support content marketing, improve SEO, increase guest engagement, and provide valuable information before, during, and after a guest's stay.

---

# Scope

This domain includes:

1. Blog Overview
2. Categories
3. Article Detail
4. Destination Guides
5. Travel Tips
6. Hotel News
7. Search & Archive
8. Blog FAQ

# Objectives

The Blog domain should:

- Publish high-quality editorial content
- Support SEO growth
- Increase guest engagement
- Promote destinations and experiences
- Strengthen brand authority
- Drive conversions through content

---

# Audience

Primary audiences include:

- Prospective guests
- Returning guests
- Travelers
- Local visitors
- Business travelers
- Search engine users

---

# Content Principles

All pages should:

- Publish accurate and valuable content
- Maintain consistent editorial quality
- Avoid duplicated information
- Link to supporting knowledge documents
- Follow Website documentation standards
