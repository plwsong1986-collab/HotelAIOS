# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Blog FAQ page on the website.

The Blog FAQ page provides answers to common questions about blog content, editorial policies, article discovery, subscriptions, and content usage, helping visitors quickly understand how to use the blog effectively.

---

# Objectives

The Blog FAQ page should:

- Answer common blog-related questions
- Improve visitor self-service
- Increase content discoverability
- Reduce repetitive inquiries
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Blog FAQ

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Content Questions
│
├── Search Questions
│
├── Subscription Questions
│
├── Usage Questions
│
├── Additional Support
│
└── Related Blog Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the FAQ
- Help visitors locate answers quickly
- Reflect the hotel's editorial standards

---

## Content Questions

Provide answers covering topics such as:

- Types of published articles
- Content update frequency
- Editorial standards
- Featured articles
- Destination information

Answers should remain consistent with official editorial policies.

---

## Search Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Searching for articles
- Browsing categories
- Archive navigation
- Filtering content
- Finding related articles

Search guidance should reflect supported website functionality.

---

## Subscription Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Newsletter availability (if supported)
- Content notifications
- Subscription management
- Email preferences
- Unsubscribing

Only officially supported subscription features should be described.

---

## Usage Questions

Provide answers covering:

- Sharing articles
- Referencing content
- Copyright
- Image usage
- Content permissions

Usage policies should remain consistent with official website policies.

---

## Additional Support

Provide support resources including:

- Contact page
- Guest Services
- Media Contact
- General Enquiries

Support options should direct visitors to official communication channels.

---

## Related Blog Pages

Provide links to:

- Blog Overview
- Categories
- Article Detail
- Destination Guides
- Travel Tips
- Hotel News
- Search & Archive

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.
