# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Destination Guides page on the website.

The Destination Guides page provides curated information about nearby destinations, attractions, neighborhoods, and local experiences, helping guests plan their trips while positioning the hotel as a trusted local travel resource.

---

# Objectives

The Destination Guides page should:

- Introduce local destinations
- Inspire trip planning
- Promote nearby attractions
- Improve destination content discovery
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Destination Guides

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Featured Destinations
│
├── Destination Categories
│
├── Travel Information
│
├── Related Articles
│
└── Related Blog Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of destination guides
- Introduce local travel inspiration
- Reflect the hotel's editorial style

---

## Featured Destinations

Present recommended destinations, including:

- Major attractions
- Historic sites
- Nature destinations
- Entertainment areas
- Shopping districts

Featured destinations should follow editorial priorities.

---

## Destination Categories

Organize destinations into logical groups, such as:

- Culture
- Nature
- Family Activities
- Food & Dining
- Shopping
- Nightlife

Categories should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.

---

## Travel Information

Provide practical travel information, including:

- Best time to visit
- Transportation options
- Suggested visit duration
- General travel tips
- Nearby points of interest

Information should remain accurate and regularly reviewed.

---

## Related Articles

Recommend articles based on:

- Similar destinations
- Travel themes
- Seasonal recommendations
- Editorial selections

Recommendations should encourage additional reading.

---

## Related Blog Pages

Provide links to:

- Blog Overview
- Categories
- Article Detail
- Travel Tips
- Hotel News
- Search & Archive
- Blog FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.
