# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Search & Archive page on the website.

The Search & Archive page helps visitors locate blog content efficiently through search, filtering, chronological archives, and category-based navigation while improving long-term content discoverability.

---

# Objectives

The Search & Archive page should:

- Help visitors find published content
- Support efficient content filtering
- Improve long-term content discovery
- Increase engagement with archived articles
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Search & Archive

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Search
│
├── Filter Options
│
├── Archive Navigation
│
├── Search Results
│
└── Related Blog Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of search and archive
- Help visitors discover published content
- Reflect the hotel's editorial style

---

## Search

Provide search capabilities supporting:

- Article titles
- Keywords
- Categories
- Tags (if supported)
- Article summaries

Search behavior should remain consistent across the website.

---

## Filter Options

Support filtering by:

- Category
- Publication date
- Year
- Month
- Topic
- Author (if supported)

Only supported filters should be displayed.

---

## Archive Navigation

Provide archive browsing using:

- Year
- Month
- Category
- Topic collections

Archive organization should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.

---

## Search Results

Display matching articles including:

- Article title
- Publication date
- Summary
- Category
- Featured image (if available)

Results should prioritize relevance and support pagination where applicable.

---

## Related Blog Pages

Provide links to:

- Blog Overview
- Categories
- Article Detail
- Destination Guides
- Travel Tips
- Hotel News
- Blog FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.
