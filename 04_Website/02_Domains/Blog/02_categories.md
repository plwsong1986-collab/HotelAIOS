# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Categories page on the website.

The Categories page organizes blog content into logical topics, helping visitors quickly discover articles relevant to their interests and improving overall content navigation.

---

# Objectives

The Categories page should:

- Organize editorial content
- Improve content discovery
- Support intuitive navigation
- Strengthen SEO through topic grouping
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Categories

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Category Directory
│
├── Featured Categories
│
├── Category Navigation
│
└── Related Blog Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of blog categories
- Help visitors browse content by topic
- Reflect the hotel's editorial style

---

## Category Directory

Present all available blog categories, including:

- Destination Guides
- Travel Tips
- Hotel News
- Experiences
- Dining
- Local Culture
- Seasonal Content
- Promotions (if applicable)

Only officially supported categories should be displayed.

---

## Featured Categories

Highlight categories that are:

- Most popular
- Frequently updated
- Editorially promoted
- Seasonally relevant

Featured categories should follow editorial priorities.

---

## Category Navigation

Each category should provide:

- Category name
- Short description
- Article count
- Link to category listing

Navigation should remain consistent across the Blog domain.

---

## Related Blog Pages

Provide links to:

- Blog Overview
- Article Detail
- Destination Guides
- Travel Tips
- Hotel News
- Search & Archive
- Blog FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.
