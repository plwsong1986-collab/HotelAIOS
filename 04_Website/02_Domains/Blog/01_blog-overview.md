# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Blog Overview page on the website.

The Blog Overview page serves as the entry point for all editorial content, helping visitors discover articles, destination inspiration, travel advice, hotel news, and other published content.

---

# Objectives

The Blog Overview page should:

- Introduce the blog
- Highlight featured content
- Improve content discovery
- Increase visitor engagement
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Blog

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Featured Articles
│
├── Latest Articles
│
├── Categories
│
├── Popular Topics
│
└── Related Blog Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of the blog
- Introduce available content
- Reflect the hotel's editorial style

---

## Featured Articles

Present selected editorial content, including:

- Featured travel stories
- Destination highlights
- Seasonal content
- Hotel recommendations
- Promotional editorial content

Featured articles should be maintained by the editorial team.

---

## Latest Articles

Display recently published articles, including:

- Publication date
- Article title
- Summary
- Featured image
- Category

Articles should be presented in reverse chronological order.

---

## Categories

Provide navigation to major content categories, including:

- Destination Guides
- Travel Tips
- Hotel News
- Experiences
- Dining
- Local Culture

Categories should remain consistent across the Blog domain.

---

## Popular Topics

Highlight frequently viewed or promoted topics based on editorial priorities.

Popularity indicators should follow the website's content strategy.

---

## Related Blog Pages

Provide links to:

- Categories
- Article Detail
- Destination Guides
- Travel Tips
- Hotel News
- Search & Archive
- Blog FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.
