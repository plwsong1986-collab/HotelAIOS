# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Travel Tips page on the website.

The Travel Tips page provides practical travel advice, planning recommendations, and useful guidance that helps guests prepare for their trips while positioning the hotel as a trusted travel resource.

---

# Objectives

The Travel Tips page should:

- Provide practical travel advice
- Help guests prepare for their trips
- Improve travel confidence
- Increase engagement with editorial content
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Travel Tips

├── Page Introduction
│
├── Featured Tips
│
├── Travel Topics
│
├── Seasonal Advice
│
├── Related Articles
│
└── Related Blog Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Page Introduction

The introduction should:

- Explain the purpose of travel tips
- Introduce practical travel guidance
- Reflect the hotel's editorial style

---

## Featured Tips

Present selected travel advice, including:

- Trip planning
- Packing recommendations
- Transportation advice
- Safety guidance
- Local travel suggestions

Featured tips should follow editorial priorities.

---

## Travel Topics

Organize travel advice into logical categories, such as:

- Planning
- Packing
- Transportation
- Budget Travel
- Family Travel
- Business Travel

Categories should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.

---

## Seasonal Advice

Provide recommendations based on seasonal conditions, including:

- Weather preparation
- Seasonal activities
- Holiday travel
- Local events
- Travel planning suggestions

Seasonal information should be reviewed regularly.

---

## Related Articles

Recommend articles based on:

- Similar travel topics
- Destination guides
- Seasonal recommendations
- Editorial selections

Recommendations should encourage additional reading.

---

## Related Blog Pages

Provide links to:

- Blog Overview
- Categories
- Article Detail
- Destination Guides
- Hotel News
- Search & Archive
- Blog FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.
