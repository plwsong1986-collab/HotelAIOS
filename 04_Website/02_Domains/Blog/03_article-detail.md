# Purpose

This document defines the structure and presentation requirements for the Article Detail page on the website.

The Article Detail page presents individual blog articles, delivering editorial content in a readable, engaging, and search-friendly format while encouraging visitors to continue exploring related content.

---

# Objectives

The Article Detail page should:

- Present editorial content clearly
- Maximize article readability
- Encourage further content discovery
- Support SEO best practices
- Maintain a consistent website experience

---

# Page Structure

```text
Article Detail

├── Article Header
│
├── Article Metadata
│
├── Featured Image
│
├── Article Content
│
├── Supporting Media
│
├── Related Articles
│
└── Related Blog Pages
```

---

# Content Components

## Article Header

The header should include:

- Article title
- Short introduction
- Featured category

Titles should accurately describe the article content.

---

## Article Metadata

Display article information including:

- Publication date
- Last updated date (if applicable)
- Author
- Reading time
- Category

Metadata should follow the website's editorial standards.

---

## Featured Image

Present a primary image that:

- Represents the article
- Supports the content
- Meets brand guidelines
- Includes descriptive alternative text

Images should comply with website media standards.

---

## Article Content

Present the main editorial content using:

- Clear section headings
- Short paragraphs
- Lists where appropriate
- Images and media when relevant
- Internal links

Content should remain accurate, well-structured, and easy to read.

---

## Supporting Media

Additional content may include:

- Image galleries
- Videos
- Maps
- Infographics
- Downloadable resources (if applicable)

Media should enhance the article without reducing readability.

---

## Related Articles

Recommend articles based on:

- Same category
- Similar topics
- Editorial recommendations
- Recent publications

Recommendations should encourage continued browsing.

---

## Related Blog Pages

Provide links to:

- Blog Overview
- Categories
- Destination Guides
- Travel Tips
- Hotel News
- Search & Archive
- Blog FAQ

Navigation should remain consistent throughout the Blog domain.

The Article Detail page should:

- Prioritize readability
- Load media efficiently

- Article-specific keywords

- Semantic headings
- Internal links
- Rich media optimization
