# Purpose

This document defines the AI safety principles, controls, and governance requirements for HotelAIOS.

AI Safety ensures that AI systems operate securely, responsibly, reliably, and consistently while protecting guests, staff, business operations, and organizational information.

---

# Objectives

The AI Safety should:

- Protect users and business operations
- Reduce AI operational risks
- Define AI safety boundaries
- Prevent unauthorized AI behavior
- Support secure AI deployment
- Enable continuous safety improvement

# Safety Principles

AI systems should:

- Operate within approved business boundaries
- Follow documented business policies
- Respect user privacy
- Produce reliable responses
- Minimize operational risks

Safety requirements should apply to every AI service.

---

# Access Control

AI services should:

- Authenticate authorized users
- Enforce role-based permissions
- Restrict administrative functions
- Protect internal operations
- Record access events

Access policies should be centrally managed.

---

# Data Protection

AI should:

- Protect confidential information
- Minimize exposure of sensitive data
- Respect data classification policies
- Apply secure data handling practices
- Support regulatory compliance where applicable

Sensitive information should only be processed when authorized.

---

# Prompt Security

Prompt management should:

- Prevent unauthorized prompt modification
- Separate prompts from business knowledge
- Validate prompt updates
- Maintain version history
- Restrict production prompt access

Prompt security should be included in the deployment process.

---

# Output Validation

AI responses should:

- Follow approved business rules
- Reference authoritative knowledge
- Avoid unsupported statements
- Maintain professional communication
- Support consistent response quality

Critical business decisions should require appropriate verification.

---

# Risk Management

Risk management should:

- Identify operational risks
- Assess potential impact
- Define mitigation strategies
- Monitor known risks
- Review risk controls regularly

Risk management should support continuous improvement.

---

# Incident Response

AI incident management should define:

- Incident identification
- Severity classification
- Response procedures
- Escalation process
- Recovery activities
- Post-incident review

All significant incidents should be documented.

---

# Governance

AI safety governance should define:

- Safety ownership
- Review responsibilities
- Approval procedures
- Compliance requirements
- Audit processes
- Continuous improvement practices

Governance should apply across the entire AI platform.

---

# Safety Principles

AI Safety should:

- Be proactive
- Be measurable
- Be auditable
- Protect business operations
- Protect user privacy
- Support scalable AI deployment
- Follow Documentation First principles
