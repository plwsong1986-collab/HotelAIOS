# Purpose

This document defines the evaluation framework for the HotelAIOS AI Layer.

It establishes standardized methodologies, quality metrics, benchmarking procedures, governance principles, and continuous improvement practices for evaluating AI models, prompts, retrieval systems, workflows, and overall AI performance.

This document is the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for AI Evaluation.

---

# Overview

Evaluation ensures that AI systems consistently deliver accurate, reliable, safe, and high-quality results.

The HotelAIOS AI Layer should continuously evaluate every major AI capability throughout its lifecycle to maintain user trust, operational stability, and enterprise governance.

Evaluation supports ongoing optimization rather than one-time validation.

- Repeatable
- Quantifiable
