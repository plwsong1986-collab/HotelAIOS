# Purpose

This document defines the UX research framework for HotelAIOS.

UX Research establishes the principles, research methodologies, participant management, usability evaluation, data analysis, governance, and continuous improvement processes required to understand user needs, validate design decisions, and improve the overall user experience across the HotelAIOS platform.

---

# Objectives

The UX Research should:

- Standardize UX research practices
- Improve user-centered decision making
- Validate design assumptions
- Identify usability issues
- Support evidence-based product development
- Enable continuous UX improvement

# Research Principles

UX research should follow these principles:

- User Centered Design
- Evidence Based Decisions
- Ethical Research
- Inclusivity
- Transparency
- Repeatability
- Documentation First
- Continuous Improvement

Research activities should generate actionable insights that improve user experience.

---

# Research Planning

Research planning should define:

- Research objectives
- Business goals
- Research questions
- Success criteria
- Scope
- Timeline
- Resource allocation

Planning should ensure that research activities align with product objectives.

---

# Research Methods

Supported research methods should include:

- User interviews
- Usability testing
- Surveys
- Contextual inquiry
- Field observation
- Card sorting
- A/B testing
- Analytics review

Research methods should be selected according to the objectives of each study.

---

# Participant Management

Participant management should include:

- Recruitment criteria
- User segmentation
- Consent management
- Scheduling
- Incentive management
- Privacy protection

Participants should represent the intended user population whenever practical.

---

# Data Collection

Research data collection should include:

- Observation notes
- Session recordings
- Survey responses
- Task completion data
- User feedback
- Behavioral metrics

Collected data should be securely stored and handled according to privacy requirements.

---

# Analysis and Insights

Research analysis should include:

- Pattern identification
- Pain point analysis
- Opportunity identification
- Usability findings
- Prioritized recommendations
- Executive summaries

Insights should be evidence based and linked to business and user goals.

---

# Research Repository

The research repository should maintain:

- Research plans
- Study results
- Interview summaries
- Usability reports
- Design recommendations
- Historical research records

The repository should support knowledge sharing across teams.

---

# Governance

UX research governance should define:

- Research ownership
- Ethical standards
- Documentation requirements
- Review procedures
- Privacy compliance
- Continuous improvement

Governance should ensure research quality and consistency across the platform.

---

# Continuous Improvement

UX research should continuously improve through:

- Research retrospectives
- Method evaluation
- Participant feedback
- Process optimization
- Tool evaluation
- Knowledge sharing

Continuous improvement should strengthen research effectiveness over time.

---

# UX Research Principles

UX Research should:

- Be user centered
- Be evidence based
- Be ethical
- Be repeatable
- Be maintainable
- Follow Documentation First principles
