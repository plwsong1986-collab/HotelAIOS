# Vision

HotelAIOS aims to become a modular, AI-native operating system for the hospitality industry.

The project combines documentation, knowledge management, website content, AI capabilities, OTA connectivity, operational workflows, analytics, integrations, deployment automation, and user experience into a unified engineering ecosystem.

---

# Strategic Principles

HotelAIOS follows these long-term principles:

- Documentation First
- Single Source of Truth (SSOT)
- One File, One Purpose
- Modular Architecture
- Progressive Expansion
- AI-Native Knowledge
- Long-Term Maintainability
- Open Collaboration

Every future initiative should align with these principles.

# Development Phases

HotelAIOS evolves through progressive phases.

```text
Documentation Foundation
        ↓
Engineering Governance
        ↓
Knowledge Platform
        ↓
AI Capabilities
        ↓
Operations & OTA
        ↓
Integration Platform
        ↓
Analytics & Intelligence
        ↓
Community Ecosystem
```

Each phase builds upon the stability of the previous phase.
